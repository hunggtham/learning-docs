# Crises, Regime Thay đổi (change / 변경) & Đường dẫn (path / 경로) Dependence — Stress tests của institutions và economic các hệ thống (systems / 시스템들)

Economic crises reveal hidden leverage, weak institutions and political các ràng buộc (constraints / 제약조건들) that are difficult to see in normal times. But crisis lịch sử (history / 이력) becomes misleading when every episode is forced into the same template. Banking crises, sovereign defaults, inflationary breakdowns, currency crises and depressions can interact yet begin through different mechanisms.

This chapter uses crises as comparative stress tests rather than a chronology of famous events.

## 1. Crisis as amplification, not only shock

A large negative shock is not enough to explain crisis severity. What matters is amplification through leverage, maturity mismatch, bank runs, chính sách (policy / 정책) các ràng buộc (constraints / 제약조건들), expectations and institutional responses.

```text
Initial shock
→ balance-sheet losses
→ funding/credit contraction
→ forced sales / unemployment
→ weaker tax revenue / confidence
→ further losses
```

Phản hồi (feedback / 피드백) loops turn disturbance into systemic crisis.

## 2. Banking crisis

Banks are fragile when short-term runnable liabilities fund illiquid/risky assets.

Asset losses can become solvency bài toán (problem / 문제); fear can create liquidity run before final losses known.

Deposit insurance and central-bank liquidity reduce runs but require supervision to điều khiển (control / 제어) moral hazard.

## 3. Credit boom and bust

Boom periods can weaken underwriting because recent defaults are low and collateral prices rise.

Rising collateral supports more borrowing, reinforcing asset prices.

When prices reverse, leverage makes losses nonlinear.

## 4. Fire-sale externality

Forced sellers ignore how their sales lower thị trường (market / 시장) prices and damage balance sheets of others holding same assets.

This is a financial externality and a rationale for liquidity regulation or lender-of-last-resort hỗ trợ (support / 지원).

## 5. Debt deflation

Falling prices/incomes raise real burden of nominal debt, causing deleveraging and further spending contraction.

Deflation is especially damaging when leverage high.

## 6. Sovereign debt crisis

Debt sustainability depends primary balance, growth, interest rates, maturity, currency denomination and political sức chứa (capacity / 용량).

Default rủi ro (risk / 위험) can raise yields, worsening sustainability and creating self-reinforcing dynamics.

## 7. Domestic vs foreign-currency debt

Own-currency sovereign has monetary options unavailable to foreign-currency borrower, but inflation and credibility các ràng buộc (constraints / 제약조건들) remain.

Foreign-currency debt creates direct balance-sheet rủi ro (risk / 위험) under depreciation.

## 8. Currency crisis

A fixed/managed exchange tỷ lệ (rate / 비율) can become vulnerable when reserves fall, chính sách (policy / 정책) inconsistent or defense costs politically unacceptable.

Expectations can accelerate reserve mất mát (loss / 손실).

## 9. Twin crises

Bank and currency crises often interact through FX liabilities and trạng thái (state / 상태) guarantees.

Depreciation worsens bank/corporate balance sheets; bank rescue worsens fiscal credibility.

## 10. Inflationary fiscal crisis

Persistent fiscal financing needs can pressure monetary hệ thống (system / 시스템) if tax/debt sức chứa (capacity / 용량) inadequate.

High inflation episodes often reflect tương tác (interaction / 상호작용) of fiscal stress, monetary accommodation, supply disruption and credibility breakdown.

## 11. Hyperinflation

Hyperinflation is not simply “high money growth” as an isolated cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘). Fiscal collapse, war/reparations, tax thất bại (failure / 실패) and currency confidence often interact.

Stabilization generally requires fiscal as well as monetary regime thay đổi (change / 변경).

## 12. Depression vs ordinary recession

Deep depressions combine severe demand/đầu ra (output / 출력) decline with financial distress, debt problems, chính sách (policy / 정책) các ràng buộc (constraints / 제약조건들) or institutional breakdown.

Using “recession” as one homogeneous treatment hides nonlinear crisis effects.

## 13. Gold-standard ràng buộc (constraint / 제약조건)

Under gold convertibility, monetary expansion may be limited by reserve/credibility concerns.

Countries leaving restrictive peg earlier can sometimes recover faster, but selection and international conditions must be analyzed historically.

## 14. Bank holiday and guarantee

Emergency closure/guarantee can stop runs but redistributes rủi ro (risk / 위험) toward trạng thái (state / 상태) and can freeze payments.

Crisis chính sách (policy / 정책) trades immediate stabilization against moral hazard/fiscal exposure.

## 15. Capital controls

Controls can slow capital flight and preserve chính sách (policy / 정책) autonomy but distort financing and create evasion.

Effectiveness depends enforcement and whether underlying solvency bài toán (problem / 문제) is addressed.

## 16. Debt restructuring

Restructuring reduces debt burden but imposes losses on creditors and can affect future thị trường (market / 시장) truy cập (access / 접근).

Delay can preserve hope or worsen debt overhang depending fundamentals.

## 17. Debt overhang

When expected future returns accrue mainly to creditors/taxes, borrowers may underinvest.

Debt relief can improve incentives if debt truly overhangs productive investment, but creates redistribution and precedent effects.

## 18. Austerity and stimulus in crisis

Fiscal consolidation may restore confidence/debt sustainability but weaken demand when multiplier high.

Expansion can stabilize đầu ra (output / 출력) but be constrained by financing/inflation rủi ro (risk / 위험).

Historical tác động (effect / 효과) depends monetary regime, exchange tỷ lệ (rate / 비율), slack and bên ngoài (external / 외부) financing.

## 19. Lender of last resort vs bailout

Liquidity lending against collateral differs conceptually from recapitalizing insolvent institution.

The latter creates fiscal transfer/quyền sở hữu (ownership / 소유권) rủi ro (risk / 위험) and stronger moral-hazard questions.

## 20. Resolution institutions

Bank resolution aims keep trọng yếu (critical / 중요) functions while allocating losses to equity/creditors rather than uncontrolled collapse.

Credible resolution changes ex ante risk-taking incentives.

## 21. Crisis-induced reform

Crises can create political cửa sổ (window / 윈도우) for regulation, fiscal reform or institutional redesign.

But reform may reflect panic and overcorrect; stability later encourages deregulation.

Institutional cycles emerge from changing salience and coalitions.

## 22. Political phân phối (distribution / 분포)

Crisis losses are allocated among debtors, creditors, taxpayers, workers and asset owners.

Phân phối (distribution / 분포) shapes political hỗ trợ (support / 지원) for inflation, default, bailout or austerity.

Chính sách (policy / 정책) cannot be understood as technocratic tối ưu hóa (optimization / 최적화) alone.

## 23. Unemployment and political effects

Mass unemployment can alter trust, party các hệ thống (systems / 시스템들), di chuyển (migration / 마이그레이션) and hỗ trợ (support / 지원) for institutional thay đổi (change / 변경).

Nhân quả (causal / 인과적) claims from historical crises require careful cục bộ (local / 로컬) variation and pre-trend phân tích (analysis / 분석).

## 24. War and economic institutions

War destroys capital and trade but can accelerate taxation, mass môi trường vận hành (production / 운영 환경), technology and trạng thái (state / 상태) sức chứa (capacity / 용량).

Effects are heterogeneous and morally/socially catastrophic; economic institutional consequences should not be confused with welfare gains.

## 25. Reconstruction

Postwar/postcrisis rapid growth may reflect catch-up, unused sức chứa (capacity / 용량), institutional reset and capital rebuilding.

High growth during reconstruction is not permanent steady-state growth tỷ lệ (rate / 비율).

## 26. Regime thay đổi (change / 변경)

Monetary, fiscal, trade or political regime changes alter expectations and behavioral parameters.

Estimating one stable coefficient across regimes can thất bại (fail / 실패) Lucas-critique-style because chính sách (policy / 정책) rules thay đổi (change / 변경) hành vi (behavior / 동작).

## 27. Lucas critique intuition

Reduced-form relationships estimated under one chính sách (policy / 정책) regime may thay đổi (change / 변경) when chính sách (policy / 정책) quy tắc (rule / 규칙) changes because agents adapt expectations/actions.

Historical chính sách (policy / 정책) evaluation therefore needs structural/institutional ngữ cảnh (context / 맥락).

## 28. Đường dẫn (path / 경로) dependence

Đường dẫn (path / 경로) dependence means early events/institutions alter returns to later choices, making lịch sử (history / 이력) affect equilibrium selection.

Mechanisms include increasing returns, mạng (network / 네트워크) effects, sunk investments, organizations and norms.

Persistence alone is not sufficient proof.

## 29. Lock-in

A technology/institution can persist because switching chi phí (cost / 비용) and coordination make alternative individually unattractive even if collectively superior.

But apparent lock-in may actually reflect hidden benefits; counterfactual comparison is required.

## 30. Hysteresis

Temporary crisis can leave persistent economic effects via skill mất mát (loss / 손실), firm destruction, lower investment, di chuyển (migration / 마이그레이션) or institutional distrust.

This links macro hysteresis to historical persistence.

## 31. Multiple equilibria

Coordination/complementarity can allow several self-consistent outcomes. Crisis or reform can shift beliefs and move hệ thống (system / 시스템) between equilibria.

Multiple-equilibrium stories are difficult to kiểm thử (test / 테스트) and should specify cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘)/predictions.

## 32. Comparative crisis phân tích (analysis / 분석)

Compare episodes along dimensions:

```text
leverage
currency denomination
banking structure
exchange-rate regime
fiscal capacity
central-bank tools
external balance
political institutions
international support
```

This is more useful than superficial similarity by decade or asset bubble.

## 33. Historical dữ liệu (data / 데이터) limitations

Old bank balance sheets, prices and employment may have inconsistent definitions and survivor độ lệch (bias / 편향).

Đo lường (measurement / 측정) bất định (uncertainty / 불확실성) should be tường minh (explicit / 명시적).

## 34. Narrative identification

Archival records help classify chính sách (policy / 정책) intentions/shocks, but researcher judgment is part of thiết kế (design / 설계).

Transparent coding and alternative interpretations matter.

## 35. Difference-in-differences in lịch sử (history / 이력)

Regional exposure to bank failures, gold-standard exit, war destruction or chính sách (policy / 정책) rollout can provide DiD variation.

Parallel trends, di chuyển (migration / 마이그레이션) and spillovers are still required các giả định (assumptions / 가정들).

## 36. Instrumental variables in lịch sử (history / 이력)

Historical instruments often exploit geography, routes or inherited institutions.

Long nhân quả (causal / 인과적) chains make exclusion restrictions especially fragile; cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘) should be traced, not only first-stage strength.

## 37. Bên ngoài (external / 외부) validity

A credible estimate from 1930s banking or 19th-century railways may not vận chuyển (transport / 전송) directly to hiện tại (current / 현재) institutions.

Historical bằng chứng (evidence / 증거) is most valuable for mechanisms and ranh giới (boundary / 경계) conditions, not mechanical parameter reuse.

## 38. Thất bại (failure / 실패) modes

Sai lầm thứ nhất là explain every crisis with “irrational bubble”.

Sai lầm thứ hai là use same chính sách (policy / 정책) conclusion across different monetary/fiscal regimes.

Sai lầm thứ ba là lời gọi (call / 호출) persistence đường dẫn (path / 경로) dependence without increasing-return/lock-in cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘).

Sai lầm thứ tư là ignore phân phối (distribution / 분포)/politics of crisis phản hồi (response / 응답).

Sai lầm thứ năm là vận chuyển (transport / 전송) historical nhân quả (causal / 인과적) estimate directly to hiện đại (modern / 현대적) ngữ cảnh (context / 맥락) without institutional comparison.

## 39. Phân tích (analysis / 분석) template

Khi phân tích crisis/regime lịch sử (history / 이력), hãy hỏi:

1. initial shock là gì?
2. leverage/maturity/currency mismatch nào amplify?
3. banking-sovereign-currency phản hồi (feedback / 피드백) có tồn tại không?
4. chính sách (policy / 정책) tools bị regime nào constrain?
5. losses được phân phối cho ai?
6. expectations/credibility thay đổi thế nào?
7. reform thay quy tắc (rule / 규칙)/incentive nào sau crisis?
8. persistence cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘) là capital, skill, organization hay norm?
9. historical thiết kế (design / 설계) identify tác động (effect / 효과) nào, under các giả định (assumptions / 가정들) gì?
10. hiện đại (modern / 현대적) bên ngoài (external / 외부) validity cần institution nào tương đồng?
