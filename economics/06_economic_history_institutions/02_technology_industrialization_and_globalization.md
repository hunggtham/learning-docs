# Technology, Industrialization & Globalization — Năng lượng (energy / 에너지), organization, productivity và structural transformation

Industrialization không phải một invention đơn lẻ. Nó là quá trình trong đó năng lượng (energy / 에너지), machinery, vận chuyển (transport / 전송), human capital, finance, organization và institutions kết hợp để làm productivity tăng bền vững và kéo labor khỏi agriculture sang manufacturing/services.

Economic lịch sử (history / 이력) cần tách ba câu hỏi: technology nào khả thi; firms/states có incentive adopt không; và gains được diffusion/phân phối ra sao.

## 1. Pre-industrial growth ràng buộc (constraint / 제약조건)

Trong Malthusian-style economy, productivity gain có thể nâng population hơn là sustained income per capita vì fertility/mortality phản ứng living standards.

This is a stylized regime, not a claim every preindustrial society was at fixed subsistence.

## 2. Escape from Malthusian dynamics

Sustained per-capita growth requires productivity/human-capital thay đổi (change / 변경) outpace demographic dilution and alter fertility incentives.

Industrialization, urbanization and demographic chuyển tiếp (transition / 전이) interacted rather than occurring independently.

## 3. Năng lượng (energy / 에너지) ràng buộc (constraint / 제약조건)

Biomass/animal power limit scalable năng lượng (energy / 에너지) density. Coal and later fossil electricity expanded usable năng lượng (energy / 에너지) dramatically.

But tài nguyên (resource / 자원) presence alone does not cause industrialization; vận chuyển (transport / 전송) chi phí (cost / 비용), kỹ thuật (engineering / 엔지니어링), capital and institutions determine use.

## 4. General-purpose technologies

Steam, electricity, computing and similar technologies affect many sectors and require complementary investment.

Productivity gain can lag invention because factories, skills, standards and organizational practices must be redesigned.

## 5. Invention vs diffusion

Frontier innovation matters, but diffusion often explains large productivity differences.

A country can truy cập (access / 접근) toàn cục (global / 전역) technology yet thất bại (fail / 실패) to adopt because capital, skills, management, thị trường (market / 시장) kích thước (size / 크기) or institutions constrain returns.

## 6. Học tập (learning / 학습) by doing

Productivity can rise through cumulative môi trường vận hành (production / 운영 환경) experience.

If học tập (learning / 학습) spills over beyond firm, private investment may be below xã hội (social / 사회적) optimum, providing one rationale for temporary industrial chính sách (policy / 정책).

## 7. Human capital

Literacy, numeracy, kỹ thuật (engineering / 엔지니어링) and technical education complement machinery and organizational độ phức tạp (complexity / 복잡도).

Mass education may be both cause and consequence of industrialization, creating endogeneity in historical correlations.

## 8. Factory hệ thống (system / 시스템)

Factory organization centralizes workers/capital, enabling specialization, monitoring and machinery use.

It also changes labor discipline, urbanization and bargaining relations.

## 9. Division of labor

Specialization raises productivity when thị trường (market / 시장) kích thước (size / 크기) supports narrower tasks.

Vận chuyển (transport / 전송) and trade enlarge thị trường (market / 시장), enabling deeper division of labor.

## 10. Management technology

Productivity differences can reflect management practices, scheduling, chất lượng (quality / 품질) điều khiển (control / 제어) and thông tin (information / 정보) các hệ thống (systems / 시스템들), not only machines.

Organizational capital is difficult to measure but historically important.

## 11. Standardization

Interchangeable parts, technical standards, accounting units and chất lượng (quality / 품질) norms lower coordination costs.

Standards can expand markets while potentially locking in incumbent technology.

## 12. Vận chuyển (transport / 전송) revolutions

Canals, railways, steamships, roads, containers and aviation lower effective distance.

Thị trường (market / 시장) truy cập (access / 접근) changes land values, specialization, di chuyển (migration / 마이그레이션) and firm location.

## 13. Railways and nhân quả (causal / 인과적) identification

Railways were often built toward growing regions, so raw growth near rail lines is endogenous.

Historical studies use planned routes, terrain chi phí (cost / 비용) or truy cập mạng (network access / 네트워크 접근) designs, each requiring exclusion các giả định (assumptions / 가정들).

## 14. Communication technology

Telegraph, telephone and internet reduce thông tin (information / 정보) delay, improve price tích hợp (integration / 통합) and organizational quy mô (scale / 규모).

Lower communication chi phí (cost / 비용) changes both thị trường (market / 시장) efficiency and firm boundaries.

## 15. Agricultural productivity

Industrialization requires feeding urban population and releasing labor. Agricultural productivity gains can lower food prices, increase rural income and enable di chuyển (migration / 마이그레이션).

Agriculture is not merely a sector left behind.

## 16. Structural transformation

Development reallocates labor across agriculture, manufacturing and services.

Aggregate productivity thay đổi (change / 변경) decomposes into within-sector gains plus reallocation.

Premature deindustrialization may occur when manufacturing peaks at lower income levels due technology/trade patterns.

## 17. Urbanization

Cities create agglomeration economies through matching, sharing suppliers/hạ tầng (infrastructure / 인프라) and kiến thức (knowledge / 지식) spillovers.

They also create congestion, disease and housing costs. Net urban productivity depends on hạ tầng (infrastructure / 인프라) and quản trị (governance / 거버넌스).

## 18. Demographic chuyển tiếp (transition / 전이)

Mortality decline often precedes fertility decline. Education, urbanization and female labor opportunities alter child quantity-quality trade-offs.

Demographic dividend requires jobs/capital/human capital; age cấu trúc (structure / 구조) alone is not growth.

## 19. Capital accumulation

Industrial machinery/hạ tầng (infrastructure / 인프라) require finance and retained earnings.

High saving helps chuyển tiếp (transition / 전이) but permanent growth still needs productivity/innovation.

## 20. Firm kích thước (size / 크기) and legal organization

Joint-stock forms, limited liability and managerial hierarchies enable larger firms and long-lived capital projects.

But corporate quy mô (scale / 규모) also raises agency and competition concerns.

## 21. Patent institutions

Patent protection can increase invention incentives but restrict diffusion and follow-on innovation.

Historical effects depend technology and alternative appropriation mechanisms.

## 22. Science-industry linkage

Early invention can be craft-based; later sectors increasingly rely formal science, laboratories and universities.

National innovation các hệ thống (systems / 시스템들) connect basic research, firms, finance and skilled labor.

## 23. Trade and industrialization

Trade gives truy cập (access / 접근) to markets, inputs and technology but exposes domestic producers to competition.

Export-led growth, import substitution and industrial chính sách (policy / 정책) outcomes depend country kích thước (size / 크기), capabilities and toàn cục (global / 전역) regime.

## 24. Comparative advantage can evolve

Capabilities are not fixed. Học tập (learning / 학습), hạ tầng (infrastructure / 인프라), skills and firm entry can thay đổi (change / 변경) comparative advantage over thời gian (time / 시간).

This creates tension between static gains from specializing today and động (dynamic / 동적) năng lực (capability / 역량) building.

## 25. Infant-industry argument

Temporary protection/subsidy can be justified if sector has học tập (learning / 학습) spillovers and private firms cannot capture them.

Chính sách (policy / 정책) requires identifying real học tập (learning / 학습) externality, not merely young industry.

Thất bại (failure / 실패) modes include permanent protection and capture.

## 26. Globalization waves

Toàn cục (global / 전역) tích hợp (integration / 통합) varies with vận chuyển (transport / 전송), technology, capital controls, di chuyển (migration / 마이그레이션) chính sách (policy / 정책) and geopolitics.

Trade/GDP alone does not summarize globalization; capital, people, kiến thức (knowledge / 지식) and giá trị (value / 값) chains matter.

## 27. Di chuyển (migration / 마이그레이션)

Mass di chuyển (migration / 마이그레이션) reallocates labor toward higher-productivity locations and transmits kiến thức (knowledge / 지식)/remittances.

It also changes wages, housing and politics in origin/destination societies.

## 28. Capital mobility

International finance funds hạ tầng (infrastructure / 인프라) and trade but can create sudden-stop vulnerability if short-term/foreign-currency debt dominates.

Historical capital-market tích hợp (integration / 통합) is regime-dependent.

## 29. Colonial trade and extraction

Colonial các hệ thống (systems / 시스템들) altered trade routes, land rights, taxation and labor institutions.

Economic legacies differ by colony and institution; phân tích (analysis / 분석) should identify mechanisms rather than use “colonialism” as one uniform treatment.

## 30. Forced labor and coercive institutions

Coercive labor can generate đầu ra (output / 출력)/rents while weakening human capital, mobility and inclusive institutions.

Long-run persistence claims require careful historical identification and alternative-channel phân tích (analysis / 분석).

## 31. Toàn cục (global / 전역) giá trị (value / 값) chains

Hiện đại (modern / 현대적) môi trường vận hành (production / 운영 환경) fragments tasks internationally. Technology and logistics allow countries/firms to enter stages rather than whole industries.

Giá trị (value / 값) capture depends capabilities, bargaining and upgrading.

## 32. Deindustrialization

Manufacturing employment share can fall because manufacturing productivity grows faster, consumption shifts to services, or môi trường vận hành (production / 운영 환경) moves abroad.

Declining employment share does not necessarily mean declining manufacturing đầu ra (output / 출력).

## 33. Dịch vụ (service / 서비스) productivity

Services phạm vi (range / 범위) from low-productivity cục bộ (local / 로컬) công việc (work / 작업) to scalable digital/financial/professional services.

Treating “services” as one sector hides major differences.

## 34. Automation and labor displacement

Technology substitutes tasks while creating new demand/tasks. Short-run displacement can be concentrated even if long-run aggregate productivity gains.

Institutions for mobility, huấn luyện (training / 학습) and insurance shape adjustment.

## 35. Productivity slowdown

Measured productivity can slow due weak innovation, diffusion, investment, demographics, mismeasurement or sector composition.

Historical diagnosis should compare competing mechanisms.

## 36. Great divergence questions

Why industrialization began earlier in some regions is contested: wages/năng lượng (energy / 에너지), institutions, colonies/trade, scientific culture, geography and political competition are among explanations.

A serious account should not reduce a multi-century divergence to one variable.

## 37. Historical natural experiments

Borders, vận chuyển (transport / 전송) routes, war destruction, chính sách (policy / 정책) zones or technology rollout can create useful variation.

Historical dữ liệu (data / 데이터) chất lượng (quality / 품질), di chuyển (migration / 마이그레이션) and spillovers remain major threats.

## 38. Thất bại (failure / 실패) modes

Sai lầm thứ nhất là tell industrialization as chuỗi (sequence / 시퀀스) of inventions only.

Sai lầm thứ hai là infer nhân quả (causal / 인과적) tác động (effect / 효과) from technology adoption where richer regions adopted first.

Sai lầm thứ ba là assume comparative advantage fixed forever.

Sai lầm thứ tư là equate manufacturing employment share with industrial strength.

Sai lầm thứ năm là use one monocausal explanation for divergence.

## 39. Phân tích (analysis / 분석) template

Khi phân tích industrialization/globalization, hãy hỏi:

1. technology changes môi trường vận hành (production / 운영 환경) ràng buộc (constraint / 제약조건) nào?
2. complementary capital/skill/organization nào required?
3. adoption incentive/barrier là gì?
4. diffusion speed và học tập (learning / 학습) spillovers ra sao?
5. vận chuyển (transport / 전송)/thị trường (market / 시장) truy cập (access / 접근) thay specialization thế nào?
6. labor reallocation có mobility frictions nào?
7. finance/institutions hỗ trợ (support / 지원) long-horizon investment ra sao?
8. trade tác động (effect / 효과) static vs động (dynamic / 동적) năng lực (capability / 역량) khác nhau thế nào?
9. phân phối (distribution / 분포) across regions/classes/sectors ra sao?
10. historical identification phân biệt competing explanations thế nào?

Industrialization tạo sustained growth nhưng không loại bỏ cycles, wars và institutional reversals. Chapter tiếp theo tập trung crises và regime changes như stress tests của economic các hệ thống (systems / 시스템들).
