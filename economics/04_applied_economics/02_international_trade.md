# International Trade — Comparative advantage, trade costs, firms và phân phối (distribution / 분포)

International trade nghiên cứu vì sao countries, regions và firms specialize và exchange; ai được lợi hoặc chịu adjustment chi phí (cost / 비용); và tariffs, trade agreements, exchange rates, logistics hay toàn cục (global / 전역) giá trị (value / 값) chains thay đổi môi trường vận hành (production / 운영 환경) như thế nào. Trade lý thuyết (theory / 이론) bắt đầu từ comparative advantage, nhưng applied trade phải đi xa hơn: trade costs, heterogeneous firms, input-output linkages và labor-market adjustment quyết định tác động (effect / 효과) thực tế.

## 1. Comparative advantage

Comparative advantage phụ thuộc opportunity chi phí (cost / 비용), không phải absolute productivity.

Một country có thể productive hơn ở mọi goods nhưng vẫn gain from specialization nếu relative productivity differences khác nhau.

Trong Ricardian benchmark, trade cho phép consumption possibilities vượt domestic môi trường vận hành (production / 운영 환경) possibility frontier thông qua specialization theo comparative chi phí (cost / 비용).

## 2. Absolute advantage không quyết định trade mẫu (pattern / 패턴) một mình

Nếu country A cần ít labor hơn country B để sản xuất cả cloth và wine, A có absolute advantage cả hai. Nhưng nếu A đặc biệt productive hơn ở cloth tương đối wine, A vẫn có comparative advantage ở cloth.

Trade mẫu (pattern / 패턴) dựa relative opportunity chi phí (cost / 비용).

## 3. Terms of trade

Terms of trade là relative export price so với import price.

Trade gain phụ thuộc không chỉ productivity mà còn international relative prices. Large country có thể ảnh hưởng world price; small country thường take world price as given.

## 4. Ricardian các giả định (assumptions / 가정들) và limits

Simple Ricardian mô hình (model / 모델) thường có one factor, perfect mobility domestically, no trade costs và full employment.

Nó giải comparative advantage rõ nhưng không mô tả phân phối (distribution / 분포) giữa workers/factors hoặc chuyển tiếp (transition / 전이) costs tốt.

## 5. Heckscher–Ohlin intuition

Countries export goods intensive in factors they have relatively abundantly, under strong các giả định (assumptions / 가정들).

Capital-abundant economy tends toward capital-intensive exports; labor-abundant economy toward labor-intensive goods.

Real dữ liệu (data / 데이터) require technology, trade costs, institutions and toàn cục (global / 전역) môi trường vận hành (production / 운영 환경) chains beyond simple factor endowment.

## 6. Stolper–Samuelson

Trade-induced goods-price changes can raise real return to factor used intensively in export sector and lower return to scarce factor.

This shows aggregate gains from trade can coexist with domestic losers.

The theorem relies strong các giả định (assumptions / 가정들); empirical phân phối (distribution / 분포) works through occupations, firms and regions, not only abstract labor/capital.

## 7. Specific-factors mô hình (model / 모델)

In short run, some capital/skills are tied to sectors. Owners/workers in import-competing sectors may lose while export sectors gain.

This mô hình (model / 모델) captures chuyển tiếp (transition / 전이) and sector-specific adjustment better than long-run factor mobility các giả định (assumptions / 가정들).

## 8. Trade costs

Trade costs include:

```text
transport
insurance
tariffs
customs time
regulatory compliance
information/search
contract enforcement
language
border frictions
```

A small reduction in iceberg trade chi phí (cost / 비용) can strongly alter trade when many marginal suppliers are near competitiveness threshold.

## 9. Gravity mô hình (model / 모델)

Empirically bilateral trade is strongly related to economic kích thước (size / 크기) and inversely related to trade costs/distance.

Stylized gravity:

```text
Trade_ij ∝ (GDP_i × GDP_j) / TradeCost_ij
```

Hiện đại (modern / 현대적) structural gravity includes multilateral resistance: bilateral trade depends on relative ease of trading with all alternatives, not only pairwise distance.

## 10. Border tác động (effect / 효과)

Trade often drops sharply across national borders even after vật lý (physical / 물리적) distance, reflecting chính sách (policy / 정책), currency, thông tin (information / 정보), networks and institutional frictions.

Border is not one treatment; decomposing cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘) requires detailed dữ liệu (data / 데이터)/thiết kế (design / 설계).

## 11. Increasing returns and new trade lý thuyết (theory / 이론)

Trade can occur between similar countries because firms have fixed costs and increasing returns plus consumers giá trị (value / 값) variety.

Larger integrated thị trường (market / 시장) allows firms quy mô (scale / 규모) môi trường vận hành (production / 운영 환경) and consumers truy cập (access / 접근) more varieties.

This explains intra-industry trade beyond comparative advantage alone.

## 12. Heterogeneous firms

Only more productive firms may cover fixed chi phí (cost / 비용) of exporting.

Trade liberalization can:

```text
expand productive exporters
shrink/exit low-productivity firms
reallocate market share
raise aggregate productivity
```

This reallocation channel is central in hiện đại (modern / 현대적) trade lý thuyết (theory / 이론).

## 13. Selection into exporting

Exporters are typically larger/more productive before exporting. Raw exporter productivity premium does not prove exporting causes productivity.

Need designs around trade-cost shocks, thị trường (market / 시장) truy cập (access / 접근) changes or firm-level entry thresholds to distinguish selection vs learning-by-exporting.

## 14. Extensive vs intensive margin

Trade growth can come from:

- existing exporters selling more existing products;
- firms entering export thị trường (market / 시장);
- new sản phẩm (product / 제품) varieties;
- new destinations.

Chính sách (policy / 정책) effects differ by margin.

## 15. Tariff basics

Small-country import tariff raises domestic price roughly by tariff, reducing consumption and imports while expanding domestic môi trường vận hành (production / 운영 환경).

Government earns revenue; consumers lose; producers gain; deadweight mất mát (loss / 손실) arises from distorted môi trường vận hành (production / 운영 환경)/consumption.

## 16. Large-country tariff and terms-of-trade tác động (effect / 효과)

A country with thị trường (market / 시장) power may lower world export price through tariff, creating terms-of-trade gain that can offset domestic distortion in lý thuyết (theory / 이론).

This “optimal tariff” kết quả (result / 결과) invites retaliation and strategic tương tác (interaction / 상호작용); it is not a simple chính sách (policy / 정책) recommendation.

## 17. Quotas

Import quota restricts quantity. It raises domestic price similarly to tariff under some conditions, but quota rents depend on license allocation.

If foreign exporters capture rents, national welfare differs from equivalent tariff revenue kept domestically.

## 18. Export subsidies

Export subsidy raises domestic producer incentive and can expand exports but costs fiscal resources and may worsen terms of trade.

Hỗ trợ (support / 지원) can also provoke countervailing measures.

## 19. Non-tariff barriers

Standards, licensing, local-content rules, procurement and customs procedures can protect legitimate objectives or act as trade barriers.

Identification must distinguish compliance chi phí (cost / 비용) from chất lượng (quality / 품질)/an toàn (safety / 안전) benefits.

## 20. Trade agreements

Agreements reduce tariffs and address standards, services, investment, intellectual thuộc tính (property / 속성), procurement and dispute resolution.

Trade creation replaces high-cost domestic môi trường vận hành (production / 운영 환경) with lower-cost partner imports. Trade diversion can replace lower-cost nonmember imports with higher-cost partner imports because preferences discriminate.

## 21. Rules of origin

Preferential tariff requires proving sản phẩm (product / 제품) originates sufficiently in member country. Complex rules create compliance costs and can reshape supply chains.

Nominal zero tariff does not imply zero effective trade chi phí (cost / 비용).

## 22. Toàn cục (global / 전역) giá trị (value / 값) chains

Môi trường vận hành (production / 운영 환경) is split across countries. Imports can be inputs into exports.

Gross trade statistics double-count giá trị (value / 값) crossing borders multiple times, so value-added trade is important.

Protection on imported inputs can hurt downstream exporters.

## 23. Effective protection

Tariff on final goods and inputs jointly determine protection to domestic giá trị (value / 값) added.

High final tariff with low đầu vào (input / 입력) tariff can create effective protection far above nominal tỷ lệ (rate / 비율); đầu vào (input / 입력) tariffs can reduce it.

## 24. Input-output propagation

Trade shock to one industry propagates through suppliers/customers.

Sector exposure should account direct imports plus upstream/downstream linkages rather than only final sản phẩm (product / 제품) competition.

## 25. Trade and labor markets

Import competition can lower employment/wages in exposed industries/regions while export opportunities benefit others.

Adjustment can persist due to geographic mobility, specialized skills and cục bộ (local / 로컬) multipliers.

Aggregate gains do not imply each region recovers quickly.

## 26. China-shock style designs

A dùng chung (common / 공통) identification chiến lược (strategy / 전략) uses differential cục bộ (local / 로컬) industry exposure to bên ngoài (external / 외부) supply growth, instrumented by export growth to other high-income countries.

Credibility depends on exclusion and pre-trends; kết quả (result / 결과) is cục bộ (local / 로컬) to particular historical shock, not universal tác động (effect / 효과) of all trade.

## 27. Trade adjustment assistance

Chính sách (policy / 정책) can insure workers/regions against trade displacement through transfers, retraining or mobility hỗ trợ (support / 지원).

Thiết kế (design / 설계) should compare trade-specific aid with broader xã hội (social / 사회적) insurance: displaced workers care about income mất mát (loss / 손실) regardless of cause.

## 28. Offshoring

Offshoring relocates tasks rather than entire sectors. It can substitute domestic tasks, lower đầu vào (input / 입력) costs and expand firms.

Net employment/wage tác động (effect / 효과) depends tác vụ (task / 작업) complementarity and đầu ra (output / 출력) quy mô (scale / 규모).

## 29. Services trade

Digital delivery reduces geographic trade chi phí (cost / 비용) for software, finance, consulting and remote services.

Regulation, licensing, dữ liệu (data / 데이터) rules and ngôn ngữ (language / 언어) replace shipping chi phí (cost / 비용) as key frictions.

## 30. Trade and innovation

Larger thị trường (market / 시장) can raise innovation incentives; import competition can induce innovation or reduce rents/resources for R&D.

Effects are heterogeneous by distance from frontier, firm productivity and intellectual-property regime.

## 31. Exchange rates vs trade chính sách (policy / 정책)

Currency depreciation changes relative prices broadly; tariff changes specific border wedge.

Pass-through, invoicing currency and toàn cục (global / 전역) giá trị (value / 값) chains mean exchange-rate changes do not map one-for-one into trade quantities.

## 32. Trade balance misunderstanding

Bilateral trade deficit does not measure bilateral “mất mát (loss / 손실)”. Overall hiện tại (current / 현재) account is tied to national saving-investment balance.

Tariff on one partner can redirect sourcing without changing aggregate deficit if macro saving-investment conditions unchanged.

## 33. Strategic trade and national bảo mật (security / 보안)

Some sectors have học tập (learning / 학습) spillovers, quy mô (scale / 규모) economies or bảo mật (security / 보안) externalities that can motivate industrial chính sách (policy / 정책).

But identifying genuine spillover and choosing instrument without capture is difficult.

Trade restriction may be second-best if more targeted subsidy/bảo mật (security / 보안) procurement addresses externality directly.

## 34. Embargoes and sanctions

Trade restrictions can impose economic costs but adjustment through alternative suppliers, evasion and domestic substitution matters.

Nhân quả (causal / 인과적) evaluation needs counterfactual trends and geopolitical co-shocks.

## 35. Carbon border adjustments

A border carbon measure attempts align carbon chi phí (cost / 비용) across domestic/import môi trường vận hành (production / 운영 환경) and reduce leakage.

Thiết kế (design / 설계) requires measuring embedded emissions and handling trade/legal/administrative độ phức tạp (complexity / 복잡도).

## 36. Empirical trade toolkit

Dùng chung (common / 공통) designs include:

- tariff reform differences across industries;
- gravity with chính sách (policy / 정책) variation;
- regional exposure shift-share designs;
- firm customs dữ liệu (data / 데이터);
- border discontinuities;
- trade-agreement timing;
- shipping/freight shocks;
- sanctions/embargo sự kiện (event / 이벤트) studies.

Trade outcomes are endogenous to economic conditions, so naive trade-GDP correlations are weak bằng chứng (evidence / 증거).

## 37. Shift-share designs

Regional exposure often combines cục bộ (local / 로컬) industry shares with national/toàn cục (global / 전역) industry shocks.

Validity requires các giả định (assumptions / 가정들) about shocks and baseline shares; serial/spatial correlation and dùng chung (common / 공통) shocks need appropriate suy luận (inference / 추론).

## 38. Welfare decomposition

Applied trade welfare can include:

```text
consumer price effects
producer profits
variety
productivity/reallocation
tariff revenue
terms of trade
adjustment costs
distribution
externalities/security
```

One price chỉ mục (index / 인덱스) alone is not whole welfare tác động (effect / 효과).

## 39. Thất bại (failure / 실패) modes

Sai lầm thứ nhất là confuse comparative advantage with absolute productivity.

Sai lầm thứ hai là infer country welfare from bilateral deficit.

Sai lầm thứ ba là analyze tariffs without input-output/global-value-chain effects.

Sai lầm thứ tư là use exporter premium as proof exporting raises productivity.

Sai lầm thứ năm là trạng thái (state / 상태) aggregate gain while ignoring concentrated/cục bộ (local / 로컬) adjustment costs.

## 40. Applied phân tích (analysis / 분석) template

Khi phân tích trade question, hãy hỏi:

1. comparative advantage, quy mô (scale / 규모)/variety hay firm heterogeneity cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘) nào chính?
2. trade chi phí (cost / 비용) thay đổi ở tariff, logistics, regulation hay thông tin (information / 정보)?
3. affected margin là price, quantity, entry, sản phẩm (product / 제품) hay destination?
4. imported goods là final goods hay intermediate inputs?
5. incidence theo bên tiêu thụ (consumer / 소비자), worker, firm, region ra sao?
6. chính sách (policy / 정책)/shock variation nào identify tác động (effect / 효과)?
7. direct exposure và input-output spillover thế nào?
8. short-run specific factors vs long-run mobility khác nhau không?
9. hiện tại (current / 현재) account macro định danh (identity / 식별자) có bị nhầm với bilateral trade kết quả (result / 결과) không?
10. externality/bảo mật (security / 보안) mục tiêu (objective / 목표) có instrument targeted hơn không?

Trade lý thuyết (theory / 이론) giải lợi ích và specialization; Development Economics hỏi vì sao productivity, institutions, human capital và structural transformation khác nhau dai dẳng giữa economies và households.
