# Development Economics — Poverty, structural transformation, institutions và trạng thái (state / 상태) sức chứa (capacity / 용량)

Development economics hỏi vì sao một số societies thoát poverty nhanh, vì sao others stagnate, và policies nào cải thiện productivity, health, education, finance và institutional sức chứa (capacity / 용량). Đây không phải “macroeconomics cho nước nghèo”; household các ràng buộc (constraints / 제약조건들), cục bộ (local / 로컬) institutions, thị trường (market / 시장) failures và political economy quan trọng không kém aggregate growth.

## 1. Poverty đo lường (measurement / 측정)

Income/consumption poverty lines tạo threshold dễ theo dõi nhưng không capture toàn bộ deprivation.

Consumption often smoother and sometimes better measured than income in informal economies.

Multidimensional poverty may include health, schooling, housing and basic services, but weighting dimensions is normative.

## 2. Absolute vs relative poverty

Absolute poverty compares resources to fixed real threshold. Relative poverty depends on phân phối (distribution / 분포), often percentage of median income.

They answer different questions: basic material deprivation vs position within society.

## 3. Poverty gap

Headcount ratio only asks who is below line. Poverty gap measures how far poor households fall below threshold.

Two regions with same headcount can differ substantially in độ sâu (depth / 깊이) of poverty.

## 4. Inequality

Gini, percentile ratios and income shares summarize phân phối (distribution / 분포) differently.

High growth can reduce poverty while inequality rises; inequality can fall during stagnation. Phân phối (distribution / 분포) should not be inferred from average GDP.

## 5. Poverty traps

A poverty trap exists when low resources themselves reduce future ability to accumulate assets/human capital.

Possible mechanisms:

```text
malnutrition → low productivity
low assets → no profitable investment
credit constraints → underinvestment
risk → low-return safe choices
low state capacity → weak services
```

Bằng chứng (evidence / 증거) must show nonlinearity/persistence, not just correlation between poverty today and tomorrow.

## 6. Credit các ràng buộc (constraints / 제약조건들)

Households/firms with high-return projects may lack collateral or formal records.

Credit thị trường (market / 시장) suffers adverse selection, moral hazard and enforcement problems.

Observed low borrowing does not prove low demand; it may reflect rationing.

## 7. Microfinance

Microcredit can relax liquidity các ràng buộc (constraints / 제약조건들), but average effects on profits/consumption are often heterogeneous rather than transformative for everyone.

Program evaluation should distinguish borrowing take-up, nghiệp vụ (business / 비즈니스) investment, consumption smoothing and long-run income.

## 8. Savings các ràng buộc (constraints / 제약조건들)

Poor households may face giao dịch (transaction / 트랜잭션) fees, lack secure accounts, temptation/self-control problems or irregular income.

Simple savings products, commitment accounts or payment hạ tầng (infrastructure / 인프라) can alter accumulation even without credit.

## 9. Rủi ro (risk / 위험) and insurance

Farm households face weather, price and health shocks. Without insurance, they may choose lower-return but safer crops/jobs.

Rủi ro (risk / 위험) can therefore reduce average productivity ex ante, not only consumption ex post.

## 10. Chỉ mục (index / 인덱스) insurance

Weather-index insurance reduces moral hazard/xác minh (verification / 확인) chi phí (cost / 비용) but creates basis rủi ro (risk / 위험): chỉ mục (index / 인덱스) may not match individual mất mát (loss / 손실).

Low take-up may reflect basis rủi ro (risk / 위험), trust, liquidity and misunderstanding, not absence of insurance giá trị (value / 값).

## 11. Health and productivity

Health affects welfare directly and can raise labor productivity/học tập (learning / 학습).

Interventions such as deworming, vaccination, sanitation or malaria prevention may have externalities.

Cost-effectiveness and generalization vary by disease môi trường (environment / 환경).

## 12. Education quantity vs chất lượng (quality / 품질)

School enrollment can rise without học tập (learning / 학습) gains if teacher absence, weak curriculum or low instructional chất lượng (quality / 품질) persist.

Development chính sách (policy / 정책) should measure học tập (learning / 학습) outcomes, not only years enrolled.

## 13. Returns to education

Private wage returns differ from xã hội (social / 사회적) returns if education has externalities/signaling.

Truy cập (access / 접근) expansion may thay đổi (change / 변경) marginal student composition and labor-market returns.

## 14. Early childhood

Nutrition, health and stimulation can have động (dynamic / 동적) complementarities: early investments may raise returns to later schooling.

Long-run evaluation is important because immediate kiểm thử (test / 테스트) effects can understate thời gian tồn tại (lifetime / 수명) outcomes.

## 15. Fertility and demographic chuyển tiếp (transition / 전이)

As mortality, female education and opportunity chi phí (cost / 비용) of childrearing thay đổi (change / 변경), fertility often falls.

Lower fertility can create temporary demographic dividend if working-age share rises and labor markets absorb workers.

Demography is opportunity, not automatic growth.

## 16. Structural transformation

Development typically reallocates labor from low-productivity agriculture toward industry/services.

Aggregate productivity growth can come from both within-sector improvement and between-sector reallocation.

## 17. Agricultural productivity

Low productivity may reflect land fragmentation, missing irrigation, credit, rủi ro (risk / 위험), đầu vào (input / 입력) chất lượng (quality / 품질), extension thông tin (information / 정보) or insecure tenure.

One đầu vào (input / 입력) subsidy may thất bại (fail / 실패) if complementary các ràng buộc (constraints / 제약조건들) bind.

## 18. Technology adoption

Farmers/firms may not adopt profitable technology due to học tập (learning / 학습) bất định (uncertainty / 불확실성), fixed costs, rủi ro (risk / 위험) or mạng (network / 네트워크) effects.

Temporary subsidy can generate experimentation/học tập (learning / 학습), but permanent subsidy may distort if technology is not socially profitable.

## 19. Land rights

Secure tenure can increase investment by improving expected returns and collateral giá trị (value / 값).

Formal titling effects depend on enforcement and cục bộ (local / 로컬) customary các hệ thống (systems / 시스템들); formalization can also shift bargaining power.

## 20. Urbanization

Cities can raise productivity through agglomeration, matching and kiến thức (knowledge / 지식) spillovers, but congestion, housing and hạ tầng (infrastructure / 인프라) các ràng buộc (constraints / 제약조건들) matter.

Urban slums may reflect both poverty and truy cập (access / 접근) to productive urban opportunity.

## 21. Agglomeration economies

Firms/workers benefit from thick markets, suppliers and kiến thức (knowledge / 지식) proximity.

Place-based hạ tầng (infrastructure / 인프라) may unlock agglomeration, but subsidies can simply relocate activity from nearby areas.

## 22. Hạ tầng (infrastructure / 인프라)

Roads, electricity, water and digital networks lower trade/môi trường vận hành (production / 운영 환경) costs and expand thị trường (market / 시장) truy cập (access / 접근).

Effects depend on complementary institutions and who gains truy cập (access / 접근).

## 23. Thị trường (market / 시장) truy cập (access / 접근)

Remote regions face high vận chuyển (transport / 전송) chi phí (cost / 비용) and thin markets. New roads can raise producer prices, lower bên tiêu thụ (consumer / 소비자) prices and shift sector composition.

Empirical designs use road rollout, historical routes or kỹ thuật (engineering / 엔지니어링) chi phí (cost / 비용) variation, but placement endogeneity is a major challenge.

## 24. Institutions

Thuộc tính (property / 속성) rights, courts, taxation sức chứa (capacity / 용량), bureaucracy and political accountability shape investment and public-good provision.

Institutions are endogenous to lịch sử (history / 이력) and phân phối (distribution / 분포) of power, so cross-country correlation is insufficient nhân quả (causal / 인과적) bằng chứng (evidence / 증거).

## 25. Trạng thái (state / 상태) sức chứa (capacity / 용량)

Trạng thái (state / 상태) sức chứa (capacity / 용량) includes ability to tax, collect thông tin (information / 정보), enforce rules and deliver services.

Low tax revenue can reflect low income, informality, weak administrative các hệ thống (systems / 시스템들) or low legitimacy.

Building sức chứa (capacity / 용량) is động (dynamic / 동적): tax các hệ thống (systems / 시스템들) may finance công khai (public / 공개) goods that strengthen compliance and growth.

## 26. Corruption

Corruption can distort procurement, licensing and công khai (public / 공개) spending. But broad corruption indices mix perceptions and multiple mechanisms.

Chính sách (policy / 정책) should mục tiêu (target / 대상) specific discretion/accountability/thông tin (information / 정보) channels rather than treat “corruption” as one variable.

## 27. Political economy

Policies create winners/losers who influence institutions. Inefficient chính sách (policy / 정책) can persist because rents are concentrated and reform gains diffuse.

Development economics therefore cannot assume benevolent planner.

## 28. Colonial/historical persistence

Historical institutions can shape land phân phối (distribution / 분포), education, legal các hệ thống (systems / 시스템들) and trạng thái (state / 상태) sức chứa (capacity / 용량) long after original shock.

Historical IV/natural experiments can be informative but exclusion/generalization are often contested.

## 29. Aid

Foreign aid can finance health/hạ tầng (infrastructure / 인프라) and provide insurance after disasters. It may also weaken cục bộ (local / 로컬) accountability, create fragmentation or respond endogenously to crises.

Aid effectiveness varies by modality and institution; country-level correlation is not enough.

## 30. Cash transfers

Unconditional/conditional cash transfers can reduce poverty and hỗ trợ (support / 지원) investment. Conditionality may raise targeted hành vi (behavior / 동작) but imposes compliance/administrative costs.

Cash benchmark is useful when comparing complex in-kind programs.

## 31. Universal basic income

UBI removes eligibility cliffs and administrative targeting but has high fiscal chi phí (cost / 비용) if meaningful in kích thước (size / 크기).

Labor-supply, financing and phân phối (distribution / 분포) effects depend on what taxes/programs replace it.

## 32. Industrial chính sách (policy / 정책)

Government may hỗ trợ (support / 지원) sectors with learning-by-doing, coordination failures or strategic spillovers.

Risks include mis-targeting, capture and inability to withdraw hỗ trợ (support / 지원).

A credible industrial chính sách (policy / 정책) needs measurable học tập (learning / 학습)/export/productivity targets and exit discipline.

## 33. Export-led development

Exports can expand thị trường (market / 시장) kích thước (size / 크기), discipline firms and accelerate technology adoption. But toàn cục (global / 전역) demand, exchange rates, imported inputs and domestic capabilities matter.

Historical success does not imply identical chiến lược (strategy / 전략) is available to every country/thời gian (time / 시간).

## 34. Tài nguyên (resource / 자원) curse

Tài nguyên (resource / 자원) wealth can raise income but also increase volatility, rent-seeking, exchange-rate pressure and institutional xung đột (conflict / 충돌).

Kết quả (outcome / 결과) depends on fiscal rules, quyền sở hữu (ownership / 소유권) and pre-existing institutions.

## 35. Randomized development economics

RCTs identify cục bộ (local / 로컬) interventions well when hiện thực (implementation / 구현) is controllable.

Limits include bên ngoài (external / 외부) validity, equilibrium effects and focus on interventions that are easy to randomize rather than necessarily largest structural các ràng buộc (constraints / 제약조건들).

## 36. Structural and macro development methods

Some questions require general-equilibrium or động (dynamic / 동적) các mô hình (models / 모델들): di chuyển (migration / 마이그레이션), trade reform, urbanization, tax sức chứa (capacity / 용량) or industrial chính sách (policy / 정책).

Reduced-form and structural approaches answer complementary questions.

## 37. Program take-up

Low take-up can reflect price, thông tin (information / 정보), trust, distance, paperwork or xã hội (social / 사회적) norms.

Diagnosing the friction is often more useful than only estimating average program tác động (effect / 효과).

## 38. Heterogeneity

Intervention can help constrained households strongly and unconstrained households little.

Average effects can obscure poverty-trap thresholds or differences by gender, geography and baseline assets.

## 39. General equilibrium

Huấn luyện (training / 학습) everyone in one skill can reduce its wage premium. Microcredit at quy mô (scale / 규모) can thay đổi (change / 변경) cục bộ (local / 로컬) competition. Road building can shift firms and land prices.

Scale-up must include thị trường (market / 시장) responses.

## 40. Thất bại (failure / 실패) modes

Sai lầm thứ nhất là equate GDP growth with broad development.

Sai lầm thứ hai là infer poverty trap from persistence alone.

Sai lầm thứ ba là generalize one RCT across countries/institutions automatically.

Sai lầm thứ tư là use broad institution/corruption chỉ mục (index / 인덱스) as nhân quả (causal / 인과적) cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘).

Sai lầm thứ năm là treat structural transformation as painless worker mobility.

## 41. Applied phân tích (analysis / 분석) template

Khi phân tích development question, hãy hỏi:

1. ràng buộc (constraint / 제약조건) chính là credit, rủi ro (risk / 위험), health, human capital, thị trường (market / 시장) truy cập (access / 접근) hay institution?
2. cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘) household/firm/trạng thái (state / 상태) nào tạo persistence?
3. treatment/chính sách (policy / 정책) nhắm đúng binding ràng buộc (constraint / 제약조건) không?
4. kết quả (outcome / 결과) là income, consumption, productivity, học tập (learning / 학습) hay resilience?
5. selection/take-up có làm comparison sai không?
6. thiết kế (design / 설계) identify cục bộ (local / 로컬) tác động (effect / 효과) nào?
7. complementary các ràng buộc (constraints / 제약조건들) có làm program thất bại (fail / 실패) không?
8. phân phối (distribution / 분포) và gender/geography heterogeneity ra sao?
9. scale-up tạo prices/di chuyển (migration / 마이그레이션)/competition effects gì?
10. trạng thái (state / 상태) sức chứa (capacity / 용량)/political economy có khiến hiện thực (implementation / 구현) khác mô hình (model / 모델) không?

Development economics đưa economics về institutional reality: same price tín hiệu (signal / 신호) có thể tạo kết quả rất khác khi credit, trạng thái (state / 상태) sức chứa (capacity / 용량), geography và xã hội (social / 사회적) các ràng buộc (constraints / 제약조건들) khác nhau. Industrial Organization tiếp tục applied lô-gic (logic / 논리) này ở cấp firms/industries, nơi demand, markups, entry và strategic conduct cần được đo trực tiếp.
