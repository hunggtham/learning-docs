# Fiscal chính sách (policy / 정책) & nghiệp vụ (business / 비즈니스) Cycles — Demand, multipliers, debt và stabilization

> **Mạch đọc:** Đặt **Fiscal chính sách (policy / 정책) & nghiệp vụ (business / 비즈니스) Cycles — Demand, multipliers, debt và stabilization** trong bản đồ [README](./README.md) để thấy đơn vị sở hữu (owner / 오너) và vị trí của nó. Nội dung đi từ **1. Aggregate demand và short-run đầu ra (output / 출력)** sang **2. Consumption hàm (function / 함수) and MPC**; điểm nối này chuẩn bị câu hỏi cho các mục sau thay vì dừng ở định nghĩa đầu tiên.

Fiscal chính sách (policy / 정책) thay đổi government spending, taxes và transfers. Nó có thể ổn định aggregate demand trong recession, bảo hiểm household income hoặc finance công khai (public / 공개) investment; đồng thời tạo financing needs, phân phối (distribution / 분포) effects và long-run incentive trade-offs. Một deficit không tự nói chính sách (policy / 정책) tốt hay xấu: cần biết cycle position, interest-growth quan hệ (relation / 관계), spending composition và institutional credibility.

## 1. Aggregate demand và short-run đầu ra (output / 출력)

Trong simple Keynesian framing:

```text
Y = C + I + G + NX
```

Nếu prices/wages sticky và resources idle, fall in private demand can reduce đầu ra (output / 출력) and employment rather than only prices.

Fiscal expansion can partially fill demand gap. If economy already near sức chứa (capacity / 용량), more demand may crowd out private activity or raise inflation.

## 2. Consumption hàm (function / 함수) and MPC

Stylized consumption:

```text
C = C0 + c(Y − T)
```

`c` is marginal propensity to consume (MPC). A higher MPC means transfers/tax cuts translate more strongly into near-term spending.

But households differ. Liquidity-constrained households may have high MPC; wealthy households may save more of temporary transfer.

## 3. Simple spending multiplier

In the simplest closed-economy fixed-price mô hình (model / 모델):

```text
Multiplier = 1 / (1 − c)
```

A government-spending increase raises income, which induces more consumption, creating rounds of demand.

This is a teaching benchmark. Real multipliers are smaller or larger depending on imports, taxes, monetary phản hồi (response / 응답), slack, expectations and financing.

## 4. Tax and transfer multipliers

Tax cut raises disposable income, but only the consumed share directly boosts demand. Transfer to high-MPC households may have stronger short-run tác động (effect / 효과) than same transfer to low-MPC households.

Composition matters as much as headline fiscal kích thước (size / 크기).

## 5. Automatic stabilizers

Progressive taxes and unemployment benefits automatically moderate cycles without new legislation.

In downturn, tax revenue falls and transfers rise, supporting disposable income. In boom, reverse happens.

Automatic stabilizers act faster than discretionary packages but may be too weak for exceptional shocks.

## 6. Discretionary fiscal chính sách (policy / 정책) and lags

Fiscal chính sách (policy / 정책) faces:

```text
recognition lag
legislative lag
implementation lag
impact lag
```

Hạ tầng (infrastructure / 인프라) can have high long-run return but slow rollout. Fast transfers may stabilize demand quickly but add less productive sức chứa (capacity / 용량).

## 7. Multiplier depends on slack

When unemployment high and firms have unused sức chứa (capacity / 용량), demand can raise real đầu ra (output / 출력) strongly. Near full sức chứa (capacity / 용량), same stimulus more likely raises prices and interest rates.

Therefore a multiplier is state-dependent, not one universal constant.

## 8. Monetary-fiscal tương tác (interaction / 상호작용)

If central bank offsets fiscal expansion by raising rates aggressively, multiplier is smaller. If chính sách (policy / 정책) tỷ lệ (rate / 비율) is constrained at lower bound or central bank accommodates demand khôi phục (recovery / 복구), multiplier may be larger.

This is why fiscal tác động (effect / 효과) cannot be analyzed independently of monetary regime.

## 9. Open-economy leakage

Part of fiscal stimulus may increase imports, transferring demand abroad. Flexible exchange tỷ lệ (rate / 비율) and capital flows can further alter tác động (effect / 효과).

Small open economies often have lower domestic multiplier unless chính sách (policy / 정책) changes exchange tỷ lệ (rate / 비율) or trade phản hồi (response / 응답) differently.

## 10. Crowding out

Government borrowing can raise interest rates or absorb financing that private investment would otherwise use, especially near sức chứa (capacity / 용량) or when monetary chính sách (policy / 정책) tightens.

But in deep recession with weak private borrowing, crowding out may be small; fiscal expansion can even crowd in investment by raising expected demand.

## 11. Ricardian equivalence as benchmark

Ricardian equivalence says forward-looking households may save a debt-financed tax cut because they anticipate future taxes, leaving demand unchanged.

The kết quả (result / 결과) requires strong các giả định (assumptions / 가정들): perfect capital markets, rational foresight, lump-sum taxes, long horizons and no borrowing các ràng buộc (constraints / 제약조건들).

Real households violate many các giả định (assumptions / 가정들), so theorem is a benchmark about intertemporal ngân sách (budget / 예산) các ràng buộc (constraints / 제약조건들), not a universal empirical law.

## 12. Government ngân sách (budget / 예산) ràng buộc (constraint / 제약조건)

Simplified debt dynamics:

```text
B_t = (1 + r)B_{t−1} + Primary Deficit_t
```

Relative to GDP:

```text
Δ(B/Y) depends on
primary balance + (r − g) × debt ratio
```

If nominal growth `g` exceeds effective interest tỷ lệ (rate / 비율) `r`, debt ratio is easier to stabilize for a given primary balance.

## 13. Debt sustainability is path-dependent

High debt can be sustainable with credible taxes, long maturity, domestic currency and strong growth; lower debt can still trigger crisis if foreign-currency exposure, rollover rủi ro (risk / 위험) or political credibility weak.

Debt ratio alone is not enough.

## 14. Fiscal không gian (space / 공간)

Fiscal không gian (space / 공간) is sức chứa (capacity / 용량) to use fiscal chính sách (policy / 정책) without creating unacceptable financing/inflation rủi ro (risk / 위험).

It depends on tax sức chứa (capacity / 용량), institutions, monetary regime, currency denomination, maturity, investor cơ sở (base / 기반) and growth prospects.

## 15. công khai (public / 공개) investment vs hiện tại (current / 현재) spending

Government spending that raises future productive sức chứa (capacity / 용량) can affect both demand now and supply later.

Hạ tầng (infrastructure / 인프라), research or health investment may have long-run returns, but dự án (project / 프로젝트) selection and thực thi (execution / 실행) chất lượng (quality / 품질) matter. Calling every expenditure “investment” does not make it productive.

## 16. Fiscal insurance and phân phối (distribution / 분포)

Transfers can stabilize household consumption when private insurance/credit markets thất bại (fail / 실패). xã hội (social / 사회적) insurance may also reduce precautionary saving and improve rủi ro (risk / 위험) sharing.

But program thiết kế (design / 설계) changes labor-supply and take-up incentives. phân phối (distribution / 분포) and efficiency need joint phân tích (analysis / 분석).

## 17. Supply-side fiscal chính sách (policy / 정책)

Taxes affect labor, saving, investment, entrepreneurship and avoidance. Tax cơ sở (base / 기반) thiết kế (design / 설계) matters more than marginal tỷ lệ (rate / 비율) in isolation.

A reform can broaden cơ sở (base / 기반) while lowering tỷ lệ (rate / 비율), or shift burden across income, consumption, thuộc tính (property / 속성) and externalities.

## 18. nghiệp vụ (business / 비즈니스) cycle as co-movement

Nghiệp vụ (business / 비즈니스) cycle is not one variable. đầu ra (output / 출력), employment, consumption, investment, credit and profits often co-move with different volatility and timing.

Investment is usually more volatile than consumption; durable goods and inventories amplify cycles.

## 19. Inventory cycle

Unexpected demand weakness creates inventory accumulation; firms cut future môi trường vận hành (production / 운영 환경) to reduce stock, amplifying downturn. Strong demand can create inventory drawdown and restocking boom.

Inventory is small share of GDP but large contributor to short-run changes.

## 20. Accelerator cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘)

If desired capital depends on expected đầu ra (output / 출력), small thay đổi (change / 변경) in growth expectations can create large swing in investment.

This helps explain why investment is more cyclical than consumption.

## 21. Financial accelerator

Falling asset prices and income weaken borrower net worth, raising external-finance premium and tightening credit. Lower investment then weakens economy further.

Balance-sheet phản hồi (feedback / 피드백) can amplify ordinary demand shock into severe recession.

## 22. Real nghiệp vụ (business / 비즈니스) cycle perspective

RBC các mô hình (models / 모델들) emphasize real shocks such as productivity and optimize intertemporal responses with flexible prices.

They are useful for disciplined động (dynamic / 동적) lập luận (reasoning / 추론) but have difficulty explaining some unemployment/monetary phenomena without added frictions.

## 23. New Keynesian perspective

New Keynesian các mô hình (models / 모델들) add nominal rigidities and imperfect competition to intertemporal tối ưu hóa (optimization / 최적화). This creates short-run role for monetary/fiscal stabilization.

The important lesson is not choosing one “school” by label; it is understanding which friction makes chính sách (policy / 정책) non-neutral.

## 24. Demand vs supply shock

Demand shock moves đầu ra (output / 출력) and inflation often in same direction; adverse supply shock can move đầu ra (output / 출력) down while inflation up.

Chính sách (policy / 정책) phản hồi (response / 응답) differs because stabilizing inflation after supply shock may worsen đầu ra (output / 출력) mất mát (loss / 손실).

## 25. Pandemic-style shock

A pandemic can combine supply các ràng buộc (constraints / 제약조건들), sector-specific demand collapse, transfers, reopening demand and logistics bottlenecks. Single demand/supply label may be insufficient.

Macro phân tích (analysis / 분석) should decompose phases and mechanisms rather than force whole episode into one curve shift.

## 26. Fiscal dominance and inflation

If persistent deficits cannot be financed through future primary surpluses and monetary authority must accommodate, fiscal conditions can undermine price stability.

But deficit itself does not mechanically cause inflation; spare sức chứa (capacity / 용량), financing cấu trúc (structure / 구조), credibility and monetary phản hồi (response / 응답) matter.

## 28. thất bại (failure / 실패) modes

Sai lầm thứ nhất là dùng một fiscal multiplier cố định cho mọi trạng thái.

Sai lầm thứ hai là coi deficit và debt là cùng một variable.

Sai lầm thứ ba là nói crowding out luôn xảy ra cùng magnitude.

Sai lầm thứ tư là đánh giá debt sustainability chỉ bằng debt/GDP.

Sai lầm thứ năm là bỏ monetary phản hồi (response / 응답) khi ước tính fiscal impact.

## 29. mô hình tư duy (mental model / 사고 모델)

Khi phân tích fiscal/nghiệp vụ (business / 비즈니스) cycle, hãy hỏi:

1. Economy đang dưới hay gần potential?
2. Shock demand hay supply, temporary hay persistent?
3. Household recipients có MPC khác nhau không?
4. Monetary chính sách (policy / 정책) sẽ accommodate hay offset?
5. Imports/capital flows tạo leakage nào?
6. Spending là transfer, hiện tại (current / 현재) purchase hay productive investment?
7. Deficit thay đổi debt maturity/currency cấu trúc (structure / 구조) ra sao?
8. `r − g` và primary balance trend thế nào?
9. Balance-sheet/credit phản hồi (feedback / 피드백) có amplify shock không?
10. Short-run stabilization có ảnh hưởng long-run sức chứa (capacity / 용량) không?

Domestic stabilization vẫn chưa đủ trong open economy. Trade balance, capital flows và exchange rates nối saving/investment decisions giữa các countries và tạo thêm chính sách (policy / 정책) các ràng buộc (constraints / 제약조건들).
