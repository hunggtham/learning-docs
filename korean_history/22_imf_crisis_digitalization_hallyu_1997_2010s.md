# Từ IMF crisis tới digital Korea và Hallyu

## 1997 như institutional shock

South Korea ký rescue package với IMF cuối 1997 giữa foreign-exchange crisis. “IMF era” (IMF 시대) trong everyday memory không chỉ là macroeconomics; nó gắn với layoffs, restructuring, household insecurity và thay đổi employment norm.

Corporate restructuring, financial reform và labor-market flexibility tăng. Lifetime employment expectation suy yếu trong nhiều sector; irregular employment trở nên visible hơn. Vì vậy post-1997 culture of competition và credential pressure có economic history cụ thể.

## Broadband infrastructure

Late 1990s–2000s, broadband rollout rất nhanh. Dense urban housing, telecom competition, government policy và educated consumer base giúp diffusion. Internet cafes/PC bang tạo shared access trước khi home broadband universal.

Network effect làm online portal, gaming và messenger ecosystem phát triển. Technology không chỉ thêm convenience; nó thay đổi coordination cost của communities, fandom và commerce.

## Hallyu

Korean Wave (한류) ban đầu nổi ở East Asia qua television drama rồi mở rộng sang K-pop, film, games, webtoon, beauty và food. Hallyu không thể giải thích chỉ bằng “government made it”. Entertainment firms, training system, broadband, platform distribution, diaspora, fan labor và global media shifts đều tham gia.

## 2002 World Cup

Co-hosted 2002 FIFA World Cup tạo mass street cheering và new image of civic public space. Event trở thành generational memory và nation-branding moment.

## Mental Model

> Post-1997 Korea là câu chuyện một shock economic buộc institutions restructure, trong khi digital infrastructure đồng thời tạo những industry và social network mới. Crisis và innovation không phải hai câu chuyện tách rời.

## Labor dualization

Post-crisis reform made firms more able to restructure workforce. Core regular employees and non-regular workers increasingly experienced different security, benefit and bargaining power. This dualization affects marriage, fertility and housing decisions because income risk is household planning input.

## Venture boom and state policy

Government promoted IT startups and broadband; chaebol also invested heavily. Dot-com style boom had failures, but failure can still build engineer pool and entrepreneurial knowledge. Ecosystem learning remains after individual firms disappear.

## Online gaming and virtual economies

PC bang density plus broadband enabled competitive online games and esports. Game server, latency, ranking and persistent account identity produced new social spaces. History of technology becomes history of leisure, profession and fandom simultaneously.

## Cultural production as industrial process

K-pop training, choreography, music production, video, social media and fan translation form a supply chain. Cultural success still depends creativity, but production/distribution infrastructure changes probability that creativity reaches global audience.
