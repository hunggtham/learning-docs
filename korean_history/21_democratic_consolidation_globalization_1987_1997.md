# 1987–1997: democratic consolidation và globalization

## Institutionalizing competition

Sau 1987, direct presidential elections trở lại. Political competition dần trở thành routine institution thay vì existential crisis mỗi lần leadership change. Civil society, press và labor movements mở rộng space, dù nhiều authoritarian-era laws/institutions tiếp tục gây debate.

## 1988 Seoul Olympics

Seoul Olympics là global showcase và infrastructure catalyst. Mega-event không chỉ là sport; nó là city redevelopment, branding, transport investment và diplomatic signal rằng South Korea đang bước vào một international position mới.

## Civilian government và reform

1990s chứng kiến civilian presidency, anti-corruption drives, reform of financial transparency và prosecution of former military leaders liên quan 1979–1980 events. Đây là ví dụ **transitional justice**: democracy phải quyết định xử lý legacy của previous regime ra sao.

## Segyehwa và integration vào global economy

“Segyehwa” (세계화, globalization) trở thành policy keyword. Korea gia nhập OECD năm 1996. Firms borrow and expand aggressively; financial liberalization tăng exposure to global capital flows.

## Vulnerability trước 1997

Rapid growth không loại bỏ fragility. High leverage, maturity mismatch và weak financial supervision tạo systemic risk. Khi regional Asian Financial Crisis lan rộng, South Korea đối mặt severe foreign-currency liquidity crisis.

## Knowledge Connection: robustness vs growth

Một system có throughput cao không đồng nghĩa robust. Trong engineering, optimization quá sát một operating condition có thể giảm resilience. Economy cũng vậy: leverage giúp expansion nhưng làm shock transmission mạnh hơn.

## Alternation and trust

Democracy becomes more robust when losing an election no longer means physical elimination of losers. Repeated peaceful competition updates actor beliefs: today’s loser may win later, making compliance rational. This repeated-game logic is central to democratic consolidation.

## Local autonomy

Revival of local elections in 1990s redistributed some decision-making away from central state. Local government matters because urban planning, welfare and development conflict differ by region. Decentralization creates experimentation but also local inequality/capture risk.

## Chaebol expansion abroad

Korean firms internationalized production and branding in 1990s. Larger global scale created foreign-currency borrowing and investment exposure. Growth strategy shifted from simple export manufacturing toward global corporate networks.

## 1997 as sudden stop

Firms/banks with short-term foreign debt relied on ability to roll debt. When lenders withdrew, liquidity crisis could occur even if physical factories still existed. Finance introduces time mismatch: asset may be valuable long term but debt due tomorrow can still bankrupt owner.
