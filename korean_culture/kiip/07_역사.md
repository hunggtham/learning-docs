# 07. 역사 — Lịch sử Hàn Quốc (38~44과)

> **Vai trò:** canonical historical foundation cho KIIP. Không học lịch sử như `왕 이름 + năm + di sản`. Mỗi period phải trả lời **xã hội trước đó gặp vấn đề gì → actor nào nổi lên → institution/technology/war nào thay đổi system → điều gì còn lại trong Korean identity, place names và public memory hôm nay**.

# Timeline trước, rồi mới zoom

```text
고조선
→ 삼국: 고구려·백제·신라
→ 남북국: 통일신라·발해
→ 고려
→ 조선
→ 개항기·대한제국
→ 일제강점기
→ 광복·분단
→ 대한민국 정부 수립·한국전쟁
→ 산업화·권위주의
→ 민주화
→ 현대 대한민국
```

Timeline là map, không phải knowledge. Ta cần biết **vì sao chuyển period**.

# 38과 — 고조선과 고대 국가

## 단군왕검 và founding memory

`단군왕검` gắn với founding narrative của Gojoseon; `기원전 2333년` là traditional date, không nên trình bày như archaeologically verified exact founding date.

Tại sao myth vẫn quan trọng? Founding myth tạo shared origin story. `개천절` hiện nay nối trực tiếp với memory này. Vì thế `단군` vừa là ancient-history keyword vừa là modern national-symbol vocabulary.

## 8조법 — từ vài điều luật suy ra society

Records về `8조법` chỉ truyền lại một phần, nhưng textbook dùng chúng để infer rằng society đã có protection of life/property và social hierarchy. Historical reasoning ở đây là **law reflects social structure**.

# 39과 — 삼국 và competition for the peninsula

## 고구려

Goguryeo phát triển ở northern peninsula/Manchurian region, nổi bật military expansion. `광개토대왕` gắn expansion; `장수왕` nối với southward policy và Pyongyang. Khi nghe `고구려`, neighborhood nên gồm northern geography, military state, 광개토대왕 và conflict với Sui/Tang.

`을지문덕` gắn với victory at `살수대첩` against Sui forces. Đây không chỉ là hero name: nó phản ánh geopolitical pressure từ continental empires.

## 백제

Baekje phát triển ở Han River region rồi chuyển capital south under pressure. Nó nổi bật maritime exchange và cultural transmission với Japanese archipelago. `무령왕릉` là major archaeological heritage giúp thấy international exchange through artifacts.

## 신라

Silla phát triển ở southeast, với `경주` là capital. `화랑`/`화랑도` là well-known social-military youth institution in textbook memory. Silla allied with Tang, defeated Baekje/Goguryeo and eventually controlled most of peninsula south of Daedong River after conflict with Tang.

`경주` vì vậy không chỉ là tourist city. Người Hàn nghe Gyeongju thường nối `신라 → 불국사 → 석굴암 → 첨성대 → 대릉원`. Geography và heritage là fossilized history.

# 남북국 — 통일신라와 발해

Sau fall of Goguryeo/Baekje, south was dominated by Unified Silla while `발해`, founded by `대조영`, developed in northern territories. Korean historiography often uses `남북국 시대` để tránh narrative rằng entire northern historical space biến mất sau Silla unification.

Unified Silla culture gắn với Buddhist heritage such as `불국사` và `석굴암`; `첨성대` belongs earlier Silla and is associated with astronomical observation/history of science.

# 40과 — 고려: reunification, Buddhism và international exchange

`왕건` founded Goryeo in 918 and reunified Later Three Kingdoms. Name `Korea` derives historically from Goryeo through foreign usage, nên dynasty name vẫn sống trong international name of country.

## 불교 và state

Buddhism was major religious/cultural force. `팔만대장경` — woodblocks of Buddhist canon — was produced in context of Mongol invasions and prayer/protection of state. Its importance lies in scale, preservation, printing knowledge and historical context, not just “nhiều tấm gỗ”. It is preserved at `해인사`.

## 청자 · 벽란도 · 국제교류

`고려청자`, especially `상감청자`, reflects craft technology/aesthetics. `벽란도` was an international trade port near Gaegyeong; it helps correct image of premodern Korea as completely isolated.

## 직지

`직지` is associated with metal movable-type printing and is famous as the oldest extant book printed with movable metal type recognized in this context. Its neighborhood is **printing technology → reproduction of knowledge → comparison with woodblock printing**, not simply a heritage title.

# 41과 — 조선: Confucian state and knowledge system

## 왜 고려에서 조선으로?

Late Goryeo faced political conflict, powerful families, land problems and external/geopolitical change. `이성계` founded Joseon in 1392. New dynasty shifted ideological/institutional center strongly toward `성리학` (Neo-Confucianism).

Capital moved to `한양`, today's Seoul. This is why Seoul's urban geography is historical text:

```text
한양 도성
├─ 경복궁: royal palace
├─ 종묘: royal ancestral shrine
├─ 사직단: state ritual space
├─ 숭례문·흥인지문: city gates
└─ 북촌·종로 등: urban historical layers
```

Modern `서울` therefore sits on Joseon capital structure; palace/gate names are not random tourist sites.

## 신분질서

Textbook commonly uses `양반·중인·상민·천민` to understand status hierarchy. Real historical status was more complex/changeable, but model shows that occupation, legal/social privilege and family status were structured unequally.

## 세종대왕 — why he occupies huge Korean cultural memory

Sejong's reign is associated with state capacity, knowledge production and `훈민정음` creation. Before Hangul, literacy depended heavily on Classical Chinese literacy, creating high learning barrier.

```text
문자 접근 장벽
→ 훈민정음 창제
→ 소리를 체계적으로 표기
→ 문자 접근성 확대
→ 오늘날 한글 identity
```

`측우기`, `앙부일구`, `자격루` belong broader scientific-administrative context of measurement of rain, time and calendrical/state knowledge. Don't attribute every device personally to Sejong; court scholars/institutions mattered.

## 경국대전

`경국대전` is Joseon legal/administrative code completed after Sejong's reign. Its significance is institutionalization: state administration becomes codified rather than dependent only on ad hoc royal orders.

# 임진왜란 và 병자호란 — war reshapes memory

`임진왜란` began with Japanese invasion in 1592. `이순신` is central public-memory figure because naval victories helped protect sea supply routes. `거북선` belongs this memory, but war was not won by one hero alone: Joseon forces, righteous armies (`의병`), Ming intervention and logistics all matter.

`병자호란` in 1636 involved Qing invasion and forced Joseon submission. It belongs transition in East Asian order from Ming-centered to Qing dominance and produced deep ideological/political trauma.

# 영조·정조, 탕평책, 실학 và 수원 화성

Factional conflict (`붕당정치`) became a major political problem. `탕평책` sought to reduce extreme factional exclusion. `영조` and `정조` are strongly associated with this policy direction.

`정조` connects to `수원 화성`, planned city/fortress project. `정약용` and technologies such as `거중기` are commonly connected to construction/Practical Learning (`실학`) context. Suwon Hwaseong is therefore architecture + state planning + technology + political history, not only a tourist wall.

`실학` broadly reflects intellectual attempts to focus more on practical governance, economy, geography, agriculture and social problems, though it was not one unified party with a single doctrine.

# 42과 — 개항, imperial pressure and 대한제국

## 흥선대원군 và opening pressure

19th-century Joseon faced internal problems and growing imperial pressure. `흥선대원군` is associated with domestic reform efforts and restrictive stance toward Western powers in parts of his rule.

`강화도조약` (1876) with Japan is treated as an unequal treaty and major opening marker. `개항` did not mean Korea simply chose modernization freely; opening occurred under power asymmetry.

## 동학농민운동, 갑오개혁, 청일전쟁

To understand late Joseon, open the neighborhood: domestic peasant grievances/reform demand (`동학농민운동`) intersected with Qing-Japanese intervention and `청일전쟁`; `갑오개혁` pursued institutional reforms amid this turbulent context. Internal reform and imperial competition were entangled.

## 대한제국

`대한제국` was proclaimed in 1897 under `고종`, asserting sovereign imperial state and pursuing modernization/reform (`광무개혁`). But Japanese influence intensified after Russo-Japanese War.

`을사늑약` (1905) deprived Korea of diplomatic sovereignty/protectorate status; 1910 annexation began colonial rule. The chain matters more than memorizing 1897–1905–1910 separately.

# 43과 — 일제강점기 và 독립운동

## Colonial rule was political, economic and cultural

`일제강점기` (1910–1945) involved loss of sovereignty and colonial governance. Policy changed across periods, but control affected administration, economy, education, language/name policy and mobilization. Avoid reducing 35 years to one identical policy phase.

## 3·1운동 — 1919

The March First Movement was a nationwide independence movement triggered in broader post-WWI/self-determination context and Korean resistance. `유관순` is a famous participant/symbol, but movement was nationwide and cannot be reduced to one person.

```text
식민지 지배
→ 독립 요구 확산
→ 3·1운동
→ 국내외 독립운동 활성화
→ 대한민국 임시정부 수립
```

`대한민국 임시정부` was established in Shanghai in 1919. `김구` is strongly associated with its later leadership and independence movement.

## Armed struggle and other routes

`봉오동 전투`, `청산리 대첩` are major armed-struggle memories. Independence movement also included diplomacy, education, press/cultural movements, fundraising and overseas networks. Again: movement = network, not one hero.

# 44과 — 광복, 분단, 한국전쟁 and modern Republic

## 광복 did not automatically mean unified state

Japan's defeat in 1945 ended colonial rule, but peninsula entered US/Soviet occupation zones amid emerging Cold War. Political conflict and international structure hardened division.

```text
1945 광복
→ 미·소 군정과 38선
→ 냉전 심화
→ 남북 별도 정부 수립
→ 1950 한국전쟁
→ 1953 정전
→ 분단 고착
```

`광복절` on August 15 therefore carries both liberation memory and connection to Republic government establishment history.

## 대한민국 정부 수립 — 1948

Republic of Korea government was established in 1948 in the southern part of peninsula; North Korea established its own state structure in the north. This must be understood in division/Cold War context, not as a simple linear “liberation → one Korea government”.

## 한국전쟁 — 1950~1953

North Korean invasion in June 1950 began full-scale war; UN forces, Chinese forces and multiple international actors became involved. Armistice in 1953 stopped active large-scale fighting but was not a peace treaty.

This is why `휴전선`, `DMZ`, separated families (`이산가족`) and military/security issues remain living vocabulary, not closed history.

# 산업화, 도시화 và 권위주의

Postwar Korea was poor and infrastructure damaged. Development strategy, export industrialization, education/infrastructure investment and global conditions contributed to rapid growth.

```text
산업화
→ 공장·도시 일자리 증가
→ 농촌 인구 도시 이동
→ 도시화
→ 소득·교육 확대
→ middle class/social structure change
```

But industrialization coexisted with authoritarian politics, labor suppression and social costs. **Economic growth did not automatically create democracy.** This is why Korean modern history is often remembered through two parallel narratives: `산업화` and `민주화`.

# 민주화 — lịch sử quay lại chapter 정치

`4·19 혁명 → 5·18 민주화운동 → 6월 민주항쟁` forms a long democratization arc. In history chapter, focus on temporal context; in politics chapter, focus institutional meaning.

```text
권위주의와 권력집중
→ 시민·학생·노동·사회운동의 저항
→ 반복되는 민주화 요구
→ 1987년 직선제 개헌 체제
```

This deliberate duplication is useful: same event can belong to multiple explanatory lenses.

# 문화유산 — heritage is evidence, not decoration

Instead of memorizing site names, ask what each site tells us:

| Heritage | Neighborhood |
|---|---|
| `고인돌` | prehistoric social organization/burial culture |
| `첨성대` | Silla, astronomy/science memory, Gyeongju |
| `불국사·석굴암` | Unified Silla Buddhist culture, Gyeongju |
| `팔만대장경·해인사` | Goryeo Buddhism, war context, woodblock knowledge preservation |
| `종묘` | Joseon royal ancestral ritual, Confucian state |
| `경복궁` | Joseon capital/palace, Seoul |
| `수원 화성` | Jeongjo, state planning, engineering, Suwon |

Place name should trigger history; history should trigger place.

# Korean public memory — tại sao lịch sử vẫn xuất hiện trong đời sống?

Korean street names, holidays, banknotes, monuments, school curricula và political language đầy historical reference. `세종대로`, `충무로`, `광화문`, `현충일`, `광복절`, `개천절`, `5·18`, `IMF` đều là examples of history compressed into everyday vocabulary.

Vì thế mục tiêu native-like understanding là nghe một từ và bật được network. Ví dụ:

```text
광화문
→ 경복궁 정문
→ 조선 한양
→ 세종대로
→ central civic/public space hiện đại
→ demonstrations, ceremonies, historical symbolism
```
