# 08. 지리 — Địa lý Hàn Quốc (45~50과)

> **Vai trò:** canonical geographic knowledge foundation. Mục tiêu là nghe một place name và bật được **administrative status → vị trí → địa hình/khí hậu → lịch sử → đô thị → 산업 → giao thông → 관광·문화유산 → 음식·특산물 → local identity**.
>
> Geography không phải học dots trên map. Place giải thích vì sao history, economy, food và dialect phát triển khác nhau.

# Trước tiên: đọc bản đồ hành chính đúng cách

Hàn Quốc có local-government units ở metropolitan/provincial level như `특별시`, `광역시`, `특별자치시`, `도`, `특별자치도`. Vì thế không gọi tất cả là “tỉnh”.

Một mental map cơ bản:

```text
서울특별시
광역시: 부산·대구·인천·광주·대전·울산
세종특별자치시
도/특별자치도: 경기, 강원, 충북, 충남, 전북, 전남, 경북, 경남, 제주 등
```

Administrative naming/status có thể được reorganized theo law; học current official names trong current-facts layer khi cần. Quan trọng là phân biệt **city-level metropolitan government** với province.

# 45과 — 자연환경: geography tạo constraint cho đời sống

## 한반도 và vị trí

Korean Peninsula (`한반도`) nằm ở East Asia, nối continental Northeast Asia với seas. Republic of Korea nằm ở southern part of peninsula; division làm land connectivity với continent bị political/security constraint dù geography vẫn là peninsula.

Ba mặt biển thường học:

- phía đông: `동해`;
- phía tây: `서해/황해`;
- phía nam: `남해`.

## 동고서저 — east high, west low

`동고서저` là shorthand cho terrain pattern: eastern side nhiều high mountains, western side generally lower with broader plains.

```text
동쪽 산지
→ 하천이 서·남쪽으로 흐르는 경우 많음
→ 서·남부에 평야 발달
→ 농업·도시 입지와 연결
```

`태백산맥` chạy gần eastern side và là major geographic spine. Vì mountain gần east coast, east-coast rivers thường shorter/steeper; west/south river systems có broader basins/plains.

## Các sông lớn — river là settlement infrastructure

`한강` đi qua Seoul metropolitan area và là central spatial symbol. `낙동강` gắn southeast/Gyeongsang; `금강` với Chungcheong; `영산강` với Honam/Jeolla. Rivers historically cung water, fertile plains, transport routes và settlement axes; hiện đại còn gắn dams, parks và metropolitan development.

## Bờ biển khác nhau tạo economy khác nhau

West/south coasts có nhiều islands, bays và tidal flats (`갯벌`); west coast có large tidal range. East coast relatively straighter and deeper near shore in many areas.

```text
서·남해 복잡한 해안
→ 갯벌·양식·어업·항만 다양성
동해 비교적 단순한 해안
→ 깊은 바다·해안 관광·어업의 다른 패턴
```

## 사계절 và monsoon climate

Spring: temperature swings, flowers, `황사·미세먼지` episodes. Summer: hot/humid, `장마`, heavy rain and typhoon risk. Autumn: generally clearer/drier, foliage. Winter: cold/dry under continental influence, snow especially in certain mountain/east-coast areas depending weather pattern.

Climate explains culture: `온돌` responds to cold winters; kimchi/fermentation and seasonal food preservation belong historical seasonal adaptation; rice agriculture connects warm/wet growing season and plains/water management.

# 46과 — 수도권: 서울·경기·인천 là một functional region

`수도권 = 서울특별시 + 경기도 + 인천광역시`. Nó không phải one administrative unit. Người có thể sống ở Gyeonggi/Incheon và commute vào Seoul, nên job/housing/transport market vượt city border.

```text
서울 중심 기능 집중
→ 주변 주거·산업도시 성장
→ 광역철도·도로 확대
→ 통근권 확대
→ 수도권 집중 심화
```

## 서울특별시 — capital as layered city

Seoul là national capital và major political, business, cultural, education/media center. Han River divides broad north/south urban imagery, nhưng `강북/강남` trong everyday speech còn mang historical-development/social meanings beyond pure compass direction.

### 역사 중심: 한양 → 서울

Joseon made `한양` capital. Vì vậy central Seoul chứa dense historical layer:

- `경복궁`: principal Joseon palace; `광화문` là main gate.
- `창덕궁`: palace nổi bật với adaptation to terrain và `후원`.
- `종묘`: royal ancestral shrine, Confucian ritual/state memory.
- `북촌한옥마을`: traditional urban residential landscape giữa palace areas.
- `숭례문(남대문)` và `흥인지문(동대문)`: historic city gates.

`종로` historically là central commercial/urban axis; `광화문·세종대로` ngày nay còn là civic/administrative/public-assembly space. Đây là lý do demonstrations, national ceremonies và history thường gặp nhau ở central Seoul.

### 한강, 남산 và modern Seoul

`한강` là transport/geographic barrier lẫn amenity; bridges và riverside parks cấu trúc daily mobility/leisure. `남산` và `N서울타워` là central landmark/viewpoint.

`강남` gắn với large-scale south-of-river development, high-value real estate, education/private-academy concentration và business/commercial districts. `여의도` thường gắn finance, National Assembly và broadcasting; `마포·홍대` gợi youth/culture/nightlife; `성수` gợi industrial-space regeneration, cafes/design; associations thay đổi theo thời gian nhưng cho thấy neighborhoods có economic identities.

### 음식 và markets

Seoul là capital migration/consumption hub nên cuisine hấp thụ food traditions từ nhiều regions; không nên ép nó thành one specialty. Native associations thường đi qua places: `광장시장` → 빈대떡·육회 등 market foods; `신당동` → 떡볶이-town image; `장충동` → 족발 association. Đây là place-food cultural association, không phải claim rằng món chỉ tồn tại ở đó.

## 경기도 — vòng đô thị bao quanh capital

Gyeonggi literally surrounds Seoul except western sea/Incheon configurations and contains huge satellite-city/industrial population.

`수원` là provincial capital; `수원 화성` nối Jeongjo, Joseon planning và engineering. `성남·판교` gắn IT/tech/business; `고양` exhibition/media/residential functions; `파주` gắn DMZ/border và publishing/art complexes; `용인` gắn large residential development, theme parks và semiconductor/industrial growth; `이천` nổi tiếng ceramics và semiconductor industry association.

Food/agriculture associations include `이천 쌀`, while northern Gyeonggi geography gần DMZ tạo security/tourism layer khác southern tech corridors.

## 인천광역시 — Korea's international gateway

Incheon combines `인천국제공항`, major seaport/logistics và metropolitan residential/industrial areas. `송도국제도시` represents planned international/business urban development.

Historically, Incheon was an opening-port city; `개항장` area/Chinatown preserve traces of late-19th-century international contact. `인천상륙작전` connects city with Korean War memory.

Food association nổi tiếng: `인천 차이나타운 → 짜장면` origin/popular-history narrative; coastal islands/seafood add maritime identity.

# 47과 — 충청: central position, administration, science and Baekje memory

`충청` comprises Chungcheong provinces plus metropolitan/special cities commonly grouped functionally: `대전`, `세종`, `충북`, `충남`.

## 세종특별자치시

Sejong was developed to redistribute central administrative functions and support balanced development. Many central ministries/agencies are located there, but **Sejong did not replace Seoul as national capital**. This distinction is high-value.

## 대전광역시

Daejeon sits at a central transport junction and is strongly associated with science/research, especially `대덕연구개발특구`. Railway/transport history helped city growth. Native association often: `대전 → 과학·연구·교통 중심`.

## 충청남도: 공주·부여 và west coast

`공주` and `부여` were Baekje capitals, so place instantly connects `백제`, royal tombs/fortresses and cultural festival. `공주 무령왕릉` is a key archaeological site; `부여 부소산성` and Baekje heritage deepen that network.

`태안반도` and west coast connect beaches, tidal flats, fisheries. `금산` is widely associated with `인삼`; `천안` with transport/industry and `호두과자` in popular travel-food memory.

## 충청북도

Chungbuk is the only landlocked province in Korea's current provincial geography, giving it different coastal access. `청주` is major city/administrative center; `직지` association leads to printing heritage. `단양` connects mountain/river scenery and `단양팔경`.

# 48과 — 전라·호남: plains, food culture, 민주화 memory and southwest coast

`호남` broadly refers Jeolla region; today think `광주광역시 + 전북 + 전남` in regional context.

Large western/southwestern plains and warmer agricultural conditions support rice/food production; long coast/islands support fisheries and salt/seafood cultures. This helps explain why “전라도 음식” has strong national reputation: agricultural and marine ingredient diversity plus regional culinary traditions.

## 전주

Jeonju is strongly associated with `전주 한옥마을`, `비빔밥`, traditional culture and Joseon dynastic roots (`전주 이씨`). `한지`, traditional food and hanok tourism form a culture-economy cluster.

## 광주광역시

Gwangju is regional metropolitan center and inseparable from `5·18 민주화운동`. `국립5·18민주묘지` turns political history into physical memory landscape. When Koreans hear “5·18”, Gwangju is not optional context.

Food association includes `광주 떡갈비` traditions in particular districts, while city identity today also includes culture/arts such as `광주비엔날레`.

## 전남

`여수` → port/coast, islands, expo memory and seafood; `순천` → bay/wetland ecology; `보성` → 녹차 fields; `담양` → bamboo landscape and `떡갈비`; `나주` → 곰탕/pear and historical regional center; `영광` → 굴비; `해남` → `땅끝` southern-end tourism image.

`목포` is southwest port city with modern-history/open-port/colonial-era urban layers and gateway toward islands.

## 판소리 and regional culture

Jeolla is strongly associated with `판소리` traditions. Do not reduce pansori to “Jeolla folk song”; it is a major Korean narrative vocal performance tradition with schools/styles and national cultural significance.

# 49과 — 경상·영남: Silla heritage + industrial southeast

`영남` broadly refers Gyeongsang region, including `부산·대구·울산·경북·경남` in common regional framing.

This region contains a striking contrast: ancient Silla/Confucian heritage and Korea's heavy-industrial/export corridor.

## 부산광역시

Busan is Korea's major port city, maritime/logistics center and second-largest-city association in common knowledge. `부산항` links export/import; mountains meet sea, shaping dense urban form.

Tourism/cultural landmarks include `해운대`, `광안리·광안대교`, `자갈치시장`, `감천문화마을`, `태종대`. Food associations: `돼지국밥`, `밀면`, seafood. Korean War history matters too: Busan served as temporary capital/refuge center, helping explain refugee-influenced food/urban culture.

## 대구광역시

Daegu historically associated with textile/manufacturing and inland basin climate; `대구분지` contributes reputation for hot summers. Modern economy is more diversified. Food identity includes local specialties/food alleys rather than one universal dish; `막창` is a common popular association.

## 울산광역시

Ulsan is iconic industrial city: automobiles, shipbuilding, petrochemicals/heavy industry. `현대자동차`, shipyard/industrial complexes make industrialization physically visible. Coastal geography enabled large industrial-port development.

## 경상북도: 경주·안동

`경주` is “museum without walls” style historical city association: `신라 → 불국사·석굴암·첨성대·대릉원`. `안동` connects Confucian/Yangban tradition, `하회마을`, masks (`하회탈`) and `안동찜닭`/`간고등어` food associations.

`포항` connects steel industry (`제철`) and east-coast port; `구미` electronics/manufacturing. Thus Gyeongbuk itself spans heritage and industrial cities.

## 경상남도

`창원` is provincial/industrial center; `거제` strongly associated shipbuilding; `통영` maritime scenery, fisheries and cultural-artistic identity; `진주` connects history, fortress and `진주남강유등축제`.

# 50과 — 강원 và 제주: mountain/east-coast world vs volcanic island world

## 강원

Gangwon's mountainous terrain is dominated by Taebaek range and east-coast landscape. This creates strong tourism, forestry/mountain, winter-sports and coastal-fishery identities, while population density is lower than capital region.

`설악산` is iconic mountain/foliage/hiking destination; `오대산` another major mountain/temple landscape; `대관령` evokes highland/pass, snow, ranch/wind-power landscapes; `강릉` connects east-coast beaches, coffee culture and historical figures/sites; `속초` connects Seoraksan, coast and North-Korean-refugee food history such as `아바이순대`; `춘천` is provincial-capital association with `닭갈비·막국수`.

`평창` → 2018 Winter Olympics, ski/highland tourism. `정동진` → sunrise/coastal railway tourism. Northern Gangwon touches DMZ, so geography immediately connects division/security history.

## 제주특별자치도 — volcano creates an entire cultural system

Jeju is not merely “an island with Hallasan”. It is a volcanic island with distinct geology, ecology, dialect and historical island culture.

`한라산` is Korea's highest mountain and central volcanic massif. `성산일출봉`, lava tubes such as `만장굴` and volcanic formations help explain why Jeju has major natural-heritage value.

Volcanic ground and wind/water conditions historically shaped settlement/agriculture. `돌담` is therefore not just pretty decoration: stone walls respond to abundant basalt and strong winds. `오름` refers to numerous parasitic volcanic cones, a core Jeju landscape word.

`해녀` are women divers harvesting marine products without conventional breathing apparatus; culture includes labor knowledge, community and transmission, not merely a tourist costume.

Food/product associations: `제주 흑돼지`, `감귤`, `갈치`, `전복`, `옥돔` and other seafood/agricultural products. Climate and island logistics help explain agriculture/fisheries specialization.

Jeju also has painful modern history, especially `제주4·3`. A native-level geographic understanding cannot present Jeju only as honeymoon resort; landscape, tourism and historical memory coexist.

# 지역어·사투리 — language is geography carried by people

`표준어` is standardized language used broadly in education/public communication; `사투리/방언` are regional varieties, not “wrong Korean”. Pronunciation, vocabulary, endings and intonation can signal origin/identity.

`경상도 사투리` is often recognized by distinctive intonation/endings; `전라도`, `충청도`, `강원도` also have regional patterns. `제주어` is especially distinctive and can be difficult for mainland speakers; its linguistic/cultural preservation matters.

Stereotypes like “충청 사람 nói chậm” or personality claims about regions are cultural stereotypes, not geographic facts. Native-like literacy includes recognizing stereotype **without treating it as truth about individuals**.

# 음식·특산물 — học bằng mechanism, không bằng shopping list

Regional food associations thường emerge from:

```text
기후 + 지형 + 생산물
+ 보존 방식
+ 역사적 이동·교류
+ 도시 시장
→ 지역 음식문화
```

Examples:

- `보성 녹차`: climate/topography + tea cultivation.
- `금산 인삼`: regional cultivation/trade identity.
- `영광 굴비`: fish preservation/trade tradition.
- `제주 감귤`: warmer island climate.
- `부산 돼지국밥·밀면`: port/refugee/urban history associations.
- `춘천 닭갈비`: city food identity developed through restaurant/market culture.

A dish may be sold nationwide; `향토음식` means strong origin/identity association, not exclusive geographic monopoly.

# Khi nghe một địa danh, hãy tự chạy 8 câu hỏi

1. `어느 행정구역인가?` — nó là 특별시, 광역시, 도 hay city trong province?
2. `어디에 있는가?` — north/south/east/west, coast/inland/island?
3. `지형·기후는?`
4. `어떤 역사가 쌓였는가?`
5. `무슨 산업·일자리가 발달했는가?`
6. `무슨 관광지·문화유산이 있는가?`
7. `무슨 음식·특산물이 연상되는가?`
8. `한국인이 그 이름을 들으면 어떤 이미지·사건을 함께 떠올리는가?`
