# 17 — FX intervention, reserves, REER và currency valuation

Một currency có thể rẻ/đắt theo một valuation khung phần mềm (framework / 프레임워크) nhưng vẫn đi ngược view trong thời gian dài. Central bank có thể can thiệp nhưng không phải lúc nào cũng đảo trend. Reserve stock có thể lớn nhưng không đồng nghĩa usable liquidity vô hạn. Chapter này nối **valuation → bên ngoài (external / 외부) balance → intervention → reserves → regime** để tránh dùng một chỉ số đơn lẻ như trading tín hiệu (signal / 신호).

Mô hình tư duy (mental model / 사고 모델):

```text
Relative prices / productivity / inflation
+ external balance
+ capital flows
+ policy regime
+ reserves / intervention
+ risk premium
→ medium-run currency pressure
```

## 1. Valuation khác timing

Currency valuation hỏi gần:

```text
Is the exchange rate expensive or cheap relative to a model/reference?
```

Trading timing hỏi:

```text
When and why will price converge, if at all?
```

Hai câu hỏi khác nhau.

Một currency có thể undervalued nhiều năm nếu productivity, capital controls, rủi ro (risk / 위험) premium hoặc chính sách (policy / 정책) regime giữ equilibrium khác textbook anchor.

## 2. Nominal exchange tỷ lệ (rate / 비율)

Nominal exchange tỷ lệ (rate / 비율) là price của one currency in terms of another.

Ví dụ:

```text
USD/KRW
EUR/USD
USD/VND
```

Nominal move không tự nói competitiveness nếu domestic/foreign price levels thay đổi khác nhau.

## 3. Real exchange tỷ lệ (rate / 비율)

Simplified bilateral real exchange tỷ lệ (rate / 비율) concept:

```text
Real FX ≈ Nominal FX × Foreign Price Level / Domestic Price Level
```

Chính xác (exact / 정확한) interpretation phụ thuộc quote convention.

Cốt lõi (core / 핵심) idea: real exchange tỷ lệ (rate / 비율) adjusts nominal FX for relative prices.

## 4. Real Effective Exchange Tỷ lệ (rate / 비율) — REER

**REER** là trade-weighted real exchange-rate chỉ mục (index / 인덱스) against a basket of trading partners.

Nó kết hợp:

```text
bilateral exchange rates
× trade weights
× relative price/cost indices
```

REER useful for broad competitiveness/valuation discussion nhưng không phải executable price.

## 5. NEER vs REER

**Nominal Effective Exchange Tỷ lệ (rate / 비율) (NEER)** aggregates nominal bilateral rates.

**REER** adds relative inflation/chi phí (cost / 비용) adjustment.

If nominal currency appreciates but domestic inflation is lower than partners, real appreciation may be smaller than nominal move suggests.

## 6. Chỉ mục (index / 인덱스) mức (level / 수준) không có universal fair giá trị (value / 값)

A REER chỉ mục (index / 인덱스) often normalized to cơ sở (base / 기반) period such as 100.

`REER = 110` không tự nghĩa “10% overvalued”. Need know:

- cơ sở (base / 기반) period;
- methodology;
- weights;
- price chỉ mục (index / 인덱스);
- structural thay đổi (change / 변경).

Fair-value suy luận (inference / 추론) requires mô hình (model / 모델)/lịch sử (history / 이력), not raw chỉ mục (index / 인덱스) arithmetic.

## 7. Purchasing Power Parity — PPP

PPP intuition says same tradable basket should not sustain arbitrarily different currency-adjusted prices after frictions.

Absolute PPP is too strong for many real settings because:

- baskets differ;
- taxes/vận chuyển (transport / 전송) differ;
- services/nontradables matter;
- chất lượng (quality / 품질) differs;
- trade barriers exist.

Relative PPP focuses on inflation differentials and long-run exchange-rate changes.

## 8. PPP works better as anchor than timer

Large deviations can persist.

Reasons include:

- productivity differences;
- capital flows;
- rủi ro (risk / 위험) premium;
- terms of trade;
- nontradable prices;
- chính sách (policy / 정책) regime.

So:

```text
PPP cheap
≠ buy now
```

## 9. Balassa–Samuelson intuition

Fast productivity growth in tradable sectors can raise wages economy-wide. Nontradable prices may rise, producing real appreciation without mất mát (loss / 손실) of tradable competitiveness in the simple sense.

Thus richer/faster-productivity economies need not converge to identical absolute price levels.

## 10. Terms of trade and equilibrium FX

Commodity exporter receiving better export prices can experience stronger real income and potentially currency appreciation pressure.

Commodity importer hit by năng lượng (energy / 에너지) shock may face opposite pressure.

But luồng (flow / 흐름) phản hồi (response / 응답) depends on hedging, fiscal saving, reserve chính sách (policy / 정책) and capital flows.

## 11. Hiện tại (current / 현재) account as tín hiệu (signal / 신호), not verdict

Current-account surplus may hỗ trợ (support / 지원) bên ngoài (external / 외부) resilience but does not guarantee currency appreciation.

Residents can recycle surplus abroad; pension funds can buy foreign assets; central bank can accumulate reserves.

Accounting định danh (identity / 식별자) constrains flows but does not determine spot direction mechanically.

## 12. Net International Investment Position — NIIP

NIIP compares bên ngoài (external / 외부) financial assets and liabilities.

A debtor country with large foreign-currency liabilities may be more vulnerable to currency depreciation through balance-sheet effects.

But composition matters:

- FDI equity vs short-term debt;
- local-currency vs foreign-currency debt;
- maturity;
- hedging.

## 13. Gross positions matter too

Two countries with same net position can have very different gross cross-border assets/liabilities.

Large gross books create:

- hedging flows;
- valuation effects;
- funding rủi ro (risk / 위험);
- rebalancing.

Net dữ liệu (data / 데이터) can hide these channels.

## 14. Reserve assets

Foreign-exchange reserves are bên ngoài (external / 외부) assets controlled by monetary authorities, usually including liquid foreign-currency assets and related reserve components according to official definitions.

They hỗ trợ (support / 지원) functions such as:

- FX liquidity;
- intervention sức chứa (capacity / 용량);
- confidence;
- external-payment resilience.

## 15. Reserve kích thước (size / 크기) needs denominator

Absolute reserves alone are hard to interpret.

Dùng chung (common / 공통) lenses include reserves relative to:

- imports;
- short-term bên ngoài (external / 외부) debt;
- broad money;
- bên ngoài (external / 외부) financing needs.

No single ratio is universal.

## 16. Usable reserves may differ from headline reserves

Potential các ràng buộc (constraints / 제약조건들):

- pledged assets;
- illiquid components;
- forward commitments;
- derivative positions;
- sovereign/central-bank institutional arrangements.

Research should distinguish headline reserve stock from effective intervention liquidity when dữ liệu (data / 데이터) permit.

## 17. Reserve adequacy is regime-dependent

A freely floating advanced economy with reserve-currency truy cập (access / 접근) has different needs from an EM defending a managed tỷ lệ (rate / 비율) under bên ngoài (external / 외부) debt pressure.

Do not apply one “months of imports” threshold universally.

## 18. FX intervention — definition

FX intervention is official hành động (action / 동작) intended to affect foreign-exchange conditions, commonly through transactions in FX markets or related instruments.

Intervention can be:

- spot;
- forward/swap;
- verbal/signaling;
- combined with liquidity/capital-flow measures.

Chính xác (exact / 정확한) institutional definitions vary.

## 19. Buying vs selling reserves

Simplified:

```text
Central bank buys foreign currency / sells domestic currency
→ reserve accumulation
→ tends to resist domestic-currency appreciation
```

```text
Central bank sells foreign currency / buys domestic currency
→ reserve use
→ tends to resist domestic-currency depreciation
```

But thị trường (market / 시장) impact depends kích thước (size / 크기), credibility, sterilization and macro regime.

## 20. Sterilized intervention

If central bank offsets domestic-liquidity tác động (effect / 효과) of FX intervention, thao tác (operation / 연산) is sterilized.

Example intuition:

```text
sell domestic currency for FX
would expand domestic liquidity
→ separate domestic operation absorbs liquidity
```

Then intervention attempts to influence FX without equivalent monetary-base thay đổi (change / 변경).

## 21. Unsterilized intervention

If liquidity tác động (effect / 효과) is not offset, intervention overlaps monetary-policy stance more directly.

Therefore empirical tác động (effect / 효과) cannot be attributed only to “FX luồng (flow / 흐름)” if rates/liquidity also thay đổi (change / 변경).

## 22. Why intervention can công việc (work / 작업)

Possible channels:

- portfolio-balance tác động (effect / 효과);
- signaling future chính sách (policy / 정책);
- market-liquidity provision;
- coordination/expectation tác động (effect / 효과);
- stop-loss/positioning dynamics.

Effectiveness is state-dependent and empirically contested across settings.

## 23. Signaling channel

Intervention can reveal chính sách (policy / 정책) preference/thông tin (information / 정보).

Thị trường (market / 시장) may infer:

```text
authority is uncomfortable with current speed/level
or
broader policy response may follow
```

But repeated ineffective intervention can weaken tín hiệu (signal / 신호) credibility.

## 24. Portfolio-balance channel

If domestic/foreign assets are imperfect substitutes, official changes in relative asset supply can affect required rủi ro (risk / 위험) premium and exchange rates.

Magnitude depends thị trường (market / 시장) độ sâu (depth / 깊이) and substitutability.

## 25. Liquidity channel

During disorderly markets, authority may provide FX liquidity rather than defend an chính xác (exact / 정확한) price mục tiêu (target / 대상).

This distinction matters:

```text
restore market functioning
≠ promise specific exchange rate
```

## 26. Intervention against fundamentals

Defending a mức (level / 수준) while monetary/fiscal/bên ngoài (external / 외부) fundamentals điểm (point / 지점) strongly opposite may consume reserves without durable tác động (effect / 효과).

Sustainability depends broader chính sách (policy / 정책) gói (package / 패키지).

## 27. One-way bet rủi ro (risk / 위험)

If thị trường (market / 시장) believes a peg/band is unsustainable, intervention can invite speculative pressure because payoff becomes asymmetric around known ranh giới (boundary / 경계).

Need analyze reserve adequacy, chính sách (policy / 정책) consistency and capital mobility together.

## 28. Impossible trinity intuition

Under simplified macro khung phần mềm (framework / 프레임워크), it is difficult to simultaneously maintain all three fully:

```text
fixed exchange rate
free capital mobility
independent monetary policy
```

Countries choose different combinations/degrees.

This helps explain why FX regime changes monetary-policy flexibility and intervention need.

## 29. Peg, band, managed float, free float

Regimes lie on spectrum.

Labels alone are insufficient; actual hành vi (behavior / 동작) can differ from official label.

Research should observe:

- realized volatility;
- intervention;
- chính sách (policy / 정책) statements;
- reserve changes;
- band rules;
- capital controls.

## 30. De facto vs de jure regime

**De jure** = officially announced khung phần mềm (framework / 프레임워크).

**De facto** = hành vi (behavior / 동작) inferred from actual chính sách (policy / 정책)/thị trường (market / 시장) outcomes.

A “float” can be heavily managed; a “band” can move frequently.

## 31. Central-bank balance sheet tương tác (interaction / 상호작용)

Reserve accumulation changes asset side of central-bank balance sheet and may create domestic liquidity unless sterilized.

Persistent intervention can therefore create:

- sterilization chi phí (cost / 비용);
- interest-rate tương tác (interaction / 상호작용);
- balance-sheet valuation changes.

## 32. Intervention dữ liệu (data / 데이터) can be noisy

Reserve thay đổi (change / 변경) is not identical to intervention because reserves also thay đổi (change / 변경) due to:

- asset valuation;
- interest income;
- government transactions;
- portfolio management.

Need direct intervention dữ liệu (data / 데이터) when available or carefully decompose proxies.

## 33. Valuation effects in reserves

If reserves contain EUR/JPY assets but reporting currency is USD, exchange-rate changes move headline reserve giá trị (value / 값) even without giao dịch (transaction / 트랜잭션).

A drop in reported reserves may partly be valuation, not selling.

## 34. Forward intervention can hide in spot reserve dữ liệu (data / 데이터)

Authorities may use forwards/swaps; commitments can affect future reserve/funding exposure without immediate spot reserve thay đổi (change / 변경).

Research should inspect forward positions where published.

## 35. Capital controls and macroprudential measures

Authorities can influence FX pressure through rules on:

- borrowing;
- derivative positions;
- reserve requirements;
- remittances;
- foreign portfolio truy cập (access / 접근).

These are not the same as spot intervention but can alter currency demand/supply and arbitrage channels.

## 36. FX valuation các mô hình (models / 모델들)

Dùng chung (common / 공통) mô hình (model / 모델) families include:

```text
PPP / REER mean reversion
BEER-style macro fundamentals
FEER-style external-balance equilibrium
terms-of-trade models
rate/yield-differential models
portfolio / risk-premium models
```

Each answers different question and relies on các giả định (assumptions / 가정들).

## 37. BEER intuition

Behavioral Equilibrium Exchange Tỷ lệ (rate / 비율) approaches relate real exchange tỷ lệ (rate / 비율) to macro fundamentals empirically, such as:

- productivity;
- terms of trade;
- net foreign assets;
- tỷ lệ (rate / 비율) differentials.

Estimated “misalignment” is model-dependent, not observable truth.

## 38. FEER intuition

Fundamental Equilibrium Exchange Tỷ lệ (rate / 비율) approaches ask which real exchange tỷ lệ (rate / 비율) is consistent with nội bộ (internal / 내부)/bên ngoài (external / 외부) balance under normative/mô hình (model / 모델) các giả định (assumptions / 가정들).

Because desired hiện tại (current / 현재) account/đầu ra (output / 출력) gap các giả định (assumptions / 가정들) matter, FEER is not a pure dữ liệu (data / 데이터) fact.

## 39. Mô hình (model / 모델) bất định (uncertainty / 불확실성) dominates precise fair-value numbers

Two reasonable valuation các mô hình (models / 모델들) can disagree materially.

Therefore better đầu ra (output / 출력) is often:

```text
valuation range
+ model assumptions
+ historical percentile
+ catalysts
+ invalidation
```

not “fair giá trị (value / 값) = 1.0837”.

## 40. Valuation z-score pitfalls

Historical z-score assumes phân phối (distribution / 분포)/stationarity meaningful.

Structural changes in:

- inflation regime;
- productivity;
- chính sách (policy / 정책) khung phần mềm (framework / 프레임워크);
- capital mobility;

can invalidate old mean.

## 41. Cheap currencies can get cheaper

Valuation has weak short-horizon timing power in many settings.

A cheap currency under crisis can continue falling because:

- deleveraging;
- reserve mất mát (loss / 손실);
- bên ngoài (external / 외부) debt;
- political/institutional rủi ro (risk / 위험);
- trend/momentum.

Rủi ro (risk / 위험) management cannot be delegated to “giá trị (value / 값) eventually works”.

## 42. Intervention sự kiện (event / 이벤트) study

A research thiết kế (design / 설계) should store:

```text
announcement / transaction timestamp
pre-event trend
size/type if known
spot + forward + rates response
subsequent policy actions
market regime
```

Concurrent macro news is a major confounder.

## 43. Reserve-loss stress map

Useful stress questions:

```text
How fast are reserves changing?
How much short-term FX debt matures?
What is current-account/funding gap?
Which liabilities are FX-denominated?
Are controls tightening?
Is monetary policy consistent with FX objective?
```

Avoid one-ratio crisis rules.

## 44. REER decomposition

If REER appreciates, ask whether driven by:

```text
nominal FX appreciation
or
domestic inflation > partner inflation
or
weight changes
```

Same REER move can imply different cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘).

## 45. Corporate competitiveness

REER is macro aggregate. Firm competitiveness depends:

- invoice currency;
- imported inputs;
- pricing power;
- môi trường vận hành (production / 운영 환경) location;
- hedging;
- competitor currency.

Do not map country REER one-to-one into a company's earnings.

## 46. Asset-investor FX valuation

Foreign investor cares not only currency valuation but total hedged/unhedged asset return:

```text
asset local return
+ FX
+ hedge carry/basis
+ tax/cost
```

A “cheap currency” does not guarantee attractive foreign-asset return.

## 49. Mental checklist

Before using reserves/REER/intervention in FX thesis, ask:

1. What regime is actually operating?
2. Is intervention spot, forward, liquidity or signaling?
3. Are reserve changes transactional or valuation-driven?
4. What denominator defines adequacy?
5. Are capital controls/arbitrage các ràng buộc (constraints / 제약조건들) material?
6. Is REER methodology comparable through thời gian (time / 시간)?
7. Which valuation mô hình (model / 모델) defines “cheap/expensive”?
8. What catalyst closes misvaluation?
9. Could funding/momentum overwhelm valuation?
10. What bằng chứng (evidence / 증거) would falsify the thesis?

## 50. Kết luận

FX valuation là **slow anchor**, không phải automatic entry trigger. Intervention là **chính sách (policy / 정책) instrument**, không phải guaranteed trend reversal. Reserves là **sức chứa (capacity / 용량) indicator**, không phải infinite firepower.

Một robust currency thesis phải nối valuation với bên ngoài (external / 외부) balance, funding, chính sách (policy / 정책) regime, positioning và executable instrument — sau đó vẫn phải trạng thái (state / 상태) bất định (uncertainty / 불확실성) và vô hiệu hóa (invalidation / 무효화).
