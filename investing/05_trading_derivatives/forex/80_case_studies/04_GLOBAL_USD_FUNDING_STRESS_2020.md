## 5. Cross-currency basis as funding stress tín hiệu (signal / 신호)

In stress, cross-currency basis can widen because borrowing USD synthetically through FX swaps becomes expensive.

Conceptually:

```text
Need USD
→ borrow local currency
→ swap into USD
→ pay extra premium
```

If many institutions compete for USD, basis can move sharply.

## 10. Central-bank swap lines

Federal Reserve has standing dollar liquidity swap lines with several major central banks.

Cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘) simplified:

```text
Foreign central bank provides its currency to Fed
Fed provides USD to foreign central bank
Foreign central bank lends USD domestically
At maturity, currencies are swapped back at same exchange rate
```

This allows foreign central bank to act as cục bộ (local / 로컬) distributor of dollar liquidity.

> **Nối mạch:** Trong **Trường hợp (case / 사례) 04 — Toàn cục (global / 전역) USD Funding Stress 2020: khi dollar shortage xuất hiện ngoài nước Mỹ**, **11. ## 11. Why swap lines reduce FX funding pressure

Without swap line:

```text
Local bank needs USD
→ compete in private dollar funding / FX swap market
→ funding premium rises
```

With swap line:

```text
Central bank can supply USD against local collateral framework
→ private scramble for dollars can ease
```

Swap lines therefore mục tiêu (target / 대상) funding thị trường (market / 시장), not exchange-rate mức (level / 수준) directly.

## 17. FIMA Repo Facility

Fed also created temporary FIMA repo facility allowing foreign official institutions to obtain dollars against US Treasury securities.

Why?

Without facility, reserve managers needing USD might sell Treasuries outright.

```text
Need USD
→ sell Treasuries
→ Treasury market pressure
```

With repo:

```text
Pledge Treasuries temporarily
→ obtain USD
→ avoid outright sale
```

This connects FX reserve management with Treasury thị trường (market / 시장) functioning.

## 25. Why swap line is not FX intervention

FX intervention typically aims to influence exchange-rate conditions by buying/selling currency in thị trường (market / 시장).

Dollar swap line instead aims to provide **funding liquidity**.

It may affect FX indirectly, but instrument mục tiêu (objective / 목표) differs.

Do not confuse:

```text
FX intervention
with
central-bank liquidity swap
```

## 37. What not to learn

Wrong lesson:

```text
"Fed swap lines always weaken USD."
```

No deterministic quy tắc (rule / 규칙).

Better lesson:

```text
Swap lines address dollar funding scarcity;
FX impact depends on broader state and expectations.
```

Wrong lesson:

```text
"USD strength means US fundamentals are strong."
```

Funding stress can strengthen USD during weak toàn cục (global / 전역)/US conditions.
