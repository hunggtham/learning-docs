# Korea Market Playbook

> File này là framework evergreen để phân tích Korean equities và Korean-listed products. Policy rate, market rules, tax và product structure có thể thay đổi, vì vậy khi ra quyết định thực tế cần kiểm tra KRX, BOK, DART/company disclosures và nguồn chính thức mới nhất.

## 1. Không tồn tại một “Korean market” đồng nhất

KOSPI, KOSDAQ, KOSPI 200 và KONEX phục vụ những company profiles khác nhau. KOSPI chứa nhiều large caps, exporters, financials và industrial groups; KOSDAQ có tỷ trọng growth, biotech, software, components và smaller companies cao hơn; KOSPI 200 là benchmark quan trọng cho index futures/options và ETFs.

Một large memory semiconductor exporter chịu global capex, USD/KRW và memory prices. Một domestic biotech KOSDAQ có thể phụ thuộc clinical milestones và financing. Một bank phụ thuộc NIM, credit cycle và property exposure. Vì vậy index-level macro view chỉ là layer đầu.

## 2. Index concentration và benchmark effect

Korean indices có thể bị ảnh hưởng mạnh bởi vài mega caps hoặc sectors. KOSPI tăng không có nghĩa median stock tăng. Khi large semiconductor names rally mạnh, index có thể đẹp trong khi breadth yếu.

Theo dõi breadth, equal-weight behavior, sector contribution và turnover distribution để biết move có rộng không. Điều này đặc biệt quan trọng khi dùng index return để infer domestic economy.

## 3. Participants: foreign, institution và retail

Korean market thường theo dõi flows của foreign investors, domestic institutions và individuals. Nhưng raw net-buy/sell data không tự nói motive.

Foreign flow có thể phản ánh global beta, USD/KRW, index rebalancing, semiconductor view hoặc regional allocation. Domestic institutions có pension/insurance/fund constraints. Retail flow có thể concentrated in themes/leverage.

Một ngày net foreign selling không đủ cho thesis. Hãy hỏi flow có broad across market, concentrated in index heavyweights, derivatives-linked hay company-specific.

## 4. Korea là export-oriented economy

Exports truyền global demand vào corporate earnings nhanh. Semiconductors, autos, machinery, ships, batteries/electronics và chemical products đều có global sensitivity.

Monthly trade data vì vậy là useful high-frequency macro signal. Nhưng total exports cần decomposition: semiconductor exports tăng mạnh trong khi other categories weak tạo different market implications so broad export recovery.

Export growth cũng cần volume-price distinction. Higher commodity/input prices can inflate nominal exports without equivalent real demand improvement.

## 5. Trade balance và energy imports

Korea imports significant energy/raw materials. Oil/LNG price shock có thể worsen trade balance, raise import prices và pressure KRW/inflation.

Một simultaneous global demand slowdown + high oil shock là particularly difficult: exporters lose volume while import bill remains high. Ngược lại semiconductor upcycle + lower energy prices creates favorable terms-of-trade setup.

## 6. Semiconductor: core cyclical engine

Memory/HBM ecosystem cần theo ASP, bit shipments, inventory, utilization, capex, technology mix, customer concentration và supply discipline.

DRAM/NAND traditional cycle có commodity-like dynamics; HBM has different bottlenecks, qualification and packaging economics. “AI boom” không automatically benefit every chip company equally.

Stock price often turns before reported earnings. Bottom may occur when inventory correction is nearing end and pricing expectations improve, even while trailing profit remains weak. Peak can occur while news is excellent if expectations/capex supply already extreme.

## 7. Semiconductor equipment và materials

Equipment/material suppliers add second-order exposure. Drivers include fab capex, process-node transitions, localization, customer concentration and qualification cycles.

Revenue can be lumpier than memory producer earnings. Backlog quality, order timing and working capital matter. A supplier with 70% one-customer exposure should not be valued like diversified global equipment leader.

## 8. Autos

Korean autos are global businesses. Key drivers include unit volume, regional mix, pricing, incentives, FX, raw materials, warranty costs and EV/hybrid product cycle.

Weak KRW can support translated overseas earnings but imported components/input costs and hedging reduce simple sensitivity. US/EU tariffs or localization rules can change production footprint economics.

## 9. Batteries

Battery ecosystem includes cell makers, cathode/anode/materials and equipment. Demand depends EV adoption, OEM inventory, subsidy/policy and capacity buildout.

High long-term TAM does not guarantee attractive shareholder return if industry overbuilds capacity, prices fall and capex consumes cash. Track utilization, ASP, raw-material pass-through, customer contracts and balance-sheet funding.

## 10. Shipbuilding

Shipbuilders are backlog businesses. Orderbook, order value, ship type, steel/input costs, labor productivity and FX drive future margin.

Current revenue reflects orders booked earlier. New orders today affect earnings years later. High backlog can improve visibility but contract quality/margins matter more than headline order value.

## 11. Banks

Korean banks require NIM, loan growth, credit cost, delinquency/NPL, capital ratios, household/property exposure and shareholder-return policy.

Rate hikes can initially support margins but later raise borrower stress and funding costs. Housing and household debt are central macro-financial channels.

P/B should be read with sustainable ROE, cost of equity, asset quality and capital allocation. Low P/B alone is not bargain signal.

## 12. Insurance

Insurers combine underwriting/liability management with investment portfolios. Interest rates affect asset yields and liability valuation. Product mix, loss ratios, capital adequacy and accounting changes matter.

Do not value insurers solely on reported earnings without understanding reserve assumptions and capital framework.

## 13. Platforms, internet và gaming

Platforms depend user engagement, advertising/commerce monetization, take rate, competition and regulation. Gaming depends title pipeline, lifecycle, user acquisition and overseas exposure.

High margins can coexist with high reinvestment/R&D. For new titles, bookings/revenue recognition timing and marketing spend can create quarter volatility.

## 14. Biotech

Biotech needs separate framework: pipeline stage, mechanism, trial design, probability of technical/regulatory success, cash runway, licensing economics and dilution.

P/E often meaningless before commercialization. Scenario probability and funding risk matter more.

## 15. Chaebol structure và governance

Korean corporate groups can have cross-holdings, controlling-family influence and complex listed affiliates. Investor should map ownership structure, related-party transactions and parent-subsidiary economics.

Holding-company discount or low P/B can persist when minority-shareholder treatment, capital allocation or governance concerns remain. Governance improvement can be catalyst, but thesis must specify mechanism rather than assume discount “must close”.

## 16. Capital allocation và shareholder return

Look at dividends, buybacks, cancellation of treasury shares, capex and M&A together. A company can announce buyback yet maintain share count if SBC/other issuance offsets it.

Per-share value growth, not headline payout alone, is goal. Compare incremental ROIC with cost of capital and alternative use of cash.

## 17. USD/KRW framework

USD/KRW should be analyzed as relative price. Key drivers:

`Fed vs BOK path + US/Korea yield differentials + exports/trade balance + China/global cycle + oil/import bill + foreign flows + risk sentiment`

No single factor dominates permanently. During global risk-off, USD can strengthen despite Korean export improvement. During strong semiconductor cycle, KRW can still weaken if US yields/USD surge.

## 18. Company-level FX sensitivity

KRW weakness is not simply “good for exporters”. Revenue currency, cost currency, production geography and hedging determine impact.

Company earning USD overseas but paying significant USD raw-material costs may have smaller net benefit. Translation benefit can also differ from transaction cash benefit.

Build a currency map: revenue by currency, costs by currency, net monetary assets/liabilities and hedge policy.

## 19. BOK reaction function

Bank of Korea balances inflation, growth and financial stability. Korea's household leverage, housing market and KRW sensitivity can complicate simple Taylor-rule intuition.

When reading BOK communication, track inflation forecast, growth forecast, housing prices, household credit, FX language and vote/dissent. Ask what variables would cause next move rather than label meeting only hawkish/dovish.

## 20. Monetary transmission in Korea

Base-rate changes transmit into money-market rates, bank funding, deposit/loan rates, mortgages, property, household consumption and corporate funding. Transmission speed differs by loan structure and refinancing cycle.

Even after hikes stop, borrowers refinancing at higher rates may continue to feel tightening. Thus policy “hold” does not mean credit conditions neutral.

## 21. Korea yield curve và credit conditions

Track short Korean yields for BOK expectations and longer yields for growth/inflation/supply. Corporate spreads add financing stress.

Banks/industrials with refinancing needs can be affected before aggregate GDP slows. Corporate bond issuance conditions are useful complement to policy-rate headlines.

## 22. Foreign flows and derivatives

KOSPI futures/options activity can interact with cash market via hedging/arbitrage. Expiry and index rebalance dates can create technical flow.

Distinguish structural foreign allocation from derivatives hedge. If cash foreigners sell while futures positioning changes opposite direction, simple net-flow headline may mislead.

## 23. Korean ETFs: wrapper trước, theme sau

KODEX, TIGER, ACE, SOL and other names are brands/wrappers. Evaluate underlying index, weighting, rebalancing, fee, tracking difference, AUM, spread and holdings.

Two “AI semiconductor” ETFs can have very different concentration and factor exposures. Theme label is marketing layer, not economic exposure definition.

## 24. Foreign-asset ETFs listed in Korea

KRW trading does not mean KRW-only exposure. Unhedged US equity ETF listed in Korea still embeds USD/underlying currency exposure.

KRW return roughly combines underlying local return and FX interaction. Hedged share class reduces currency sensitivity but incurs hedge/carry/tracking costs.

Always identify three currencies when relevant: trading currency, underlying economic currency and your liability/base currency.

## 25. Leveraged/inverse ETFs và ETNs

Leveraged/inverse funds commonly target daily multiples, so compounding/path dependency can make multi-day return diverge from simple multiplier.

ETN adds issuer credit risk because it is note/debt claim rather than fund ownership of basket. Read issuer and redemption terms.

## 26. KRX Gold và gold wrappers

KRX Gold, gold ETF/ETN and overseas gold products have different custody, tax/account, tracking and FX structures. World gold price usually in USD, so KRW investor outcome depends product design and currency exposure.

Do not compare only ticker returns without decomposing spot gold, USD/KRW and fees.

## 27. Domestic và foreign bond ETFs

Korean government/corporate bond ETFs depend BOK/yield curve/credit. US Treasury ETF listed Korea adds US duration and potentially USD/KRW exposure.

A “bond ETF” label hides large variation. 3-month Treasury exposure and 30-year Treasury exposure are fundamentally different duration bets.

## 28. Source hierarchy

For company fundamentals, start with official filings/company IR rather than portal summaries. DART is key disclosure system; KRX provides market/product information; BOK covers monetary/macro; official trade/statistical agencies cover exports and activity.

Broker research/news are useful synthesis, not replacement for primary numbers. Maintain distinction between source fact and analyst interpretation.

## 29. Earnings-season workflow

Before earnings, update consensus/key drivers: volume, ASP, FX, margins, orders/backlog, credit cost or sector-specific KPI. Define what market seems to expect, not only your forecast.

After report, separate reported beat/miss from forward drivers. Check guidance, revisions to capex, inventory, orderbook and shareholder-return plans. Then update normalized earnings and valuation.

## 30. Cyclical valuation

Low P/E at peak earnings can be value trap; high P/E at trough can precede recovery. For semis, chemicals, shipping or steel, use mid-cycle earnings, balance sheet and replacement/supply cycle, not trailing multiple alone.

P/B can be more useful in banks/asset-heavy names when paired with sustainable ROE. EV/EBITDA may be useful in industrials but needs normalized EBITDA/capex awareness.

## 31. Expectations matter more than “good company” label

A great company can underperform if valuation implies impossible growth. A cyclical company can rally while current earnings bad if market anticipates recovery.

Build thesis as gap between your forward distribution and market-implied expectations. “Company tốt” is description, not investment edge.

## 32. Daily dashboard

Daily monitoring should be minimal: KOSPI/KOSDAQ performance and breadth, USD/KRW, foreign cash/futures flow, US 2Y/10Y, major semiconductor/global tech moves, China risk indicators and oil.

Do not change long-term thesis from every daily move. Dashboard exists to flag unusual transmission.

## 33. Weekly dashboard

Weekly review can add Korea yields/credit, sector relative performance, export high-frequency signals when available, inventory/price data for semiconductors, China PMI/property/credit context and earnings revisions.

Write what changed and which thesis assumption it affects.

## 34. Monthly dashboard

Monthly: exports by category, semiconductor exports, trade balance, CPI/labor/activity, BOK stance, household credit/housing, company guidance/capex and valuation percentiles.

This cadence is better for macro thesis than reacting to every headline.

## 35. Fed-hawkish scenario

If Fed path reprices higher: US front-end yields and USD often strengthen, pressure KRW and long-duration growth multiples. But Korean exporters may receive translation support.

Assess net result through FX sensitivity, global demand effect and foreign-flow response. Do not label whole KOSPI bearish automatically.

## 36. China stimulus scenario

China stimulus matters by destination. Property/infrastructure stimulus may support steel/chemicals/machinery differently from household-consumption stimulus. Manufacturing subsidies could even increase competitive supply in sectors Korea exports.

Ask “stimulus to whom and through which spending channel?” rather than buy all China-sensitive stocks.

## 37. Oil shock scenario

Supply-driven oil spike worsens Korea import bill and inflation, potentially pressures KRW/household real income. Refiners, petrochemicals, airlines, utilities and exporters have different sensitivities.

Refinery profit depends crack spreads/inventory timing, not crude price alone. Airline fuel cost and FX can both worsen.

## 38. AI/semiconductor upcycle scenario

AI capex can support HBM, memory, equipment, packaging, power/cooling and related exporters. But supply expansion can eventually compress margins.

Track order visibility, capacity addition and customer concentration to distinguish early-cycle earnings acceleration from late-cycle extrapolation.

## 39. Global recession scenario

Exports, cyclicals and KRW may weaken; BOK could gain easing room if inflation allows, while long-duration bonds may benefit. Banks face credit-cost risk; defensives may outperform.

Severity depends financial stress and policy response. Recession without credit crisis differs from balance-sheet recession.

## 40. Thesis template cho Korean stock

For each stock write: business model; sector cycle; three operating KPIs; revenue/cost currency exposure; balance-sheet/refinancing risks; governance/capital allocation; normalized earnings; valuation; bull/base/bear scenarios; catalysts; invalidation conditions; liquidity/position-size limits.

Then explicitly note which macro variables matter: Fed/BOK, USD/KRW, China, oil, semiconductors or domestic housing/credit.

## 41. Mental model cuối cùng

Analyze Korea through:

`Global cycle → exports/trade → KRW/BOK/credit → sector economics → company earnings → governance/capital allocation → expectations/valuation → flows/liquidity`

The framework prevents two common mistakes: treating Korea only as a semiconductor proxy, or treating every Korean stock as a macro trade.
