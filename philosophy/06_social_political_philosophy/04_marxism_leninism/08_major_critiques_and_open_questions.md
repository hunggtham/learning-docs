# Major critiques — kiểm tra từng lớp của Marxian và Marxist–Leninist lý thuyết (theory / 이론)

> **Mạch đọc:** [README](./README.md) là bản đồ owner của **Major critiques — kiểm tra từng lớp của Marxian và Marxist–Leninist lý thuyết (theory / 이론)**. Route đi từ claim lịch sử/chính trị → giả định chức năng và nhân quả → phản ví dụ, dữ liệu và alternative explanation → câu hỏi mở, để phê phán kiểm tra từng lớp thay vì phủ nhận hoặc bảo vệ cả hệ thống một lần.

Sau khi đi từ Marx tới Lenin, doctrine và historical hiện thực (implementation / 구현), critique không nên bắt đầu bằng verdict “đúng/sai toàn bộ”. Một tradition lớn chứa claims khác loại: lý thuyết (theory / 이론) of lịch sử (history / 이력), giá trị (value / 값) lý thuyết (theory / 이론), lớp (class / 클래스) phân tích (analysis / 분석), account of trạng thái (state / 상태), chuyển tiếp (transition / 전이) chiến lược (strategy / 전략), institutional thiết kế (design / 설계) và normative critique. Một objection chỉ có force trong phạm vi claim nó thực sự chạm tới.

Chapter này vì vậy dùng nguyên tắc:

```text
identify claim
→ identify mechanism
→ identify evidence standard
→ state strongest objection
→ state possible reply
→ mark what remains unresolved
```

Mục tiêu không phải cân bằng giả tạo giữa mọi position, mà tránh lỗi dùng one criticism để bác bỏ toàn bộ tradition hoặc dùng one surviving insight để bảo vệ mọi historical hiện thực (implementation / 구현).

## Historical materialism: law of lịch sử (history / 이력) hay ràng buộc (constraint / 제약조건) khung phần mềm (framework / 프레임워크)?

Strong deterministic reading có thể được viết như:

```text
productive forces develop
→ existing relations become fetters
→ class conflict intensifies
→ new mode of production emerges
```

Bài toán (problem / 문제) is that twentieth- and twenty-first-century lịch sử (history / 이력) does not follow one universal chuỗi (sequence / 시퀀스). Industrialization has coexisted with liberal democracy, authoritarian capitalism, xã hội (social / 사회적) democracy, fascism, communist revolution and many hybrid paths.

A strong deterministic lý thuyết (theory / 이론) therefore faces counterevidence unless it specifies phạm vi (scope / 범위) conditions broad enough to explain variation without becoming unfalsifiable.

A weaker reading treats môi trường vận hành (production / 운영 환경) relations and productive sức chứa (capacity / 용량) as **các ràng buộc (constraints / 제약조건들) and nhân quả (causal / 인과적) pressures**, not a unique historical clock. Then claim becomes more plausible but also less predictive:

```text
material structure shapes feasible institutions and conflicts
≠
material structure uniquely determines political outcome
```

This is a recurring sự đánh đổi (trade-off / 트레이드오프) in xã hội (social / 사회적) lý thuyết (theory / 이론): stronger claims predict more but are easier to falsify; weaker frameworks travel better but explain less by themselves.

Historical materialism can be read as a claim about patterned constraints and mechanisms, or as a deterministic law of history. Keeping those readings separate makes it possible to test functional explanations instead of assuming inevitability.

## Functional explanation: does capitalism fall because it blocks productive forces?

Some reconstructions of Marxian lịch sử (history / 이력) use functional explanation: relations of môi trường vận hành (production / 운영 환경) persist because they help develop productive forces, then are replaced when they become obstacles.

The critique is nhân quả (causal / 인과적): saying institution exists **because of the hàm (function / 함수) it performs** requires a cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘) of selection. In biology, natural selection can provide such cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘). In xã hội (social / 사회적) institutions, what selects the more productivity-enhancing arrangement? Competition? Lớp (class / 클래스) struggle? Trạng thái (state / 상태) sức chứa (capacity / 용량)? War? Political coalition?

Without selection cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘), functional explanation risks reading kết quả (outcome / 결과) backward.

A stronger phiên bản (version / 버전) therefore needs:

```text
institutional variation
→ differential performance / power
→ mechanism selecting or preserving some arrangements
→ observed historical change
```

Analytical Marxists such as G. A. Cohen made this issue tường minh (explicit / 명시적), which is one reason the tradition is useful even when one rejects parts of its answer.

Functional explanation asks whether institutions persist because they solve a coordination problem or because power reproduces them. The claim that capitalism blocks productive forces therefore needs a mechanism and counterevidence, not only a historical narrative.

## Lớp (class / 클래스): central variable versus total explanation

Marxian lớp (class / 클래스) phân tích (analysis / 분석) is strongest when it identifies durable differences in quyền sở hữu (ownership / 소유권), điều khiển (control / 제어), bargaining position and dependence on labor markets. It becomes weaker when every xã hội (social / 사회적) cleavage is reduced to lớp (class / 클래스).

A multidimensional mô hình (model / 모델) can hold:

```text
class
status
race / ethnicity
citizenship
religion
nation
 gender / household relation
organization
```

These variables can interact. For example, lớp (class / 클래스) position can shape exposure to rủi ro (risk / 위험) while citizenship controls legal truy cập (access / 접근) to jobs; gender can shape unpaid care burden; race can alter housing/credit truy cập (access / 접근); nationalism can override lớp (class / 클래스) coalition.

The burden of proof depends on claim strength. “Lớp (class / 클래스) matters” is empirically modest. “Lớp (class / 클래스) is the ultimately decisive explanation of all major xung đột (conflict / 충돌)” is much stronger and requires bằng chứng (evidence / 증거) showing why alternative dimensions are derivative rather than independently nhân quả (causal / 인과적).

Class can be a central variable for ownership, work and distribution without being a total explanation of every identity or outcome. Moving from class position to class consciousness requires an explicit mechanism of organization, experience and interpretation.

## From lớp (class / 클래스) position to lớp (class / 클래스) consciousness: missing cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘)

A structural quan hệ (relation / 관계) does not automatically produce dùng chung (common / 공통) political định danh (identity / 식별자). Collective hành động (action / 동작) faces coordination, thông tin (information / 정보), trust and free-rider problems.

A more complete chuỗi (chain / 사슬) is:

```text
shared structural position
→ perceived common interest
→ communication
→ organization
→ identity / consciousness
→ collective action
```

Break any arrow and similar lớp (class / 클래스) positions can produce different political hành vi (behavior / 동작). This is one place where Marxist lý thuyết (theory / 이론) benefits from political sociology rather than relying on lớp (class / 클래스) cấu trúc (structure / 구조) alone.

Class position does not mechanically produce class consciousness; organization, conflict, ideology and institutions mediate the transition. The labor theory of value is another focused claim whose target and assumptions must be stated before critique.

## Labor lý thuyết (theory / 이론) of giá trị (value / 값): what exactly is being challenged?

Marx's giá trị (value / 값) lý thuyết (theory / 이론) is often criticized as if it were simply the claim that observed thị trường (market / 시장) price equals embodied labor thời gian (time / 시간). That is too crude. His khung phần mềm (framework / 프레임워크) distinguishes giá trị (value / 값) from thị trường (market / 시장) price and later tries to explain môi trường vận hành (production / 운영 환경) prices and average profit.

The serious debate asks whether labor-value magnitudes are necessary or successful explanatory variables for:

- relative prices;
- profit and exploitation;
- allocation of xã hội (social / 사회적) labor;
- dynamics of accumulation;
- reproduction of lớp (class / 클래스) relations.

Mainstream economics generally explains price and factor income using preferences, scarcity, môi trường vận hành (production / 운영 환경) functions, marginal conditions, rủi ro (risk / 위험), bargaining and thị trường (market / 시장) cấu trúc (structure / 구조) without requiring labor values.

The critique therefore has two levels:

```text
price-theory critique:
can the model explain observed relative prices and profit rates?

social-theory critique:
does rejecting labor values remove the explanation of power/exploitation?
```

They are not the same.

Critiquing labor theory of value requires specifying whether the target is price formation, surplus, exploitation or a transformation from values to production prices. The transformation problem makes the mathematical and conceptual bridge explicit.

## Transformation bài toán (problem / 문제): values, môi trường vận hành (production / 운영 환경) prices and profit rates

A major technical issue arises because industries use different ratios of labor to machinery/materials. If only living labor creates surplus giá trị (value / 값) in Marx's khung phần mềm (framework / 프레임워크), industries with more labor should mechanically generate different profit rates. Yet competition tends to equalize profit rates across capitals.

Marx addresses this in *Capital* Volume III through transformation from values to **prices of môi trường vận hành (production / 운영 환경)**, but the consistency and interpretation of this transformation generated a long literature.

The important học tập (learning / 학습) điểm (point / 지점) is not to memorize one “solution.” It is to see why the bài toán (problem / 문제) exists:

```text
surplus tied to living labor
+
different capital compositions
+
competitive profit-rate equalization
→ need a mapping from values to production prices
```

Any reconstruction has to show that its equations and definitions remain coherent.

Transformation problems concern how value categories relate to observed prices and profit rates. Exploitation can also be analyzed through power, contract and surplus without accepting every version of labor theory of value, but the alternative mechanism must be named.

## Exploitation without labor lý thuyết (theory / 이론) of giá trị (value / 값)

Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy notes that thinkers including G. A. Cohen and John Roemer attempted to reconstruct exploitation without making it wholly dependent on labor lý thuyết (theory / 이론) of giá trị (value / 값).

This matters because “Marxian exploitation” can refer to at least three ideas:

1. **surplus-labor quan hệ (relation / 관계)** — workers perform more labor than embodied in goods they receive;
2. **asset inequality** — unequal quyền sở hữu (ownership / 소유권) forces some actors into disadvantageous exchange;
3. **domination/phụ thuộc (dependency / 의존성)** — one side has power because the other lacks acceptable alternatives.

If labor-value lý thuyết (theory / 이론) fails, (1) may need reformulation. Claims (2) and (3) can still be analyzed using thuộc tính (property / 속성), bargaining and power.

Therefore the rational cấu trúc (structure / 구조) is not:

```text
labor theory false → exploitation impossible
```

but:

```text
which definition of exploitation?
→ which premises does it require?
→ which evidence supports those premises?
```

Exploitation may arise under formally voluntary contracts when outside options, ownership and bargaining power are asymmetric. Profit analysis then asks whether surplus reflects exploitation, entrepreneurship, risk bearing, scarcity rents or a mixture.

## Voluntary đặc tả hợp đồng (contract / 계약) versus structural phụ thuộc (dependency / 의존성)

A central normative disagreement is whether a voluntarily signed employment đặc tả hợp đồng (contract / 계약) can still be exploitative.

Libertarian approaches often emphasize legitimate thuộc tính (property / 속성) acquisition and consent. Marxian/socialist approaches emphasize background phân phối (distribution / 분포) and dependence: if truy cập (access / 접근) to productive assets is highly unequal, “choice” may occur inside a constrained option set.

The cốt lõi (core / 핵심) distinction is:

```text
transaction-level voluntariness
≠
background fairness / independence
```

Both can be evaluated separately. Đặc tả hợp đồng (contract / 계약) can be non-coerced in a narrow legal sense while background institutions remain unequal; conversely inequality alone does not prove every giao dịch (transaction / 트랜잭션) unjust.

This is why normative phân tích (analysis / 분석) needs tường minh (explicit / 명시적) premises rather than importing moral conclusion from economic vocabulary.

Profit has competing explanations that may coexist; separating surplus value from entrepreneurship, risk and rents is an identification task. Crisis theory adds a dynamic question about how these mechanisms can generate instability without predicting one inevitable event.

## Profit: surplus giá trị (value / 값) versus entrepreneurship, rủi ro (risk / 위험) and rents

Hiện đại (modern / 현대적) economics distinguishes returns associated with capital, rủi ro (risk / 위험), innovation, entrepreneurship, monopoly rent and scarcity. Marxian lý thuyết (theory / 이론) analyzes profit through surplus giá trị (value / 값) at the mức (level / 수준) of capitalist môi trường vận hành (production / 운영 환경) and phân phối (distribution / 분포).

A good comparison asks what each khung phần mềm (framework / 프레임워크) explains rather than replacing one term with another.

For a concrete firm, observed profit can reflect multiple components:

```text
normal return / financing cost
innovation rent
market power
risk compensation
scarcity rent
accounting conventions
labor bargaining outcomes
```

Therefore an empirical claim that all observed profit maps directly to one Marxian category needs additional công việc (work / 작업).

Crisis theory offers tendencies and mechanisms, not a single timetable. Falling profit rates are one proposed tendency whose countertendencies, measurement choices and historical scope determine whether it is testable.

## Crisis lý thuyết (theory / 이론): tendency is not a single prediction

Marx discusses several crisis-generating mechanisms across his works: disproportionality, credit, overaccumulation, realization problems, competitive pressure and profitability dynamics. Later Marxists developed different crisis theories.

This plurality matters. If every recession is explained by whichever cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘) fits afterward, lý thuyết (theory / 이론) becomes too flexible. A strong crisis explanation needs ex ante cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘) and measurable indicators.

For example:

```text
hypothesis
→ expected leading conditions
→ mechanism
→ observable pattern
→ rival explanation
```

A banking crisis driven by leverage and liquidity is not automatically bằng chứng (evidence / 증거) for every Marxian crisis lý thuyết (theory / 이론).

Falling profit can be offset by productivity, cheap inputs, turnover, finance or institutional change. The result is a conditional hypothesis, which matters when comparing revolutionary transformation with reform strategies.

## Falling tỷ lệ (rate / 비율) of profit: tendency, countertendencies and testability

Marx's discussion of a **tendency of the tỷ lệ (rate / 비율) of profit to fall** links accumulation and rising capital intensity with pressure on profitability, while also discussing counteracting forces.

The empirical challenge is specification. Which profit tỷ lệ (rate / 비율)? Which denominator? At firm, sector or economy mức (level / 수준)? How giá trị (value / 값) categories map to national accounts? What countertendencies are allowed?

If countertendencies can explain every reversal, the hypothesis risks weak falsifiability. If definitions are fixed and dữ liệu (data / 데이터) ánh xạ (mapping / 매핑) transparent, it becomes a legitimate empirical research program—even if results disagree.

Revolution and reform differ in transition burden, coordination requirements and institutional risk; neither follows automatically from a tendency. The transition question then asks who constrains authority while new institutions are being built.

## Revolution versus reform: chuyển tiếp (transition / 전이) burden

Revolutionary chiến lược (strategy / 전략) argues that existing trạng thái (state / 상태)/thuộc tính (property / 속성) structures may be too deeply tied to lớp (class / 클래스) power for incremental reform to transform them. Reformist socialist and social-democratic traditions điểm (point / 지점) to historical gains through elections, unions, welfare states and regulation.

The debate is not merely “fast versus slow.” It concerns institutional reversibility and power:

```text
Can reform alter ownership/control deeply enough?
Can revolutionary concentration of power remain accountable?
Which path preserves correction mechanisms?
Which path can survive organized opposition from losers?
```

Historical cases show both reform durability and quay lui (rollback / 롤백), as well as revolutionary transformation and authoritarian consolidation. No general answer follows from one trường hợp (case / 사례).

A transition needs a theory of accountability, coercion, information and contestability, not only a destination. Planning and knowledge add a practical boundary: computational power does not remove dispersed information and incentive problems.

## Chuyển tiếp (transition / 전이) bài toán (problem / 문제): who constrains the chuyển tiếp (transition / 전이) authority?

Marxist-Leninist chuyển tiếp (transition / 전이) concentrates political sức chứa (capacity / 용량) to transform thuộc tính (property / 속성) and suppress counterrevolution. Critics ask how this sức chứa (capacity / 용량) is prevented from becoming a new durable hierarchy.

A complete chuyển tiếp (transition / 전이) lý thuyết (theory / 이론) needs at least:

```text
selection of leaders
constraints on coercion
independent information channels
succession rules
rights of dissent
local autonomy
error correction
conditions for decentralization
```

Without these, “withering away” remains an endpoint description rather than cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘).

Planning can coordinate known objectives and resources, but local knowledge, feedback and incentives limit central computation. Public and private power then ask who sets rules, owns assets and can discipline the other side.

## Planning and kiến thức (knowledge / 지식): computational power is not the whole bài toán (problem / 문제)

Central planning critiques often invoke dispersed kiến thức (knowledge / 지식): cục bộ (local / 로컬) conditions, tacit kiến thức (knowledge / 지식) and changing preferences are difficult to aggregate centrally. Hiện đại (modern / 현대적) computation reduces some processing các ràng buộc (constraints / 제약조건들) but does not automatically solve:

- truthful reporting;
- mục tiêu (objective / 목표) selection;
- incentive tính tương thích (compatibility / 호환성);
- innovation under bất định (uncertainty / 불확실성);
- political legitimacy;
- lỗi (error / 오류) correction.

Likewise markets do not solve every thông tin (information / 정보) bài toán (problem / 문제). Prices can be distorted by monopoly, externalities, asymmetric thông tin (information / 정보) or missing markets.

The real comparison is kiến trúc (architecture / 아키텍처):

```text
what information exists?
who observes it?
what incentive exists to report truthfully?
how is information aggregated?
how fast does error feed back?
who bears cost of error?
```

This lets planning and thị trường (market / 시장) institutions be compared symmetrically.

Public and private power may substitute for or reinforce one another; the boundary is institutional and empirical, not a moral binary. Prediction and retrodiction test whether a theory actually constrains what would count as evidence.

## Công khai (public / 공개) power and private power

Liberal critique of Marxism–Leninism emphasizes concentration of trạng thái (state / 상태)/party power and risks to civil liberty. Marxian critique of capitalism emphasizes private power through quyền sở hữu (ownership / 소유권), employment, credit and thị trường (market / 시장) concentration.

These can both be true in different dimensions. Political economy should measure both:

```text
public domination risk
private domination risk
```

A khung phần mềm (framework / 프레임워크) that recognizes only one kind of power will systematically miss institutional failures on the other side.

Prediction asks what a theory expects before an outcome; retrodiction reconstructs why an observed case fits. Without conditions that could disconfirm it, a narrative risks unfalsifiability rather than explanatory strength.

## Prediction, retrodiction and unfalsifiability

A lý thuyết (theory / 이론) is easy to protect if every thất bại (failure / 실패) can be reclassified as “not the real lý thuyết (theory / 이론)” and every success counted as confirmation. The mirror-image lỗi (error / 오류) also exists: treating every bad historical kết quả (outcome / 결과) under a self-described Marxist trạng thái (state / 상태) as proof against every Marxian analytical claim.

A disciplined kiểm thử (test / 테스트) records before evaluation:

```text
claim
scope condition
expected observation
possible falsifier
auxiliary assumptions
```

Then historical bằng chứng (evidence / 증거) can actually revise belief rather than only decorate prior commitments.

Decomposing a theory into mechanism, scope condition, observable implication and rival explanation shows what survives critique. The final sources section should preserve those distinctions and identify which claims remain open.

## What survives decomposition?

Decomposing the tradition leaves a mixed picture rather than one verdict. Some claims—such as the centrality of quyền sở hữu (ownership / 소유권), bargaining power, institutional reproduction and capital concentration—remain live research questions. Other claims, especially strong deterministic readings or technical value-theory propositions, face substantial theoretical and empirical criticism.

This is exactly why a thư viện kiến thức (knowledge library / 지식 라이브러리) should not store “Marxism = true/false.” It should store a map of claims, mechanisms, objections and bằng chứng (evidence / 증거) boundaries.

The next chapter [Later Marxist traditions](09_later_marxist_traditions.md) shows how thinkers within or near the tradition responded by revising lớp (class / 클래스) lý thuyết (theory / 이론), culture, exploitation, methodology and institutional thiết kế (design / 설계) rather than preserving every thành phần (component / 컴포넌트) unchanged.

What survives decomposition should be anchored to sources that distinguish primary texts, historical evidence and later empirical tests. An open question is stronger when its unresolved assumption is named explicitly.
