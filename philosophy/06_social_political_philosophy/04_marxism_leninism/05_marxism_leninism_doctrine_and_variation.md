**Marxism–Leninism** vì vậy không nên được dùng như tên gọi ngược cho toàn bộ những gì Marx và Lenin từng viết. It is better understood as a historically systematized khung phần mềm (framework / 프레임워크) built from authoritative interpretations of Marx, Engels and Lenin, later institutionalized in Soviet political life and adapted elsewhere.

## From intellectual tradition to doctrine

An intellectual tradition normally contains disagreement. Scholars and activists argue about interpretation, bằng chứng (evidence / 증거), chiến lược (strategy / 전략) and institutional thiết kế (design / 설계). Once a ruling organization needs an official doctrine, the hàm (function / 함수) changes.

Doctrine must do more than generate inquiry. It may need to:

```text
identify canonical authors
→ define authorized vocabulary
→ organize curriculum
→ explain history
→ justify institutions
→ guide cadres and policy language
→ distinguish orthodox from heterodox interpretation
```

This transformation matters epistemically. A concept can hàm (function / 함수) at once as analytical claim, educational category and legitimacy ngôn ngữ (language / 언어). Those functions must be separated when evaluating truth or explanatory giá trị (value / 값).

## Canon formation: why “Marx–Engels–Lenin” becomes a lineage

Marx and Engels wrote across decades and changed emphasis. Lenin wrote in different strategic contexts. Later systematization tends to reduce this plurality into a more coherent lineage.

This does not mean the lineage is fabricated; there are real continuities. But codification performs **selection and compression**:

```text
large heterogeneous corpus
→ selected canonical texts
→ standardized concepts
→ teachable doctrine
```

Every compression loses some ambiguity and debate. Therefore when a textbook presents a clean proposition, a source-critical reader should ask whether it is direct primary-text claim, later synthesis, or institutional formulation.

## Dialectical materialism: worldview, phương thức (method / 메서드), or doctrine?

In later Marxist-Leninist doctrine, **dialectical materialism** often functions as a general worldview and methodology: reality is material, changing, relational and characterized by tensions or contradictions.

The term can be used at several levels:

1. **ontological** — reality is not fundamentally dependent on mind/spirit;
2. **methodological** — objects should be studied dynamically, relationally and historically;
3. **doctrinal** — a set of named laws/categories taught as part of official philosophy.

These levels should not be collapsed. The methodological advice “study các hệ thống (systems / 시스템들) historically and relationally” is weaker than a universal ontological claim, and both are different from a fixed classroom lược đồ (schema / 스키마).

A useful discipline is to translate dialectical ngôn ngữ (language / 언어) into a testable cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘) whenever possible:

```text
contradiction → which variables or relations are in tension?
quantity → quality → what threshold mechanism?
negation → what concrete transformation?
interconnection → which causal pathways?
```

If no cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘) or bằng chứng (evidence / 증거) can be specified, dialectical vocabulary risks becoming post-hoc description.

## Historical materialism inside doctrine

Later Marxism–Leninism commonly presents **historical materialism** as ứng dụng (application / 애플리케이션) of materialist-dialectical phân tích (analysis / 분석) to society. Productive forces, relations of môi trường vận hành (production / 운영 환경), lớp (class / 클래스) cấu trúc (structure / 구조) and political superstructure are organized into a more systematic explanatory kiến trúc (architecture / 아키텍처) than one finds in any single Marx văn bản (text / 텍스트).

The strength of this codification is pedagogical clarity. The rủi ro (risk / 위험) is **mechanical determinism**: treating xã hội (social / 사회적) thay đổi (change / 변경) as a fixed chuỗi (sequence / 시퀀스) where economy mechanically produces politics and ideology.

A deeper reading should preserve tương tác (interaction / 상호작용):

```text
productive forces ↔ production relations
production relations ↔ class structure
class structure ↔ political institutions
political institutions ↔ property and production
ideas / ideology ↔ organization and legitimacy
```

This retains historical-materialist emphasis without turning it into one-way reductionism.

## Scientific socialism: historical label versus contemporary epistemic tiêu chuẩn (standard / 표준)

**Scientific socialism (chủ nghĩa xã hội khoa học, 과학적 사회주의)** historically contrasts socialism grounded in an account of xã hội (social / 사회적) development with utopian blueprinting.

But “scientific” here is not self-certifying. Under contemporary philosophy of science, a xã hội (social / 사회적) lý thuyết (theory / 이론)'s claims still require:

```text
clear concepts
→ operationalization where possible
→ evidence
→ causal or comparative reasoning
→ exposure to counterevidence
→ revision when claims fail
```

This distinction protects two things at once: historical accuracy about how the tradition describes itself, and hiện đại (modern / 현대적) epistemic discipline about what counts as bằng chứng (evidence / 증거).

## Party leadership as doctrinal principle

Lenin's organizational lý thuyết (theory / 이론) enters Marxism–Leninism as a cốt lõi (core / 핵심) principle of **communist party leadership**. The party is commonly described as organized leadership of the working lớp (class / 클래스) and political center of socialist construction.

To understand this institutionally, decompose the claim into functions:

```text
representation
coordination
cadre selection
policy formulation
ideological education
state leadership
social mobilization
```

Each hàm (function / 함수) creates a corresponding accountability question. For example, if party claims biểu diễn (representation / 표현), what cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘) links party preferences to those represented? If party selects cadres, what constrains patronage or thông tin (information / 정보) filtering? If party leads the trạng thái (state / 상태), where does administrative expertise end and political direction begin?

## Party–trạng thái (state / 상태) quan hệ (relation / 관계): overlap is not định danh (identity / 식별자)

A Marxist-Leninist political hệ thống (system / 시스템) may contain both **party institutions** and **trạng thái (state / 상태) institutions**. They can overlap in personnel, chính sách (policy / 정책) direction and hierarchy but remain analytically distinct.

This matters because different channels perform different roles:

```text
party → political line, cadre system, organizational leadership
state → law, administration, budgeting, coercive/legal apparatus, service delivery
mass organizations / social bodies → participation, mobilization, representation channels
```

Actual arrangements vary by country and period. A description that says only “the party controls everything” may be too coarse to explain how decisions are made, while a purely formal constitutional description may understate party influence. Institutional phân tích (analysis / 분석) needs both formal and informal authority.

## Democratic centralism inside doctrine

**Democratic centralism** becomes a cốt lõi (core / 핵심) organizational vocabulary in Marxist-Leninist các hệ thống (systems / 시스템들). The broad principle combines discussion/participation with unified hiện thực (implementation / 구현) after decisions.

Its real meaning depends on rules, not name. Độ sâu (depth / 깊이) phân tích (analysis / 분석) asks:

```text
who participates in discussion?
who controls agenda?
what information is available?
how are leaders appointed/elected?
can minorities preserve positions?
what sanctions follow dissent?
what feedback exists after implementation?
```

Different answers produce very different institutional các hệ thống (systems / 시스템들) under the same label.

## Chuyển tiếp (transition / 전이): socialism and communism as different analytical levels

In Marxist-Leninist vocabulary, socialism is often treated as a transitional xã hội (social / 사회적) formation, while communism represents a more developed classless horizon.

This creates at least four separate chuyển tiếp (transition / 전이) tasks:

1. **thuộc tính (property / 속성) transformation** — who owns or controls major productive assets;
2. **coordination transformation** — how môi trường vận hành (production / 운영 환경) and allocation decisions are made;
3. **lớp (class / 클래스) transformation** — how old/new occupational and quyền sở hữu (ownership / 소유권) groups thay đổi (change / 변경);
4. **trạng thái (state / 상태) transformation** — why coercive lớp (class / 클래스) institutions should eventually diminish rather than become permanent.

If these are collapsed into one word “chuyển tiếp (transition / 전이),” cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘) disappears.

> **Chuyển mạch:** Đặt trong câu hỏi lớn của **Marxism–Leninism — doctrine, systematization, institutions và variation**, sau nội dung của **Chuyển tiếp (transition / 전이): socialism and communism as different analytical levels**, **Planning is not equivalent to công khai (public / 공개) quyền sở hữu (ownership / 소유권)** chỉ rõ tài liệu chuẩn và vị trí sở hữu để người học biết phần nào cần quay lại khi muốn đào sâu. ## Planning is not equivalent to công khai (public / 공개) quyền sở hữu (ownership / 소유권)

A doctrinal emphasis on công khai (public / 공개)/xã hội (social / 사회적) quyền sở hữu (ownership / 소유권) does not logically fix one single coordination cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘). State-owned assets can be coordinated by detailed command planning, negotiated targets, enterprise autonomy, thị trường (market / 시장) prices, competitive procurement or hybrids.

Therefore three variables must be kept separate:

```text
ownership
coordination
political control
```

A hệ thống (system / 시스템) can thay đổi (change / 변경) one without fully changing the others. China and Vietnam later provide especially important examples of continued party quy tắc (rule / 규칙) and significant quyền sở hữu trạng thái (state ownership / 상태 소유권) alongside extensive thị trường (market / 시장) coordination.

This is why [Historical implementation](06_historical_implementation_and_evidence.md) codes actual institutions rather than inferring them directly from ideological labels.

## Doctrine and chính sách (policy / 정책): do not assume one-to-one ánh xạ (mapping / 매핑)

Official doctrine can constrain chính sách (policy / 정책) ngôn ngữ (language / 언어) and legitimate broad goals, but actual chính sách (policy / 정책) also responds to:

```text
resource constraints
administrative capacity
war/security
technology
fiscal conditions
international trade
leadership conflict
social resistance
policy failure and learning
```

Thus ideological continuity can coexist with large chính sách (policy / 정책) thay đổi (change / 변경). Conversely, a chính sách (policy / 정책) may be justified using doctrinal ngôn ngữ (language / 언어) even when its cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘) resembles institutions found in very different ideological các hệ thống (systems / 시스템들).

A useful research quy tắc (rule / 규칙) is:

```text
doctrine ≠ policy
policy ≠ institution
institution ≠ outcome
```

Dấu vết (trace / 추적) each arrow instead of assuming định danh (identity / 식별자).

## National adaptation: transmission is never pure copying

After Soviet codification, communist movements and states adapted doctrine to different historical contexts. Agrarian cấu trúc (structure / 구조), colonial lịch sử (history / 이력), nationalism, war, development mức (level / 수준) and geopolitical position changed emphasis.

A conceptual genealogy might look like:

```text
Marx / Engels
→ Lenin
→ Soviet codification
→ national adaptation
→ reform / reinterpretation over time
```

This is why the same broad label can coexist with substantially different economic institutions and political practices across Soviet Union, China, Vietnam or Cuba.

## Marxism–Leninism, Stalinism and continuity debate

**Marxism–Leninism** as official doctrine and **Stalinism** as ideas, institutions and practices associated especially with Stalin's period overlap, but they are not perfect synonyms.

The serious historical issue is continuity and discontinuity. Some institutions developed from earlier Bolshevik practices; others intensified or changed qualitatively under Stalin. A responsible phân tích (analysis / 분석) therefore avoids two shortcuts:

```text
"Stalin = direct execution of every Marx/Lenin idea"
"Stalin = completely unrelated to Leninist tradition"
```

Both remove the historical công việc (work / 작업) needed to dấu vết (trace / 추적) institutional development.

## Doctrine as legitimacy ngôn ngữ (language / 언어)

Once institutionalized, ideology can perform a legitimacy hàm (function / 함수): explaining why ruling institutions are justified, what historical direction society is pursuing, and how chính sách (policy / 정책) sacrifices are interpreted.

This does not mean every doctrinal statement is false because it serves legitimacy. **Hàm (function / 함수) and truth are different dimensions.** A claim can be true and politically useful; false and useful; or contested and useful.

For each doctrinal proposition, separate:

```text
epistemic question: is the claim supported?
functional question: what political/institutional role does the claim play?
```

This distinction prevents ideology critique from becoming a genetic fallacy.

## Institutionalization creates an error-correction bài toán (problem / 문제)

When doctrine becomes tied to authority, criticism of lý thuyết (theory / 이론) may be interpreted as criticism of institution. That can create **epistemic lock-in**: actors have incentive to report bằng chứng (evidence / 증거) compatible with authorized categories.

The general cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘) is not unique to Marxist-Leninist states; corporations, bureaucracies and scientific paradigms can also filter unwelcome thông tin (information / 정보). But where political and doctrinal authority are strongly linked, the stakes of dissent may be higher.

An institutional phân tích (analysis / 분석) therefore asks whether there are channels for:

```text
negative information
policy experimentation
revision of theory
local feedback
independent verification
leadership correction
```

These variables become central in the Soviet/China/Vietnam trường hợp (case / 사례) studies later in the mô-đun (module / 모듈).

> **Chuyển mạch:** Đặt trong câu hỏi lớn của **Marxism–Leninism — doctrine, systematization, institutions và variation**, **Mô hình tư duy (mental model / 사고 모델) bàn giao** gom các mảnh từ **Institutionalization creates an error-correction bài toán (problem / 문제)** thành một kết luận có thể mang sang phần kế tiếp. ## Mô hình tư duy (mental model / 사고 모델) bàn giao

Marxism–Leninism should now be understood as **a historically codified and institutionalized ideological hệ thống (system / 시스템)**, not a timeless synonym for Marx's complete thought.

Its kiến trúc (architecture / 아키텍처) can be decomposed as:

```text
materialist worldview / dialectical method
→ historical-materialist social analysis
→ theory of capitalism and class
→ party leadership and revolutionary transition
→ socialist construction
→ long-run communist horizon
```

But kiến trúc (architecture / 아키텍처) alone does not tell us what actual institutions did. The next chapter therefore changes bằng chứng (evidence / 증거) kiểu (type / 타입): from textual/doctrinal reconstruction to historical institutional phân tích (analysis / 분석).

> **Chuyển mạch:** Trong **Marxism–Leninism — doctrine, systematization, institutions và variation**, **Mô hình tư duy (mental model / 사고 모델) bàn giao** nêu điều cần giải thích; **Sources và reading anchors** đối chiếu nó với bằng chứng hoặc nguồn kiểm chứng. Phần còn lại của file dùng kết quả này để khép lại mạch giải thích.
