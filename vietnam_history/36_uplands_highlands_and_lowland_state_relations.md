# Uplands và Central Highlands: trade, autonomy và quan hệ không đều với lowland states

## Vì sao mountain history thường bị viết mỏng?

History textbook thường có nhiều source từ court, capital, inscription, tax archive và monument ở lowland. Mountain communities để lại ít hơn loại evidence mà centralized states thích sản xuất. Kết quả là source bias dễ bị nhầm thành historical absence.

Chapter này hỏi: **upland/highland societies đã tham gia economy, diplomacy, warfare và state formation của vùng Việt Nam như thế nào, và vì sao lowland state thường vừa cần uplands vừa khó kiểm soát chúng hoàn toàn?**

Điểm xuất phát là bỏ binary “civilized lowland / backward highland”. Different ecology tạo different production, mobility và political incentives; khác không đồng nghĩa thấp hơn.

## Upland không phải một vùng duy nhất

“Miền núi” bao gồm nhiều historical spaces rất khác: northern uplands giáp southern China và Laos; Trường Sơn chạy dọc miền Trung; Central Highlands nối coastal Vietnam với Laos/Cambodia; western river valleys mở các east–west route.

Cũng không có một generic “người miền núi”. Việt Nam hiện có nhiều ethnic communities với language family, settlement pattern, political history và religious tradition khác nhau.

Vì vậy chapter này dùng **uplands/highlands** như geographical analytical category, không phải ethnic identity.

## Ecology tạo mobility khác lowland delta

Large delta hỗ trợ dense wet-rice settlement và fixed infrastructure. Mountain ecology thường phân mảnh thành valley, slope, forest và plateau. Transport cost cao hơn nhưng resource diversity khác: forest products, animal products, resin, wax, aromatic wood, mineral, elephant, medicinal plants và upland crops.

Một lowland state có thể cần những products này nhưng không đủ incentive/capacity để station official trong every valley.

```text
high-value upland resources
        ↓
lowland/coastal demand
        ↓
trade route + broker
        ↓
tribute / exchange / alliance
```

Indirect relation vì thế thường economical hơn direct administration.

## “Không bị cai trị trực tiếp” không có nghĩa isolated

Một community có thể nằm ngoài routine tax bureaucracy nhưng tham gia long-distance trade. Oxford scholarship về Laos/Vietnam nhấn mạnh maritime trade dựa một phần lớn vào forest products từ upland hinterlands.

Điều này đảo một assumption quen thuộc: port wealth không chỉ do sea. Behind maritime exchange là supply chain đi sâu vào mountains.

Hội An là ví dụ. Merchant ở riverfront chỉ là downstream end của một chain có thể bắt đầu bằng product gathered/produced xa coast.

## Coast–upland axis của Champa

Champa đặc biệt cho thấy mountain và sea nối nhau. Thu Bồn, passes và valleys liên kết sacred/political centres với upland resource zone và port.

Chăm–upland relations có thể gồm exchange, tribute, alliance, warfare, intermarriage và religious connection. Không nên assume coastal ruler directly governed all highland communities chỉ vì map tô cùng một color.

Chapter 34 vì thế phải được đọc cùng chapter này.

## Đại Việt và northern uplands: frontier là negotiation zone

Northern dynasties của Đại Việt cần mountain frontiers vì security, trade route và relation với China/Laos. Nhưng power ở frontier thường mediated qua local hereditary leaders, powerful families và communities có knowledge về terrain.

Court có thể grant title, demand tribute, arrange marriage, recognize local ruler hoặc send military expedition. Những mechanism này cho thấy sovereignty premodern có gradient.

```text
capital order
   ↓
local intermediary
   ↓
community compliance / bargaining
```

Nếu intermediary strong, centre gets stability at low administrative cost; nếu relation break, same frontier can become rebellion/border-conflict zone.

## Thổ ty và indirect rule như technology of governance

Trong Sinosphere frontier governance, systems tương tự **native chieftain / thổ ty** cho phép state recognize local hereditary authority while incorporating it nominally into broader hierarchy.

Logic rất pragmatic: central state thiếu information và personnel để govern mountain society directly; local leader có legitimacy/network; exchange recognition for tribute/order.

Nhưng indirect rule creates trade-off. Centre gains reach but not full standardization. Local elite retains autonomy and can switch allegiance if regional balance changes.

## Mountain passes là political infrastructure

Pass không chỉ là geography. Ai giữ pass có thể control troop movement, caravan và information.

Các east–west routes qua Trường Sơn nối central coast với Laos/Cambodia. Northern passes nối Red River world với Guangxi/Yunnan. Vì vậy highland settlement near pass có strategic weight disproportionate to population size.

Premodern political map should therefore mark **corridors**, not only capitals.

## Zomia: useful corrective nhưng không phải universal law

James C. Scott popularized **Zomia** để mô tả broad upland mainland Southeast Asia như space nơi nhiều communities historically avoided lowland state incorporation. Framework này hữu ích vì nó hỏi liệu mobility, crop choice và social organization có thể reduce state legibility.

Nhưng later scholarship warns against turning this into claim “mountains kill states” or every highland group deliberately escaped government. Cambridge work emphasizes enormous diversity: some highland communities built complex organization, participated deeply in trade, or formed close ties with states.

Vì vậy library dùng Zomia như **question generator**, không như answer.

Hãy hỏi: community này tránh state thật không? hay state không thấy lợi ích direct rule? hay relation mediated through trade/tribute? Evidence nào phân biệt các possibilities?

## Colonial rule thay đổi mountain classification

French colonial administration intensified mapping, ethnography, roads, plantation/resource survey và ethnic classification. Groups previously connected by flexible frontier relations increasingly bị đặt trong fixed administrative categories.

Classification helps colonial state make population **legible**: name, territory, chief, tax status, labor potential. Nhưng label có thể freeze fluid identities hoặc simplify complex local distinctions.

Đây là connection trực tiếp với chapter 30: archive không chỉ record identity; administration có thể help produce identity categories.

## Central Highlands và plantation frontier

Central Highlands có plateau suitable cho certain cash crops và became increasingly important under colonial and later economic expansion. Road building, plantation projects and migration changed land relation.

Economic logic:

```text
commercial crop demand
      ↓
land becomes monetized/valuable
      ↓
external migration + concession + infrastructure
      ↓
pressure on customary land/use systems
```

When land previously managed through village/community/customary rights enters titled/concession system, conflict is not only “ethnic”; it is also collision between property regimes.

## War in the Central Highlands: geography turns into strategy

During twentieth-century conflicts, Central Highlands became strategically important because routes, plateau airfields/roads and connection between coastal lowlands, Laos/Cambodia and southern regions mattered militarily.

Recent Cambridge scholarship on Vietnam’s ethnic minorities notes that Highlander communities were recruited and courted by multiple political/military actors, while resettlement, discrimination, militarization and autonomy movements complicated the picture.

Do not write “Montagnards supported side X” as a blanket sentence. Different communities, leaders and periods produced different alignments.

## FULRO: autonomy question cannot be reduced to Cold War camp

**FULRO** emerged in the 1960s from grievances among some highland/minority movements over autonomy, land, representation and treatment by lowland-dominated states, within a larger wartime environment.

Its history shows why national-war narrative alone is insufficient. Actors may have goals that do not map neatly onto the binary North/South or communist/anti-communist.

This is a good example of nested conflict:

```text
international Cold War
  + civil/state conflict
  + local autonomy / land grievances
```

Multiple layers can be true at once.

## 1975 không kết thúc mountain transformation

After 1975, state-building, internal migration, agricultural expansion, forestry policy, hydropower and market integration continued to transform highlands.

Coffee expansion in Central Highlands later connected smallholder/enterprise production to global commodity markets. Road/electricity/school access increased integration, but land pressure and ecological change also intensified.

Historical continuity nằm ở same core problem: centre seeks resource/integration; local communities negotiate costs and benefits under changing property and administrative systems.

## Ethnicity: category, lived identity và state classification

Modern official ethnic categories are useful for administration/statistics but historian must not project them unchanged backward for centuries.

Language, clan, locality, religion, marriage network and political affiliation can shape identity differently across time. A present ethnonym may consolidate older subgroups or change meaning under classification.

Vì vậy sentence “dân tộc X đã sống ở đúng territory này từ thời Y” cần evidence cụ thể, không chỉ modern map.

## Market integration không xóa cultural difference ngay lập tức

Road, school, mobile phone và market increase interaction nhưng do not mechanically produce assimilation. A community can participate in national market while preserving ritual, language or kinship form.

Conversely, cultural change can occur without formal state coercion through intermarriage, schooling, migration and media.

Assimilation/integration therefore are multi-dimensional processes similar to chapter 14’s frontier discussion.

## Places để đọc upland history

**Tây Bắc** giúp đọc river valley, pass và frontier relation với Laos/China. **Hà Giang/Cao Bằng/Lạng Sơn mountain corridors** cho thấy border trade + military geography. **Kon Tum–Pleiku** giúp đọc plateau, mission/colonial and wartime layers. **Buôn Ma Thuột/Đắk Lắk** nối indigenous highland societies, colonial plantation logic, war và later coffee economy.

Field reading phải hỏi: settlement ở valley hay plateau? road được mở khi nào? crop landscape có ancient hay recent? sacred/community space nằm đâu so với administrative centre?

## Compare lowland and upland state capacity

Lowland delta tends to make population easier to census/tax because settlement dense and agriculture fixed. Upland mobility/dispersal can raise information cost.

Nhưng technology changes this equation. Road, satellite mapping, digital registry và mobile network dramatically reduce distance/information barriers. A region hard to govern in 1500 can be administratively visible in 2000.

Therefore geography is constraint, not destiny.

## Recap và đường đọc tiếp

Uplands are not blank margins. They supply commodities, corridors, military depth and communities with political agency. Lowland states historically use a spectrum from trade/alliance/tribute to indirect rule and direct administration. Colonial and modern states increase mapping/standardization, but integration creates new land, identity and ecological conflicts.
