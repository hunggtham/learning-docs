## 1. Tinbergen: bốn loại câu hỏi

Một hành vi (behavior / 동작) có thể được hỏi ở nhiều mức (level / 수준):

- **cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘)**: neural, hormonal, cognitive tiến trình (process / 프로세스) hiện tại;
- **development (ontogeny)**: trait phát triển qua life lịch sử (history / 이력) thế nào;
- **hàm (function / 함수)**: trait có thể ảnh hưởng fitness ra sao;
- **phylogeny**: trait evolved across species lineage thế nào.

Cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘) và hàm (function / 함수) complement, not replace each other.

Ví dụ jealousy có proximate triggers, appraisal và học tập (learning / 학습) lịch sử (history / 이력); evolutionary hypothesis about mate retention is separate claim requiring separate bằng chứng (evidence / 증거).

## 2. Natural selection không có mục tiêu trung tâm

**Chọn lọc tự nhiên (natural selection)** changes frequency of heritable variants when they affect reproductive success under particular environments.

Evolution does not “want” happiness, morality or perfect health.

Anthropomorphic phrase “evolution designed X for Y” can be shorthand, but should not imply conscious planner.

## 3. Adaptation, by-product và drift

A trait may be:

- adaptation shaped by selection;
- by-product of another adaptation;
- consequence of developmental ràng buộc (constraint / 제약조건);
- neutral or near-neutral variation;
- culturally acquired mẫu (pattern / 패턴).

Not every hành vi (behavior / 동작) with plausible hàm (function / 함수) is adaptation.

## 4. Adaptationist storytelling

Weak evolutionary explanation often starts after observation:

```text
Behavior X exists
→ invent ancestral benefit Y
→ conclude X evolved for Y
```

This is post-hoc story unless Y predicts new patterns that distinguish competing accounts.

Good evolutionary hypothesis asks for bằng chứng (evidence / 증거) from:

- comparative species dữ liệu (data / 데이터);
- cross-cultural mẫu (pattern / 패턴);
- developmental timing;
- sex/age/ecology differences predicted in advance;
- genetic/physiological cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘);
- chi phí (cost / 비용)–benefit trade-offs.

> **Hypothesis quy tắc (rule / 규칙):** adaptation claim should create risky prediction, not just plausible narrative.

> **Chuyển mạch:** Trong **Tiến hóa, di truyền và hành vi — Evolution, Genetics & hành vi (behavior / 동작)**, **5. Mismatch** tiếp nhận điểm tựa từ **4. ## 5. Mismatch

An evolved cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘) can hàm (function / 함수) differently in hiện đại (modern / 현대적) môi trường (environment / 환경) from ancestral conditions.

Preference for energy-dense food is dùng chung (common / 공통) mismatch example, but “ancestral môi trường (environment / 환경)” was not one static cave. Human ecologies varied widely.

## 6. Behavioral genetics

Behavioral genetics estimates how genetic and environmental differences contribute to variation in traits.

Classical methods include twin/adoption designs; hiện đại (modern / 현대적) methods use measured DNA, genome-wide association studies (GWAS), polygenic scores and family-based genomic designs.

No phương thức (method / 메서드) is assumption-free.

## 7. Heritability

**Hệ số di truyền (heritability)** estimates proportion of phenotypic variance associated with genetic variance in a specific population/môi trường (environment / 환경)/mô hình (model / 모델).

It does **not** mean:

```text
“60% intelligence of this person is genetic”
```

## 9. Heritability does not explain group differences

This is especially important.

If a trait is heritable **within** group A and group B, this does not tell whether mean difference **between** groups is genetic, environmental or both.

Recent methodological công việc (work / 작업) reiterates that within-group heritability is uninformative by itself about causes of between-group differences.

Therefore using heritability estimate to explain ethnic, national, lớp (class / 클래스) or gender mean difference is invalid without much stronger nhân quả (causal / 인과적) bằng chứng (evidence / 증거).

## 12. Polygenic kiến trúc (architecture / 아키텍처)

Complex psychological traits are usually **polygenic**: thousands of variants can each contribute tiny effects.

There is no single “gene for intelligence”, “gene for depression” or “gene for aggression” in the simplistic sense.

Genes influence molecular/developmental pathways whose effects depend ngữ cảnh (context / 맥락).

## 13. GWAS

**Genome-Wide Association Study (GWAS)** tests associations between genetic variants and phenotype across large samples.

GWAS tín hiệu (signal / 신호) is association, not automatically nhân quả (causal / 인과적) variant or cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘). Linkage disequilibrium means associated marker may tag nearby nhân quả (causal / 인과적) biology.

Large N is necessary because individual effects are tiny.

## 14. Polygenic scores

**Điểm đa gene (polygenic score)** aggregates many variant weights to predict statistical liability/trait variation.

Polygenic score:

- is probabilistic;
- depends discovery mẫu (sample / 표본);
- often loses predictive accuracy across ancestry/ngữ cảnh (context / 맥락);
- can capture indirect family/xã hội (social / 사회적) pathways;
- is not destiny.

## 16. Gene–môi trường (environment / 환경) correlation

Three classic forms:

- **passive rGE**: parents transmit genes and correlated environments;
- **evocative rGE**: person's traits evoke responses;
- **active rGE**: person selects environments matching dispositions.

Thus môi trường (environment / 환경) exposure is often not random.

## 17. Gene–môi trường (environment / 환경) tương tác (interaction / 상호작용)

**Tương tác gene–môi trường (environment / 환경) (G×E)** occurs when tác động (effect / 효과) of genotype differs across environments or vice versa.

> **hiện tại (current / 현재) bằng chứng (evidence / 증거):** G×E exists for complex traits; specific tương tác (interaction / 상호작용) claims require large, well-measured and replicated samples.

## 19. Epigenetics

**Biểu sinh (epigenetics)** refers to molecular regulation affecting gene expression without changing DNA chuỗi (sequence / 시퀀스), such as DNA methylation and chromatin processes.

Môi trường (environment / 환경) can influence epigenetic states.

But popular claim “trauma rewrites genes and passes exactly to grandchildren” exceeds human bằng chứng (evidence / 증거). Intergenerational xã hội (social / 사회적) transmission and direct exposure are major confounders.

> **Established:** epigenetic regulation matters biologically.
>
> **Debated/hypothesis:** strong transgenerational inheritance of specific psychological trauma signatures in humans.

## 20. Gene expression is context-sensitive

A DNA chuỗi (sequence / 시퀀스) does not prescribe fixed hành vi (behavior / 동작) directly. Genes participate in cellular processes whose expression changes by tissue, developmental stage and môi trường (environment / 환경).

This is why “gene for hành vi (behavior / 동작) X” is usually misleading shorthand.

## 21. Cultural evolution

Human hành vi (behavior / 동작) also changes through xã hội (social / 사회적) transmission. Norms, ngôn ngữ (language / 언어), institutions and technology can spread much faster than genetic evolution.

**Đồng tiến hóa gene–culture (gene–culture coevolution)** studies phản hồi (feedback / 피드백) between biological and cultural inheritance.

Lactase persistence is chuẩn gốc (canonical / 정본) biological example; psychological domains may also show phản hồi (feedback / 피드백) between xã hội (social / 사회적) practice and selection pressure.

## 28. Intelligence and genetics

Cognitive-test hiệu năng (performance / 성능) has substantial heritability in many populations, and hiện đại (modern / 현대적) molecular genetics identifies highly polygenic associations.

- heritability changes across age/ngữ cảnh (context / 맥락);
- education and health matter;
- score is đo lường (measurement / 측정) construct;
- group differences cannot be inferred from within-group heritability;
- polygenic prediction is ancestry/ngữ cảnh (context / 맥락) dependent.

## 31. Moral naturalistic fallacy

“Evolved” does not mean:

- good;
- desirable;
- unavoidable;
- morally justified.

Evolutionary explanation is descriptive, not ethical prescription.

## 32. Race/ancestry caution

Genetic ancestry, socially defined race and ethnicity are related but not equivalent constructs.

Group labels often bundle môi trường (environment / 환경), discrimination, geography, di chuyển (migration / 마이그레이션) and socioeconomic lịch sử (history / 이력).

## 33. dùng chung (common / 공통) misconceptions

### “Heritability 70% means 70% of me is genetic”

Sai.

### “High heritability means cannot thay đổi (change / 변경)”

Sai.

### “If two groups differ and trait is heritable, difference is genetic”

Sai suy luận (inference / 추론).

### “Evolutionary explanation means hành vi (behavior / 동작) is inevitable”

Sai.

### “Epigenetics proves trauma is biologically inherited for many generations”

Too strong for human psychological claims.

### “A polygenic score predicts destiny”

Sai.

## 34. Evidence-status checklist for evolutionary claims

Before accepting adaptation claim, ask:

1. Is phenomenon reliable?
2. What alternative học tập (learning / 학습)/cultural account exists?
3. Does hypothesis make novel prediction?
4. Cross-cultural/comparative bằng chứng (evidence / 증거)?
5. Developmental cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘)?
6. chi phí (cost / 비용)/sự đánh đổi (trade-off / 트레이드오프) predicted?
7. Could trait be by-product?
8. Is dữ liệu (data / 데이터) independent of story used to generate hypothesis?

## 35. mô hình tư duy (mental model / 사고 모델)

```text
Genetic variation
      ↕
Development ↔ Environment
      ↕          ↕
 Learning ↔ Culture
      ↓
Behavior / trait distribution
```

For evolutionary lịch sử (history / 이력):

```text
Mechanism now
≠ Developmental origin
≠ Evolutionary function
≠ Phylogenetic history
```
