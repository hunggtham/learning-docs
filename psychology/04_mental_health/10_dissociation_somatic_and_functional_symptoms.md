# Phân ly, triệu chứng cơ thể và Functional Neurological Disorder

Triệu chứng cơ thể có thể gây disability rõ ràng ngay cả khi structural lesion không giải thích đầy đủ pattern. Một trong những lỗi nguy hiểm nhất là biến điều này thành nhị nguyên “hoặc bệnh thật, hoặc do tâm lý”. Modern clinical science xem symptoms qua nhiều level: nervous-system function, attention, expectation, learning, interoception, stress, behavior và social context.

> **Trạng thái bằng chứng tổng quát:** symptoms trong dissociation, somatic symptom presentations và Functional Neurological Disorder (FND) là real clinical phenomena. Một số behavioral/neurophysiological patterns có empirical support; các mechanistic models như predictive processing, altered agency hoặc salience-network accounts là **current theories/hypotheses**, chưa phải causal explanation duy nhất đã established.

Đây là tài liệu giáo dục, không dùng để self-diagnose.

## 1. Phân ly là gì?

**Phân ly (dissociation)** mô tả disruption trong integration của consciousness, memory, identity, perception hoặc sense of self/world.

Experiences có thể gồm:

- depersonalization: cảm giác tách khỏi chính mình;
- derealization: môi trường cảm giác unreal/distant;
- memory gaps trong một số contexts;
- discontinuity in awareness or identity.

Mild dissociation-like experiences có thể xảy ra trong fatigue, stress hoặc intense absorption. Clinical significance phụ thuộc frequency, distress, impairment và differential diagnosis.

## 2. Dissociation không đồng nghĩa psychosis

Derealization có thể rất distressing nhưng person thường vẫn recognize experience “feels unreal” rather than firmly believing world literally does not exist.

Psychosis involves different phenomena such as delusions/hallucinations and reality-testing changes. Differential diagnosis cần professional assessment.

## 3. Trauma và dissociation

Trauma exposure có association với dissociative symptoms trong many studies, nhưng **trauma không phải necessary cause cho mọi case**.

> **Established evidence:** trauma/stress can be important risk/context factors.
>
> **Limitation:** không được infer hidden trauma chỉ vì dissociation exists. Correlation và retrospective reports không support universal trauma-cause claim.

## 4. Somatic symptoms

Pain, fatigue, dizziness, gastrointestinal distress, palpitations và other bodily symptoms có thể bị influence bởi biological disease, autonomic state, attention, expectation, learning, behavior và context.

Một symptom không trở nên “imaginary” chỉ vì stress influence it. Pain itself is perceptual experience generated by nervous system, not direct meter of tissue damage.

## 5. Somatic Symptom Disorder

In diagnostic systems, **Somatic Symptom Disorder** does not require symptom to be medically unexplained. Focus is excessive/distressing thoughts, feelings or behavior related to symptoms alongside clinically significant burden.

This distinction matters: diagnosis should not be shorthand for “doctors found nothing”.

## 6. Functional Neurological Disorder

**Functional Neurological Disorder (FND)** includes motor, sensory, seizure-like or cognitive symptoms incompatible with recognized patterns of structural neurological disease in clinically meaningful ways.

Modern diagnosis increasingly emphasizes **positive clinical signs** of functional dysfunction, not diagnosis merely by exclusion.

Examples can include functional weakness, tremor or functional seizures, but exact assessment belongs to trained clinicians.

## 7. Symptoms are not fabricated by default

FND is not equivalent to malingering or “pretending”. Intentional symptom production is a different question.

A person can have involuntary symptoms produced by altered nervous-system function without structural lesion explaining them.

This distinction is critical for stigma and treatment engagement.

## 8. Predictive processing model

One influential current model frames perception/action as inference combining sensory input with prior expectation. In FND, overly precise priors or altered weighting could bias action/perception.

> **Trạng thái bằng chứng:** predictive-processing accounts are **current mechanistic hypotheses/theories**. They integrate several findings but are not uniquely proven causal models.

Do not write:

```text
FND = false belief causes symptom
```

That oversimplifies distributed motor, sensory, attentional, agency and contextual mechanisms.

## 9. Sense of agency

Some studies report altered experience of agency — feeling less control over movement/action — in FND.

Agency is itself an inference integrating intention, motor prediction and sensory feedback. Disturbance here is plausible mechanism, but effect varies across symptoms and patients.

## 10. Attention and symptom amplification

Attention can alter sensory gain. Monitoring a body signal repeatedly can increase salience and subjective intensity.

This is not “thinking about it creates fake symptom”; it reflects normal nervous-system modulation of perception.

> **Established principle:** attention influences perception.
>
> **Hypothesis/application:** exactly how attention contributes to a specific FND symptom must be assessed case by case.

## 11. Interoception

**Interoception** is perception/inference of internal bodily state. Altered interoceptive processing has been proposed in functional symptoms and anxiety-related bodily experiences.

Evidence supports differences in some tasks/populations, but no single interoception deficit explains all FND or somatic symptoms.

## 12. Stress is neither necessary nor sufficient

Stress can increase arousal, muscle tension, autonomic symptoms and attentional narrowing. It may trigger/worsen symptoms in some people.

But saying “FND is caused by stress” is too strong. Some patients report no obvious stress trigger; many stressed people never develop FND.

## 13. Neural findings

Neuroimaging literature reports altered activity/connectivity in networks involving salience, limbic, motor, attention and self-monitoring functions.

> **Established evidence:** group-level neural differences have been observed.
>
> **Debated interpretation:** which differences are cause, consequence, compensation or comorbidity remains uncertain.

A brain scan difference does not automatically establish one pathophysiology.

## 14. Comorbidity

Anxiety, depression, trauma-related symptoms, pain or fatigue may co-occur but are not required for FND diagnosis.

Treatment should address comorbidity when present without assuming it is the hidden cause of every neurological symptom.

## 15. Treatment as rehabilitation and relearning

FND treatment may involve education, physiotherapy, occupational therapy, psychological intervention and symptom-specific rehabilitation depending presentation.

A useful learning frame is to restore automatic movement, shift attention, reduce unhelpful avoidance and update expectation through successful action.

> **Evidence status:** multidisciplinary and specialized rehabilitation approaches have growing evidence, but treatment effect and optimal package vary by symptom subtype and study quality.

## 16. Psychotherapy role

Psychotherapy can help when symptoms interact with stress, avoidance, trauma, emotion regulation or illness beliefs. It should not be framed as “therapy proves symptom is psychological”.

Outcome benefit does not prove one causal theory.

## 17. Dissociative identity claims và memory caution

Historical controversies around recovered memory and suggestive therapy make source monitoring essential.

Dissociative symptoms are real; however, highly specific autobiographical claims elicited through suggestive procedures should not be treated as automatically historically accurate.

## 18. Common misconceptions

### “Không thấy lesion nghĩa là không có bệnh”

Sai. Function can be disordered without structural lesion visible on routine test.

### “Functional nghĩa là fake”

Sai.

### “FND luôn do trauma”

Sai. Trauma can be risk factor, not universal requirement.

### “Neuroimaging đã chứng minh exact mechanism”

Quá mạnh. Current imaging supports distributed-network involvement but causal interpretation remains active research.

### “Nếu psychotherapy giúp thì cause chắc chắn psychological”

Sai. Treatment response does not uniquely identify cause.

## 19. Mental model

```text
Predisposition
 + nervous-system state
 + attention / expectation
 + learning history
 + interoception / agency
 + stress / context
        ↓
Functional symptom pattern
        ↓
behavior + feedback + treatment
        ↓
maintenance or recovery
```

No single arrow is universal for every person.
