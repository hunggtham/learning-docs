# Groups, Networks, Organizations & Bureaucracy — Relational cấu trúc (structure / 구조) và organized hành động (action / 동작)

> **Mạch đọc:** Đặt **Groups, Networks, Organizations & Bureaucracy — Relational cấu trúc (structure / 구조) và organized hành động (action / 동작)** trong bản đồ [README](./README.md) để thấy đơn vị sở hữu (owner / 오너) và vị trí của nó. Nội dung đi từ **1. xã hội (social / 사회적) group** sang **2. Primary and secondary groups**; điểm nối này chuẩn bị câu hỏi cho các mục sau thay vì dừng ở định nghĩa đầu tiên.


Xã hội (social / 사회적) outcomes phụ thuộc không chỉ individual attributes mà còn positions in groups/networks và rules of organizations. Sociology of organization asks how coordination, hierarchy, routines and informal relations shape hành vi (behavior / 동작); mạng (network / 네트워크) sociology asks how ties create truy cập (access / 접근), influence and ràng buộc (constraint / 제약조건).

## 1. xã hội (social / 사회적) group

A group involves sustained tương tác (interaction / 상호작용), dùng chung (shared / 공유) định danh (identity / 식별자) or recognized membership. A statistical category like “people age 30–39” is not automatically a group.

## 2. Primary and secondary groups

Primary groups involve intimate durable relations; secondary groups are more tác vụ (task / 작업)/role oriented.

Hiện đại (modern / 현대적) life mixes both: workplaces can create friendships; families also coordinate tasks/resources.

## 3. In-group and out-group

Group boundaries shape trust, solidarity and stereotypes. In-group favoritism can arise without tường minh (explicit / 명시적) hostility toward outsiders.

## 4. tham chiếu (reference / 참조) groups

People evaluate themselves relative to groups they belong to or aspire to phép nối (join / 조인).

Tham chiếu (reference / 참조) groups influence consumption, career expectations and định danh (identity / 식별자).

## 5. Group kích thước (size / 크기)

As groups grow, communication/coordination costs rise and direct mutual monitoring weakens.

Larger groups can mobilize more resources but face free-riding.

## 6. Dyads and triads

A dyad depends directly on two members and dissolves if one leaves. A triad introduces coalition, mediation and majority possibilities.

Adding one actor can qualitatively thay đổi (change / 변경) relational cấu trúc (structure / 구조).

## 7. xã hội (social / 사회적) networks

A mạng (network / 네트워크) consists nodes and ties. Nodes may be people, firms, organizations or countries.

Mạng (network / 네트워크) questions focus on relational position, not only nút (node / 노드) attributes.

## 8. Degree and centrality

Degree counts ties; centrality concepts ask different forms of structural importance.

High degree, brokerage and closeness are distinct advantages and should not be collapsed into “influence”.

## 9. Strong and weak ties

Strong ties provide trust/hỗ trợ (support / 지원); weak ties can connect actors to nonredundant thông tin (information / 정보) and opportunities.

Weak ties are not always better; hàm (function / 함수) depends on tác vụ (task / 작업) and mạng (network / 네트워크) cấu trúc (structure / 구조).

## 10. Structural holes

A broker connecting otherwise disconnected groups can truy cập (access / 접근) diverse thông tin (information / 정보) and điều khiển (control / 제어) coordination.

Brokerage can create advantage but also trust burden.

## 11. Closure

Dense networks facilitate trust, norms and monitoring.

Closure and brokerage can be complementary: one supports cooperation, the other novelty/truy cập (access / 접근).

## 12. Homophily

Ties form disproportionately among similar actors due preferences, opportunity structures and institutional sorting.

Homophily contributes to segregation and unequal thông tin (information / 정보) truy cập (access / 접근).

## 13. Diffusion

Ideas, behaviors and innovations spread through networks.

Observed clustering can reflect diffusion or homophilous selection; nhân quả (causal / 인과적) identification needs designs separating them.

## 14. Threshold các mô hình (models / 모델들)

Individuals may adopt hành vi (behavior / 동작) when enough contacts adopt.

Different thresholds can generate cascades or stable nonadoption even with similar preferences.

## 15. xã hội (social / 사회적) capital

Xã hội (social / 사회적) capital refers resources accessible through relationships—thông tin (information / 정보), trust, hỗ trợ (support / 지원), obligations.

It can benefit insiders while excluding outsiders, so it is not uniformly socially beneficial.

## 16. Collective hành động (action / 동작)

Groups need coordinate contributions to dùng chung (shared / 공유) goals.

Selective incentives, định danh (identity / 식별자), monitoring and leadership can overcome free-riding.

## 17. Organizations

Organizations are formalized collectives with roles, boundaries, goals and procedures.

They solve coordination problems but create hierarchy, agency and inertia.

## 18. Bureaucracy

Bureaucracy coordinates large-scale activity through specialization, hierarchy, written rules, records and offices.

Its strengths—predictability and impersonality—can also create rigidity and goal displacement.

## 19. Formal vs informal organization

Official charts describe formal authority; real công việc (work / 작업) often relies on informal networks, norms and shortcuts.

Ignoring informal organization misrepresents hiện thực (implementation / 구현).

## 20. Routines

Routines store organizational bộ nhớ (memory / 메모리) and reduce quyết định (decision / 결정) costs.

They can improve độ tin cậy (reliability / 신뢰성) but persist after môi trường (environment / 환경) changes.

## 21. Organizational culture

Organizations develop dùng chung (shared / 공유) các giả định (assumptions / 가정들), ngôn ngữ (language / 언어) and rituals.

“Culture” should be linked to recruitment, incentives, leadership and routines rather than treated as vague atmosphere.

## 22. Goal displacement

Measures/procedures created to serve goals can become goals themselves.

Examples include optimizing metrics rather than underlying dịch vụ (service / 서비스) chất lượng (quality / 품질).

## 23. Goodhart-style cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘)

When a measure becomes mục tiêu (target / 대상), actors can game it or shift effort away from unmeasured dimensions.

This links organizational sociology to principal-agent economics.

## 24. Institutional isomorphism

Organizations may become similar due coercive rules, professional norms and imitation under bất định (uncertainty / 불확실성).

Similarity therefore does not always prove one cấu trúc (structure / 구조) is technically optimal.

## 25. Organizational fields

Firms, regulators, professions, suppliers and associations interact within fields that define legitimate practices.

Field-level pressures shape organizations beyond nội bộ (internal / 내부) efficiency.

## 26. Legitimacy

Organizations seek xã hội (social / 사회적) acceptance as well as technical hiệu năng (performance / 성능).

Adopting policies can be ceremonial if formal structures decouple from actual practice.

## 27. Decoupling

An organization may maintain official chính sách (policy / 정책) for legitimacy while everyday routines differ.

Research should compare formal documents with hiện thực (implementation / 구현) bằng chứng (evidence / 증거).

## 28. Professionalization

Professions điều khiển (control / 제어) expertise through credentials, associations and ethical standards.

This can improve chất lượng (quality / 품질) and also create closure/entry barriers.

## 29. Organizational ecology

Population ecology studies founding, survival and thất bại (failure / 실패) across organizational populations.

Density can increase legitimacy early and competition later.

## 30. Inertia

Large organizations may thay đổi (change / 변경) slowly due sunk routines, politics, regulation and độ tin cậy (reliability / 신뢰성) requirements.

Inertia can be thất bại (failure / 실패) or valuable stability depending môi trường (environment / 환경).

## 31. Power in organizations

Power comes from formal authority, expertise, điều khiển (control / 제어) of resources/thông tin (information / 정보), mạng (network / 네트워크) brokerage and alternatives.

Titles are only one nguồn (source / 소스).

## 32. Principal-agent relations

Organizations delegate tasks where goals/thông tin (information / 정보) differ.

Monitoring, incentives and professional norms are alternative quản trị (governance / 거버넌스) mechanisms.

## 33. Street-level bureaucracy

Frontline workers translate chính sách (policy / 정책) into practice under limited resources and ambiguous rules.

Discretion means hiện thực (implementation / 구현) can differ from formal chính sách (policy / 정책).

## 34. Organizations and inequality

Hiring, promotion, scheduling and evaluation routines can generate inequality even without tường minh (explicit / 명시적) discriminatory intent.

Opportunity structures are organizational, not only individual.

## 35. Remote/hybrid organization

Communication technology changes visibility, coordination and mạng (network / 네트워크) formation.

Effects depend tác vụ (task / 작업) interdependence, onboarding, documentation and informal kiến thức (knowledge / 지식) flows.

## 36. nền tảng (platform / 플랫폼) organizations

Platforms coordinate participants through algorithms, ratings and rules rather than classic hierarchy alone.

Algorithmic quản trị (governance / 거버넌스) can concentrate rule-setting power while decentralizing tác vụ (task / 작업) thực thi (execution / 실행).

## 37. mạng (network / 네트워크) đo lường (measurement / 측정)

Mạng (network / 네트워크) dữ liệu (data / 데이터) can come from surveys, communication logs, affiliations or observed tương tác (interaction / 상호작용).

Missing ties and ranh giới (boundary / 경계) definition strongly affect metrics.

## 38. thất bại (failure / 실패) modes

Sai lầm thứ nhất là reduce mạng (network / 네트워크) effects to number of friends.

Sai lầm thứ hai là infer influence from similarity without handling homophily.

Sai lầm thứ ba là describe formal org chart as actual workflow.

Sai lầm thứ tư là treat bureaucracy as inherently bad rather than coordination form with trade-offs.

Sai lầm thứ năm là lời gọi (call / 호출) every dùng chung (shared / 공유) workplace hành vi (behavior / 동작) “culture” without transmission/enforcement cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘).

## 39. phân tích (analysis / 분석) template

1. group/mạng (network / 네트워크) ranh giới (boundary / 경계) là gì?
2. ties/resources luồng (flow / 흐름) theo đường nào?
3. centrality/closure/brokerage cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘) nào matter?
4. formal and informal rules differ where?
5. routines/metrics shape hành vi (behavior / 동작) how?
6. authority/tài nguyên (resource / 자원)/thông tin (information / 정보) power ở đâu?
7. legitimacy pressures vs technical efficiency thế nào?
8. homophily vs influence thế nào?
9. organization reproduces inequality by which routine?
10. bằng chứng (evidence / 증거) observes stated cấu trúc (structure / 구조) or actual tương tác (interaction / 상호작용)?

Groups and organizations distribute opportunities. Chapter tiếp theo focuses directly on stratification: lớp (class / 클래스), status, mobility and cumulative advantage.
