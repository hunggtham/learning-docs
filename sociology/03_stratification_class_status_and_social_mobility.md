# Stratification, lớp (class / 클래스), Status & xã hội (social / 사회적) Mobility — Cách inequality được tạo, truyền và biến đổi

> **Mạch đọc:** Đặt **Stratification, lớp (class / 클래스), Status & xã hội (social / 사회적) Mobility — Cách inequality được tạo, truyền và biến đổi** trong bản đồ [README](./README.md) để thấy đơn vị sở hữu (owner / 오너) và vị trí của nó. Nội dung đi từ **1. Inequality vs stratification** sang **2. Income vs wealth**; điểm nối này chuẩn bị câu hỏi cho các mục sau thay vì dừng ở định nghĩa đầu tiên.


Xã hội (social / 사회적) stratification là patterned inequality giữa xã hội (social / 사회적) positions/groups về income, wealth, authority, prestige, bảo mật (security / 보안) và opportunities. Sociology không chỉ hỏi “ai có nhiều hơn”, mà hỏi institutions nào phân bổ resources, boundaries nào hạn chế mobility, và advantages/disadvantages tích lũy qua life course như thế nào.

## 1. Inequality vs stratification

Inequality có thể là bất kỳ difference về resources/outcomes.

Stratification nhấn mạnh difference được tổ chức thành tương đối durable hierarchy gắn với xã hội (social / 사회적) categories và institutions.

## 2. Income vs wealth

Income là luồng (flow / 흐름); wealth là stock of assets minus liabilities.

Wealth thường bất bình đẳng hơn income và truyền intergenerational advantage qua housing, education, nghiệp vụ (business / 비즈니스) capital và buffer against shocks.

## 3. lớp (class / 클래스)

Lớp (class / 클래스) can be defined through income, occupation, employment quan hệ (relation / 관계), quyền sở hữu (ownership / 소유권)/điều khiển (control / 제어) of productive assets or thị trường (market / 시장) situation.

Different lớp (class / 클래스) schemes answer different questions; do not treat “lớp (class / 클래스)” as one self-evident variable.

## 4. Status

Status refers prestige/xã hội (social / 사회적) honor. High income and high status often correlate but not perfectly.

Academic, religious or artistic positions can carry status disproportionate to income.

## 5. Power

Power is sức chứa (capacity / 용량) to shape outcomes even with resistance, or to set agendas/rules defining choices.

Economic resources, organizational position, expertise and mạng (network / 네트워크) centrality can all generate power.

## 6. Socioeconomic status

SES indexes combine education, occupation and income for practical phân tích (analysis / 분석).

Useful descriptively, but aggregation can obscure different mechanisms of each thành phần (component / 컴포넌트).

## 7. Absolute and relative mobility

Absolute mobility asks whether children have higher/lower lớp (class / 클래스)/income than parents in absolute terms.

Relative mobility asks how strongly origins predict destinations compared with others.

A growing economy can produce high absolute upward mobility while relative mobility remains low.

## 8. Intergenerational elasticity

Income persistence can be summarized by elasticity/correlation between parent and child economic outcomes.

Đo lường (measurement / 측정) requires long-run income and careful vòng đời (lifecycle / 생명주기) timing; single-year income is noisy.

## 9. Mobility tables

Chuyển tiếp (transition / 전이) matrices show movement between origin and destination classes/quantiles.

Observed mobility combines structural thay đổi (change / 변경) in occupational shares with exchange mobility.

## 10. Equality of opportunity

Conceptually distinguishes circumstances outside individual điều khiển (control / 제어) from effort/choices.

Operationalization is normative and difficult because “effort” itself is shaped by circumstances.

## 11. Cumulative advantage

Initial advantage can compound through better schools, networks, confidence, credentials, health and wealth.

Small early differences can widen without one discriminatory sự kiện (event / 이벤트) at every stage.

## 12. Matthew tác động (effect / 효과)

Recognition/resources often luồng (flow / 흐름) toward already recognized actors, reinforcing advantage.

Seen in science, careers and cultural markets.

Need distinguish cumulative advantage from true productivity differences.

## 13. xã hội (social / 사회적) reproduction

Families transmit economic, cultural and xã hội (social / 사회적) resources across generations.

Transmission mechanisms include wealth, neighborhood, school truy cập (access / 접근), ngôn ngữ (language / 언어) practices, networks and expectations.

## 14. Cultural capital and education

Schools may reward communication styles, kiến thức (knowledge / 지식) and dispositions more dùng chung (common / 공통) in advantaged families.

This can reproduce inequality even under formally equal rules.

## 15. xã hội (social / 사회적) capital and networks

Job thông tin (information / 정보)/referrals often travel through networks. mạng (network / 네트워크) segregation can reproduce unequal opportunity.

Referrals may improve match chất lượng (quality / 품질) while also excluding outsiders.

## 16. Credentialism

Credentials can tín hiệu (signal / 신호) skill, standardize selection and also serve as closure mechanisms restricting truy cập (access / 접근) to occupations.

Need distinguish genuine skill yêu cầu (requirement / 요구사항) from status competition.

## 17. Opportunity hoarding

Groups may monopolize valuable opportunities through licensing, neighborhood restrictions, legacy admissions, nội bộ (internal / 내부) networks or organizational rules.

Cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘) requires identifiable ranh giới (boundary / 경계) and điều khiển (control / 제어) over tài nguyên (resource / 자원).

## 18. Closure

Xã hội (social / 사회적) closure restricts truy cập (access / 접근) to resources/status through categories or credentials.

Closure may protect chất lượng (quality / 품질)/an toàn (safety / 안전) in some contexts and create rent/exclusion in others.

## 19. Labor-market segmentation

Labor markets can contain primary jobs with stability/careers and secondary jobs with low bảo mật (security / 보안)/mobility.

Workers may face barriers moving between segments.

## 20. Precarious employment

Temporary, gig, informal and unstable công việc (work / 작업) can shift rủi ro (risk / 위험) from organization to worker.

Precarity depends not only đặc tả hợp đồng (contract / 계약) kiểu (type / 타입) but predictability, bargaining power and xã hội (social / 사회적) protection.

## 21. Occupational prestige

Occupations differ in status beyond pay.

Prestige rankings can shape aspirations and xã hội (social / 사회적) tương tác (interaction / 상호작용); cross-cultural/thời gian (time / 시간) comparison requires caution.

## 22. Spatial inequality

Neighborhoods differ in schools, vận chuyển (transport / 전송), pollution, an toàn (safety / 안전), jobs and networks.

Residential sorting makes place both kết quả (outcome / 결과) and cause of inequality.

## 23. Segregation

Segregation measures uneven phân phối (distribution / 분포) of groups across neighborhoods/schools/workplaces.

It can kết quả (result / 결과) from discrimination, preferences, income inequality, housing chính sách (policy / 정책) and mạng (network / 네트워크) effects.

## 24. Neighborhood effects

Living in disadvantaged/advantaged area may affect outcomes through peers, schools, an toàn (safety / 안전), pollution and networks.

Selection into neighborhoods is a major identification challenge.

## 25. Education expansion

Expanding education can increase average attainment while relative advantage persists through institution chất lượng (quality / 품질), trường dữ liệu (field / 필드) of study or postgraduate credentials.

This is credential inflation/qualitative differentiation rather than simple equalization.

## 26. Health inequality

Health follows socioeconomic gradients through material resources, stress, công việc (work / 작업) conditions, môi trường (environment / 환경), hành vi (behavior / 동작) and healthcare truy cập (access / 접근).

Reverse causality also matters: poor health reduces earnings/employment.

## 27. Status stress

Relative position and perceived điều khiển (control / 제어) can affect stress and health.

Bằng chứng (evidence / 증거) needs separate status mechanisms from material deprivation.

## 28. Gender stratification

Gender inequality can arise through occupational sorting, care responsibilities, organizational routines, bargaining, norms and discrimination.

Raw gaps should be decomposed cautiously; controls may themselves be outcomes of gendered institutions.

## 29. Race and ethnicity as xã hội (social / 사회적) stratification categories

Race/ethnicity classifications are historically and institutionally constructed and can shape discrimination, residential patterns, networks and tài nguyên (resource / 자원) truy cập (access / 접근).

Categories vary across countries/thời gian (time / 시간); avoid treating them as fixed biological mechanisms.

## 30. Intersectionality

Xã hội (social / 사회적) positions intersect: lớp (class / 클래스), gender, ethnicity, di chuyển (migration / 마이그레이션) status and age can produce experiences not reducible to additive independent effects.

Empirically, tương tác (interaction / 상호작용) and institutional ngữ cảnh (context / 맥락) matter; intersectionality is more than listing identities.

## 31. di chuyển (migration / 마이그레이션) and stratification

Migrants may face credential nonrecognition, ngôn ngữ (language / 언어) barriers, legal status các ràng buộc (constraints / 제약조건들) and mạng (network / 네트워크) segmentation.

Second-generation outcomes depend education, neighborhoods and discrimination as well as parental capital.

## 32. Elite reproduction

Elite positions can reproduce through selective schools, internships, networks, inheritance and cultural signals.

Formal meritocratic selection can coexist with unequal truy cập (access / 접근) to preparation.

## 33. Meritocracy

Meritocratic institutions reward measured achievement, but what counts as merit and truy cập (access / 접근) to skill-building are socially organized.

Sociological phân tích (analysis / 분석) distinguishes merit ideal, đo lường (measurement / 측정) and opportunity cấu trúc (structure / 구조).

## 34. Poverty

Poverty can be absolute, relative, episodic or persistent.

Dynamics matter: households may cycle around threshold due shocks.

Poverty is not identical to low lớp (class / 클래스) status or xã hội (social / 사회적) exclusion, though related.

## 35. xã hội (social / 사회적) exclusion

Exclusion concerns inability to participate in valued institutions/relations, not only income.

It can involve housing, digital truy cập (access / 접근), documentation, vận chuyển (transport / 전송), stigma or political/civic barriers.

## 36. Welfare institutions

Taxes/transfers/services reshape market-generated inequality and risks.

Comparative welfare regimes differ in decommodification, family roles, targeting/universality and labor-market institutions.

Detailed tax/economic incidence belongs in Economics/công khai (public / 공개) Economics.

## 37. Inequality and xã hội (social / 사회적) cohesion

High inequality may affect trust, residential segregation, politics and health, but causality runs both directions and institutions mediate.

Avoid broad claims from country-level correlations alone.

## 38. đo lường (measurement / 측정)

Use income/wealth distributions, mobility panels, occupations, education, neighborhoods, mạng (network / 네트워크) dữ liệu (data / 데이터) and qualitative life histories.

Different measures capture different dimensions; one inequality chỉ mục (index / 인덱스) is not complete xã hội (social / 사회적) stratification.

## 39. thất bại (failure / 실패) modes

Sai lầm thứ nhất là use income as synonym for lớp (class / 클래스)/wealth/status.

Sai lầm thứ hai là equate high absolute mobility with equal opportunity.

Sai lầm thứ ba là lời gọi (call / 호출) every persistent advantage discrimination without tracing cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘).

Sai lầm thứ tư là “điều khiển (control / 제어) away” institutional pathways and lời gọi (call / 호출) residual the only inequality.

Sai lầm thứ năm là treat xã hội (social / 사회적) categories as timeless/universal.

## 40. phân tích (analysis / 분석) template

1. dimension: income, wealth, status, authority hay bảo mật (security / 보안)?
2. position/category được defined thế nào?
3. institution nào allocates tài nguyên (resource / 자원)?
4. family/mạng (network / 네트워크)/place transmission cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘) nào?
5. barriers/closure ở chuyển tiếp (transition / 전이) nào?
6. advantage accumulates across life course ra sao?
7. absolute vs relative mobility khác thế nào?
8. spatial/organizational sorting có matter không?
9. category intersection/ngữ cảnh (context / 맥락) nào?
10. bằng chứng (evidence / 증거) descriptive hay identifies nhân quả (causal / 인과적) cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘)?

Stratification is produced through institutions. Chapter tiếp theo studies those institutions—family, education, công việc (work / 작업), religion, media and political/civic organization—as interconnected các hệ thống (systems / 시스템들) rather than isolated topics.
