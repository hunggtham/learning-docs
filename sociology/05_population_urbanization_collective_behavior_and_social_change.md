# Population, Urbanization, Collective Hành vi (behavior / 동작) & Xã hội (social / 사회적) Thay đổi (change / 변경) — Demography, cities, movements và transformation

Societies thay đổi (change / 변경) when population structures, di chuyển (migration / 마이그레이션), technology, institutions and collective hành động (action / 동작) interact. Sociology studies not only whether population grows or cities expand, but how demographic composition changes relationships, institutions and inequality; how urban không gian (space / 공간) concentrates opportunity and disadvantage; and how collective actors transform norms/rules.

## 1. Demography as xã hội (social / 사회적) cấu trúc (structure / 구조)

Population is organized by age, sex/gender, household form, di chuyển (migration / 마이그레이션) status, education and geography.

Changes in composition can alter schools, labor markets, housing, care các hệ thống (systems / 시스템들) and politics even if individual hành vi (behavior / 동작) stays constant.

## 2. Fertility

Fertility rates reflect family preferences plus education, childcare, housing, công việc (work / 작업), norms, health and chính sách (policy / 정책).

Avoid monocausal cultural explanations when institutional các ràng buộc (constraints / 제약조건들) differ.

## 3. Mortality

Mortality varies by income, occupation, môi trường (environment / 환경), gender, region and health các hệ thống (systems / 시스템들).

Life expectancy is population summary and can hide within-group inequality.

## 4. Di chuyển (migration / 마이그레이션)

Di chuyển (migration / 마이그레이션) reallocates people across places and networks.

Push/pull ngôn ngữ (language / 언어) is only a start; legal status, networks, costs, thông tin (information / 정보) and household chiến lược (strategy / 전략) matter.

## 5. Chuỗi (chain / 사슬) di chuyển (migration / 마이그레이션) and networks

Previous migrants reduce thông tin (information / 정보)/housing/job-search costs for later migrants.

Networks can make flows persistent after original economic gap changes.

## 6. Assimilation and incorporation

Migrants may converge on ngôn ngữ (language / 언어), education, occupation and định danh (identity / 식별자) at different rates.

Incorporation depends institutions and destination ngữ cảnh (context / 맥락), not one tuyến tính (linear / 선형) assimilation đường dẫn (path / 경로).

## 7. Transnationalism

Migrants can maintain economic, family and cultural ties across borders through remittances, communication and travel.

Di chuyển (migration / 마이그레이션) does not imply complete xã hội (social / 사회적) detachment from origin.

## 8. Age cấu trúc (structure / 구조)

Population aging changes phụ thuộc (dependency / 의존성), care demand, labor supply and intergenerational relations.

Ageing is demographic fact; its xã hội (social / 사회적) tác động (effect / 효과) depends retirement, di chuyển (migration / 마이그레이션), family and welfare institutions.

## 9. Cohorts

Birth cohorts share historical exposures at similar life stages.

Observed age differences can reflect aging, period shocks or cohort experience; separate them conceptually.

## 10. Household transformation

Later marriage, lower fertility, divorce, single-person households and longer lifespan reshape housing/care/xã hội (social / 사회적) isolation patterns.

Household statistics should not be read as direct measures of loneliness or family breakdown.

## 11. Urbanization

Urbanization concentrates population in towns/cities and changes density, specialization, anonymity and xã hội (social / 사회적) networks.

Cities create both agglomeration opportunities and congestion/exclusion.

## 12. Urban ecology

Neighborhood patterns emerge from land markets, vận chuyển (transport / 전송), institutions, discrimination and household sorting.

Spatial mẫu (pattern / 패턴) is not purely cultural preference.

## 13. Segregation

Residential segregation can reproduce school, an toàn (safety / 안전), pollution and mạng (network / 네트워크) inequalities.

Measure spatial phân phối (distribution / 분포) explicitly; citywide diversity can coexist with neighborhood segregation.

## 14. Gentrification

Investment/in-migration can raise amenities/thuộc tính (property / 속성) values while displacing or economically pressuring incumbent residents.

Need distinguish direct displacement, exclusion of future low-income residents and cultural/political displacement.

## 15. Housing as xã hội (social / 사회적) hạ tầng (infrastructure / 인프라)

Housing determines location, wealth, stability, school truy cập (access / 접근) and neighborhood ties.

Tenure các hệ thống (systems / 시스템들) and zoning shape stratification beyond shelter consumption.

## 16. Công khai (public / 공개) không gian (space / 공간)

Parks, transit, streets and commercial spaces cấu trúc (structure / 구조) encounter and inclusion.

Rules/bảo mật (security / 보안)/thiết kế (design / 설계) can make công khai (public / 공개) spaces accessible to some groups more than others.

## 17. Rural sociology

Rural areas are shaped by agriculture, tài nguyên (resource / 자원) extraction, aging, dịch vụ (service / 서비스) truy cập (access / 접근) and outmigration.

Do not treat rural as merely “not urban”; institutional/economic structures differ.

## 18. Collective hành vi (behavior / 동작)

Collective hành vi (behavior / 동작) includes crowds, rumors, panics, fads and emergent coordinated hành động (action / 동작) outside established routines.

Hiện đại (modern / 현대적) research avoids assuming crowds are inherently irrational.

## 19. Xã hội (social / 사회적) movements

Movements pursue/thay đổi (change / 변경) collective claims through networks, organizations, identities and repertoires.

Movement emergence requires more than grievance—resources, opportunities, framing and networks matter.

## 20. Tài nguyên (resource / 자원) mobilization

Movements need money, skills, leadership, organizations and communication channels.

Widespread dissatisfaction alone does not create sustained movement.

## 21. Political opportunity

Institutional openness, elite divisions, repression and allies can thay đổi (change / 변경) movement chiến lược (strategy / 전략)/likelihood.

This is descriptive analytic framing, not guidance for influencing political outcomes.

## 22. Framing processes

Movements define problems, responsibility and hành động (action / 동작) through frames.

Frame resonance depends cultural ngữ cảnh (context / 맥락) and audience networks.

## 23. Repertoires of contention

Groups draw from historically available forms of collective hành động (action / 동작) such as petitions, strikes, demonstrations or boycotts.

Repertoires evolve with technology/institutions.

## 24. Diffusion of protest/innovation

Collective hành động (action / 동작) can spread through networks and observation.

Need separate dùng chung (common / 공통) shocks from xã hội (social / 사회적) contagion.

## 25. Countermovements

Institutional thay đổi (change / 변경) can mobilize groups seeking to resist/reverse it.

Xã hội (social / 사회적) thay đổi (change / 변경) is relational xung đột (conflict / 충돌), not one-direction progression.

## 26. Collective efficacy

Communities can coordinate informal xã hội (social / 사회적) điều khiển (control / 제어) when trust/cohesion combine with willingness to act.

Cohesion alone is not sufficient if residents lack resources or dùng chung (shared / 공유) expectations.

## 27. Technology and xã hội (social / 사회적) thay đổi (change / 변경)

Technology changes communication, surveillance, công việc (work / 작업), family coordination and organization quy mô (scale / 규모).

Xã hội (social / 사회적) institutions shape adoption/use; technological determinism is insufficient.

## 28. Digital networks

Online networks reduce communication chi phí (cost / 비용) and enable large-scale weak-tie coordination.

They also amplify algorithmic sorting, misinformation and visibility inequalities.

## 29. Globalization

Globalization connects di chuyển (migration / 마이그레이션), môi trường vận hành (production / 운영 환경), culture, organizations and institutions across borders.

It creates convergence in some practices and hybrid/cục bộ (local / 로컬) differentiation in others.

## 30. World-systems perspective

Toàn cục (global / 전역) division of labor can be analyzed through unequal positions in môi trường vận hành (production / 운영 환경)/trade networks.

Useful as structural lens but should not replace firm-level/institutional bằng chứng (evidence / 증거) or assume countries occupy immutable categories.

## 31. Modernization lý thuyết (theory / 이론)

Modernization theories link industrialization/urbanization/education to institutional/cultural thay đổi (change / 변경).

Historical diversity and đường dẫn (path / 경로) dependence challenge simple tuyến tính (linear / 선형) chuỗi (sequence / 시퀀스) toward one endpoint.

## 32. Phụ thuộc (dependency / 의존성) perspectives

Phụ thuộc (dependency / 의존성) approaches emphasize how bên ngoài (external / 외부) economic relationships can constrain domestic development.

Specific mechanisms—terms of trade, debt, quyền sở hữu (ownership / 소유권), technology điều khiển (control / 제어)—should be identified rather than relying on label alone.

## 33. Xã hội (social / 사회적) thay đổi (change / 변경) through institutions

Major thay đổi (change / 변경) often combines demographic/economic pressure with organizations, legal reform, movement activity and norm thay đổi (change / 변경).

One trigger rarely explains durable transformation.

## 34. Diffusion vs independent adaptation

Similar reforms across countries may spread through imitation/professional networks or emerge independently under dùng chung (common / 공통) pressures.

Comparative thiết kế (design / 설계) should distinguish both.

## 35. Đường dẫn (path / 경로) dependence

Earlier institutions/hạ tầng (infrastructure / 인프라) can shape later options through sunk costs, networks and organizations.

Persistence alone is not đường dẫn (path / 경로) dependence; specify increasing returns/lock-in cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘).

## 36. Xã hội (social / 사회적) resilience

Communities/institutions respond to disasters, recessions or demographic shocks through resources, networks and quản trị (governance / 거버넌스).

Resilience should not romanticize adaptation that shifts burden to vulnerable groups.

## 37. Environmental sociology

Environmental risks are socially phân tán (distributed / 분산) and mediated by môi trường vận hành (production / 운영 환경), regulation, housing and inequality.

Detailed vật lý (physical / 물리적) mechanisms belong in environmental science; sociology focuses exposure, institutions and collective phản hồi (response / 응답).

## 38. Rủi ro (risk / 위험) society

Hiện đại (modern / 현대적) societies generate manufactured risks through technology/complex các hệ thống (systems / 시스템들) and depend expert institutions to assess them.

Rủi ro (risk / 위험) perception and actual hazard should be analytically separated.

## 39. Đo lường (measurement / 측정) and methods

Population/xã hội (social / 사회적) thay đổi (change / 변경) uses censuses, surveys, GIS, administrative dữ liệu (data / 데이터), historical records, mạng (network / 네트워크) dữ liệu (data / 데이터), ethnography and comparative cases.

Generic thiết kế (design / 설계)/phương thức (method / 메서드) details live in Research Methods.

## 40. Thất bại (failure / 실패) modes

Sai lầm thứ nhất là use demographic trend as direct nhân quả (causal / 인과적) explanation without institution cơ chế (mechanism / 메커니즘).

Sai lầm thứ hai là treat cities as homogeneous units and ignore neighborhood sorting.

Sai lầm thứ ba là explain xã hội (social / 사회적) movements by grievance alone.

Sai lầm thứ tư là technological determinism.

Sai lầm thứ năm là tell xã hội (social / 사회적) thay đổi (change / 변경) as one tuyến tính (linear / 선형) modernization đường dẫn (path / 경로).

## 41. Phân tích (analysis / 분석) template

1. demographic composition nào changed?
2. institution/thị trường (market / 시장) absorbs thay đổi (change / 변경) how?
3. spatial phân phối (distribution / 분포) matters how?
4. networks/di chuyển (migration / 마이그레이션) create phản hồi (feedback / 피드백) nào?
5. organizations/resources enable collective hành động (action / 동작) how?
6. frames/norms legitimate thay đổi (change / 변경) or resistance how?
7. technology interacts with institutions how?
8. toàn cục (global / 전역) vs cục bộ (local / 로컬) mechanisms differ where?
9. đường dẫn (path / 경로) dependence/trọng yếu (critical / 중요) events nào?
10. bằng chứng (evidence / 증거) separates dùng chung (common / 공통) shock, selection and diffusion không?
