# Xã hội (social / 사회적) Institutions — Family, Education, công việc (work / 작업), Media và Civic Life

> **Mạch đọc:** Đặt **xã hội (social / 사회적) Institutions — Family, Education, công việc (work / 작업), Media và Civic Life** trong bản đồ [README](./README.md) để thấy đơn vị sở hữu (owner / 오너) và vị trí của nó. Nội dung đi từ **1. Family as institution** sang **2. Household vs family**; điểm nối này chuẩn bị câu hỏi cho các mục sau thay vì dừng ở định nghĩa đầu tiên.


Institutions organize recurring xã hội (social / 사회적) problems: care, reproduction, học tập (learning / 학습), môi trường vận hành (production / 운영 환경), communication, collective quyết định (decision / 결정) and belonging. Sociology studies how these các hệ thống (systems / 시스템들) distribute roles/resources, how formal rules interact with informal norms, and how institutions reinforce or transform one another.

This chapter keeps institutional mechanisms general and cross-links domain-specific legal/economic/lịch sử (history / 이력) material instead of duplicating it.

## 1. Family as institution

Family organizes care, reproduction, socialization, emotional hỗ trợ (support / 지원), inheritance and tài nguyên (resource / 자원) pooling.

Family forms vary historically and cross-culturally; one household form should not be treated as universal baseline.

## 2. Household vs family

Household is co-residential/economic đơn vị (unit / 단위); family is kinship/recognized relational cấu trúc (structure / 구조).

They overlap but are not identical.

## 3. Division of household labor

Paid công việc (work / 작업) and unpaid care/housework are jointly allocated within households.

Outcomes depend wages, bargaining power, norms, chính sách (policy / 정책) and life-course events.

## 4. Bargaining inside households

Household decisions need not reflect one dùng chung (shared / 공유) utility. Resources/outside options can influence bargaining.

Who receives income or legal rights can affect spending and autonomy.

## 5. Marriage and partnership markets

Partnering patterns reflect preferences, xã hội (social / 사회적) networks, institutions and opportunity structures.

Educational assortative mating can increase household-level inequality when high earners pair with high earners.

## 6. Fertility

Fertility decisions interact with education, housing, childcare, labor markets, gender norms and bất định (uncertainty / 불확실성).

Fertility tỷ lệ (rate / 비율) changes should not be explained by one cultural attitude without institutional mechanisms.

## 7. Intergenerational transmission

Families transmit wealth, ngôn ngữ (language / 언어), health, expectations, networks and cultural capital.

Transmission can occur before formal schooling begins.

## 8. Education as institution

Education transmits kiến thức (knowledge / 지식)/skills, credentials, norms and xã hội (social / 사회적) networks.

It can promote mobility while also reproducing inequalities in preparation, tracking and school chất lượng (quality / 품질).

## 9. School sorting

Residential location, admissions, tracking and private resources sort students across peer/school environments.

Observed school differences combine composition and institution effects.

## 10. Tracking

Grouping students by ability/hiệu năng (performance / 성능) can mục tiêu (target / 대상) instruction but may khóa (lock / 잠금) in early đo lường (measurement / 측정) errors and unequal expectations.

Effects depend mobility between tracks and tài nguyên (resource / 자원) chất lượng (quality / 품질).

## 11. Credentialing

Degrees tín hiệu (signal / 신호) standardized achievement and facilitate selection.

Credential inflation occurs when higher credentials become required for jobs without equivalent skill thay đổi (change / 변경).

## 12. Peer effects

Peers can affect norms, thông tin (information / 정보), competition and học tập (learning / 학습).

But students select schools/friends, so peer similarity is not automatically influence.

## 13. công việc (work / 작업) as institution

Công việc (work / 작업) organizes income, thời gian (time / 시간), status, định danh (identity / 식별자) and xã hội (social / 사회적) networks.

Employment quan hệ (relation / 관계) also allocates authority and rủi ro (risk / 위험).

## 14. Occupational communities

Professions/occupations develop norms, ngôn ngữ (language / 언어) and identities beyond individual firms.

Professional norms can constrain employer authority.

## 15. Emotional labor

Jobs may require workers to manage/display emotions according to organizational expectations.

This creates effort often invisible in tác vụ (task / 작업) descriptions.

## 16. Alienation

Alienation frameworks examine mất mát (loss / 손실) of điều khiển (control / 제어)/meaning in công việc (work / 작업) when tasks/products/processes are separated from worker autonomy.

Hiện đại (modern / 현대적) applications should specify autonomy, tác vụ (task / 작업) fragmentation or giá trị (value / 값) xung đột (conflict / 충돌) rather than use term generically.

## 17. Labor tiến trình (process / 프로세스)

Organizations thiết kế (design / 설계) workflows to coordinate/điều khiển (control / 제어) môi trường vận hành (production / 운영 환경).

Technology can increase autonomy for some workers and surveillance/standardization for others.

## 18. Workplace inequality

Hiring, promotion, informal networks, scheduling and hiệu năng (performance / 성능) evaluation can create cumulative differences.

Organizational routines matter beyond individual prejudice.

## 19. Precarity and rủi ro (risk / 위험) shifting

Temporary/gig/on-call arrangements can shift demand/income rủi ro (risk / 위험) from firms to workers.

Xã hội (social / 사회적) protection and bargaining institutions alter consequences.

## 20. Religion as institution

Religion can organize meaning, community, moral norms, charity and authority.

Secularization is multidimensional: belief, practice, institutional authority and công khai (public / 공개) influence may thay đổi (change / 변경) differently.

## 21. Religious markets/networks

Participation can be shaped by xã hội (social / 사회적) networks, di chuyển (migration / 마이그레이션) and organizational competition.

Avoid reducing religion to private belief only.

## 22. Media as institution

Media organizations select, frame and distribute thông tin (information / 정보) under professional, commercial, political and technological các ràng buộc (constraints / 제약조건들).

Media effects depend audience selection and mạng (network / 네트워크) diffusion.

## 23. Agenda setting

Media can influence what issues people consider salient without fully determining opinions.

Salience and persuasion are different mechanisms.

## 24. Framing

How an issue is categorized/presented affects interpretation by highlighting certain causes, values or solutions.

Frame effects depend prior beliefs and ngữ cảnh (context / 맥락).

## 25. Algorithms and platforms

Recommendation các hệ thống (systems / 시스템들) shape exposure while users self-select content.

Observed polarization/filtering can reflect both algorithmic ranking and người dùng (user / 사용자) preferences/mạng (network / 네트워크) homophily.

## 26. Civic associations

Clubs, unions, professional groups, neighborhood organizations and NGOs create xã hội (social / 사회적) ties and collective sức chứa (capacity / 용량).

Associational life can bản dựng (build / 빌드) trust/skills but also mobilize exclusionary goals.

## 27. Civil society

Civil society refers organizations/relations between households, markets and trạng thái (state / 상태) that enable collective hành động (action / 동작) and công khai (public / 공개) expression.

Its ranh giới (boundary / 경계) varies across institutional contexts.

## 28. Political socialization

Family, school, peers, media and institutions shape civic kiến thức (knowledge / 지식) and participation norms.

This is descriptive sociology; it should not be used to steer political choice.

## 29. Participation

Civic participation depends resources, recruitment networks, institutional truy cập (access / 접근) and perceived efficacy.

Participation inequalities can amplify tài nguyên (resource / 자원) inequalities.

## 30. Organizations and biểu diễn (representation / 표현)

Formal biểu diễn (representation / 표현) structures can differ from whose preferences are actually heard.

Analyze selection, agenda-setting and organizational sức chứa (capacity / 용량) rather than assume equal voice.

## 31. Law as xã hội (social / 사회적) institution

Law formalizes categories, rights and sanctions but hiện thực (implementation / 구현) depends organizations, enforcement and legal consciousness.

Detailed legal content belongs in law domains; sociology studies xã hội (social / 사회적) thao tác (operation / 연산) of law.

## 32. Medical/health institutions

Healthcare organizes expertise, professional authority, truy cập (access / 접근) and classification of illness.

Health outcomes also depend xã hội (social / 사회적) determinants outside medical care.

## 33. Institutional interdependence

Family chính sách (policy / 정책) affects labor supply; education affects công việc (work / 작업)/status; housing shapes schools; media shapes công khai (public / 공개) attention; welfare chính sách (policy / 정책) changes household risks.

Institutional phân tích (analysis / 분석) should dấu vết (trace / 추적) cross-system effects.

## 34. Institutional thay đổi (change / 변경)

Thay đổi (change / 변경) can come from demographic shifts, xã hội (social / 사회적) movements, technology, legal reform, economic shocks and organizational diffusion.

Formal reform may be decoupled from practice if hiện thực (implementation / 구현) incentives unchanged.

## 35. Institutional trust

Trust can reflect hiệu năng (performance / 성능), fairness, định danh (identity / 식별자) and repeated experience.

Low trust may reduce compliance/cooperation; forced compliance can also affect trust.

## 36. Comparative institutions

Same nominal institution—school, family chính sách (policy / 정책), union, media hệ thống (system / 시스템)—can hàm (function / 함수) differently under different financing/quản trị (governance / 거버넌스) regimes.

Cross-country labels need operational detail.

## 37. Institutional dữ liệu (data / 데이터)

Bằng chứng (evidence / 증거) can combine administrative records, surveys, organizations, interviews, chính sách (policy / 정책) documents and longitudinal outcomes.

Formal chính sách (policy / 정책) văn bản (text / 텍스트) alone is insufficient for hiện thực (implementation / 구현) claims.

## 38. thất bại (failure / 실패) modes

Sai lầm thứ nhất là treat family/school/công việc (work / 작업) as isolated các hệ thống (systems / 시스템들).

Sai lầm thứ hai là infer institution tác động (effect / 효과) from composition differences alone.

Sai lầm thứ ba là equate formal chính sách (policy / 정책) with actual practice.

Sai lầm thứ tư là explain media influence without separating selection from exposure.

Sai lầm thứ năm là treat civic participation as equally accessible across groups.

## 39. phân tích (analysis / 분석) template

1. institution solves/organizes recurring bài toán (problem / 문제) nào?
2. roles/rules/resources được phân bố ra sao?
3. formal vs informal practices khác nhau ở đâu?
4. selection/composition vs institutional tác động (effect / 효과) thế nào?
5. which groups gain/lose truy cập (access / 접근)/authority?
6. cross-institution spillovers nào?
7. organization implements quy tắc (rule / 규칙) bằng incentives nào?
8. technology/demography thay đổi (change / 변경) institution ra sao?
9. comparative regime differences nào?
10. bằng chứng (evidence / 증거) observes chính sách (policy / 정책), practice hay kết quả (outcome / 결과)?

Institutions operate within changing populations, cities and toàn cục (global / 전역) connections. The final sociology cốt lõi (core / 핵심) chapter studies demographic thay đổi (change / 변경), urbanization, collective hành vi (behavior / 동작) and xã hội (social / 사회적) transformation.
